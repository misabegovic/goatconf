## https://tuzla-airport.ba/en/rent-a-car-2/ (Tuzla International Airport)

Rent a car
It is possible to rent a car at Tuzla International Airport. Within the airport there are numerous car rental agencies, which are located in the incoming hall.
(The Bosnian page, https://tuzla-airport.ba/rent-a-car/, says the same: "U sklopu aerodroma nalaze se brojne rent- a- car agencije, koje se nalaze u dolazećem holu.") The page names no agencies. Airport opening hours on the page: every day 06:30 - 23:00.

Agencies that list an office at Tuzla Airport on their own sites: CARWIZ ("Tuzla Airport - CARWIZ rent a car", https://www.carwiz.rent/en/offices/bosnia-and-herzegovina/tuzla-airport/), and Enterprise with Alamo (below).

## https://www.enterprise.com/en/car-rental-locations/ba/tuzla-international-airport-jfht.html (WebFetch summary, 2026-10-14)

- Counter: "Rental Counter is located in terminal", on the first floor across from the exit gate; no shuttle needed.
- Age: "Mini and Economy - minimum age 21 years."; 23 for Compact, Intermediate, Full Size and Specialty; maximum age 75.
- Licence: "All drivers must present a fully valid driving license."; held for at least two years; foreign licences in a different script need an International Driving Permit or a certified translation.
- Insurance: third-party liability included "FREE OF CHARGE"; collision damage waiver optional ("The customer is liable for the full cost of the vehicle if this coverage product is not purchased.").
- Abroad: a travel permit, "Charge is 75.00 EUR." plus VAT; Zero Excess mandatory outside Bosnia and Herzegovina.
- One way: "One Way Drops are allowed with a minimum advance notice of 24 hours." Drop fees apply to one-ways to and from Sarajevo, Mostar and Tuzla.

A web search on 2026-10-14 also showed Auto Europe's advice for Bosnia (https://www.autoeurope.co.uk/driving-information-bosnia/, not fetched): renters need to be at least 21 and have held a licence for two years.

## https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/bosnia-and-herzegovina/safety-and-security (UK government, "Road travel")

Your UK driving licence is valid as long as you're driving your own vehicle or a car hired outside of Bosnia and Herzegovina. If you're renting or using someone else's vehicle within the country, you must also have the 1968 version of the international driving permit (IDP) with you in the car.
You cannot buy an IDP outside the UK, so get one before you travel.
It's illegal to drive without at least third-party insurance. The Border Police can request printed documents to show you have it.

## https://www.rac.co.uk/drive/travel/country/bosnia-herzegovina/ (RAC, linked from the UK government's advice)

You must use dipped headlights at all times
The speed limit is 50km/h in built-up areas, 80km/h on open roads, 100km/h on dual carriageways and 130km/h on motorways, unless the signs say otherwise
It's compulsory to wear seat belts in the front and rear seats
Dial 112 in an emergency
From 2nd August 2021, drivers will no longer require an insurance green card for taking their vehicles to Bosnia-Herzegovina.
