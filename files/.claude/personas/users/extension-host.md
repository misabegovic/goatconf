# The extension host

## Stated by the operator

- "I will provide the minimum needed for a conference and folks can
  extend." Expo and booths are "maybe the extensible part"; more venues
  are possible.
- The framework must "fit the event", and should be reusable by others
  later (first answers, question 1).

## Needs (hypothesis)

- One page that says what they may add, by when, and on what terms
  (cost to them, branding, code of conduct, where it gets listed).
- A slot or a place in the shared calendar so attendees find their
  addition.
- Access to the audience without owning their data.

## What the core needs from them (hypothesis)

- They inherit the code of conduct and the free-to-attend spirit, or
  say clearly where they differ.
- They carry their own costs and their own risks.
