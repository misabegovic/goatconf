# Speaker: Carmine Paolino

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-14 before his session
has a slot or a topic, and listed second, after Paul Campbell; he agreed to
a public profile and to promotion on social media (source:
sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--carmine-paolino.md). His public
profile is the record `speakers/carmine-paolino.md`, built into
`site/speaker-carmine-paolino.html`. This page records only what the
sources say and what bears on GoatConf.

## Scope

- **Who:** "a software engineer, founder, and musician in Berlin", who runs Plenty, "the company behind this work" (source: sources/web/speakers/paolino-me-2026-10-14.md).
- **RubyLLM:** he created and maintains it, an open-source Ruby gem with one framework for AI applications across about twenty model providers; RubyLLM 2.0 and 2.1 were announced on 5 and 8 October 2026 (source: sources/web/speakers/paolino-me-2026-10-14.md).
- **Also builds:** Chat with Work, ArchSpec, Schematist, Kamal Backup, and the Spotifast and ZapFast desktop apps; co-founded Freshflow; worked at OLX, Capgemini and the Alan Turing Institute (source: sources/web/speakers/paolino-me-2026-10-14.md).
- **Community:** helps organise Berlin.rb, founded the Floppy Disco music collective, and makes house and disco as Crimson Lake (source: sources/web/speakers/paolino-me-2026-10-14.md).
- **Talks:** thirteen on RubyEvents, among them the RubyLLM keynote at San Francisco Ruby Conference 2025, the closing keynote at RubyConf TH 2026, "Ruby Is the Best Language for Building AI Web Apps" at RubyConf Austria, Balkan Ruby and Rubycon 2026, and "RubyLLM 2.0: Beyond Agents" at EuRuKo 2026 (source: sources/web/speakers/rubyevents-carmine-paolino-2026-10-14.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet.
- **Bio on the site:** the opening of his About page, word for word and in the first person, unlike the other profiles so far (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--carmine-paolino.md).
- **Role on the site and cards:** "Creator of RubyLLM, founder of Plenty" (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--carmine-paolino.md).
- **Photo:** the portrait on his own site, cropped to centre his face; he wears a cap and sunglasses in it. Not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision (source: sources/web/speakers/paolino-me-2026-10-14.md).
