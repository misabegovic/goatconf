# Images of Tuzla for GoatConf

Photos of Tuzla for the GoatConf website and social posts, sorted three ways:
what is free to use now, what is worth asking for, and what nobody has
photographed well yet. Part of [Tuzla for GoatConf](tuzla.md).

> Agent-authored synthesis, 2026-10-07. It describes what was found on
> Wikimedia Commons and on local websites that day. It is not a decision
> about which images the site uses.

Every free image is a Wikimedia Commons photo under public domain, CC0,
CC BY or CC BY-SA. Nothing NonCommercial, NoDerivatives or unclear is kept.
Each file sits in `assets/images/tuzla/` at 1600 pixels wide, under 600 KB,
with a `.jpg.json` file beside it holding the author, licence, credit line
and source. Each licence was recorded on 2026-10-07 under
`sources/web/images/`, so the credit can be checked even if the Commons page
changes.

## Freedom of panorama

The photographer's licence covers the photo, not the things in it. Bosnia
and Herzegovina's freedom of panorama does not cover commercial use of works
of art standing in public space. So where a modern sculpture or memorial is
the main subject, the photo licence alone may not be enough for commercial
use. That matters here for the Freedom Square fountain, the Kapija memorial
and the Slana Banja memorial; each carries a note in the table and in its
`.jpg.json`. GoatConf is free, which lowers the risk, but sponsor material
and anything sold should avoid these three or get the city's consent. The
goat statue, once photographed, will have the same issue: it is a modern
sculpture, so a photo of it is safest on non-commercial pages, and the
sculptor or the city should be asked before it goes on merchandise.

Two of the subjects are places of mourning. The Kapija plaque names the 71
young people killed there on 1995-05-25 (as written on the plaque in
`assets/images/tuzla/kapija-memorial.jpg`). Use it only where the page tells
that story, never as decoration.

in June light, morning or late afternoon:
