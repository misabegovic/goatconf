The International Airport Tuzla is 15 km away from Tuzla. It is one of four international airports in Bosnia and Herzegovina. In recent years, the airport has expanded significantly in terms of passenger numbers and is currently the second-busiest airport in the country, after Sarajevo International Airport. The airport is renowned as a hub for low-cost airlines and serves passengers from Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as neighboring countries, including Croatia and Serbia.
