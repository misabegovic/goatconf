30/03/2026
Tuzla, 29 March 2026: Tuzla International Airport will today mark the arrival of a second
Wizz Air based aircraft, along with the expansion of its route network from Tuzla, further
strengthening Bosnia and Herzegovina’s international connectivity.
The addition of a second based aircraft represents a major step forward in the airport’s
capacity development, enabling increased operations, a wider range of destinations, and
continued growth in passenger traffic. This expansion further reinforces the long-term
partnership with Wizz Air, as well as the strategic importance of Tuzla International Airport
within the regional aviation market.
Alongside increased capacity, several new routes to European destinations are being
introduced from Tuzla, including connections to major cities, further enhancing links with
key markets.
Dževad Halilčević, Director of Tuzla International Airport, said:
“The arrival of a second based aircraft and the expansion of our destination network mark
an important milestone in the continued development of our airport. The introduction of new
routes further strengthens our international connectivity and creates new opportunities for
cooperation, tourism, and passenger mobility. We will continue to invest in infrastructure
improvements and expand our route offering, with the aim of reinforcing our position in the
regional market.”
Vera Jardan, Corporate Communications Manager at Wizz Air, added:
“The launch of the new route between Tuzla and Bratislava marks an exciting milestone in
expanding travel opportunities from Bosnia and Herzegovina across Europe. We are thrilled
to introduce this direct connection, which opens up new possibilities for passengers seeking
both vibrant city breaks and seamless travel experiences. This new route will enhance
connectivity between Bosnia and Herzegovina and Slovakia, fostering tourism, cultural
exchange, and a dynamic flow of travellers in both directions.”
