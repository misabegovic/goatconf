---
title: "A photo of Meša and Ismet for the week 4 post"
date: 2026-11-07
kind: conversation
---

On Saturday 7 November 2026, told that the week 4 post "Meet at Meša and
Ismet" was the one to go out that day, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> So... The photo seems generic. Wdyt if we make it a split and actually show Ismet and Mesa statue in the street?

The agent said no photo of the monument was in the brain and offered
options; the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Find a good photo

The agent found, on Wikimedia Commons, one photo of each statue by
Sebleouf (February 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0): "Tuzla - Meša Selimović Statue 2
(2019).jpg" and "Tuzla - Ismet Mujezinović statue (2019).jpg"
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=76981455,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=76790523), and mocked up a
split card with the two side by side. A third Sebleouf photo of Meša has a
building under renovation, wrapped in a beer advertisement, behind it. The
only Commons photo with both statues in one frame, "Tuzla-Mesa Selimovic
Ismet Mujezinovic.jpg" by TheMiner, is 408 by 544, too small for a card.
The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I want a photo where we can see both of them and there is no construction going on behind

The agent found on Flickr "Tuzla, Monuments (Bosnia & Herzegovina)" by
Goran Necin, taken 2013-06-27, CC BY-SA 2.0
(https://www.flickr.com/photos/33581336@N02/9548315453): both statues
facing each other, a bank sign and a beer poster behind. Another Flickr
photo with both, by StaneStane, is CC BY-NC-SA and was left out. The
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Do we have a different angle where we can see the walkable street line behind?

Before the agent's further search finished (Flickr had started refusing
its requests), the organiser shared a photo of their own finding, writing,
verbatim:

> Maybe this?

It is a low, wide-angle summer shot of both statues facing each other with
the Korzo, its trees and café umbrellas running back behind them, 1200 by
744. Where it is from was not said. Asked where it came from and so which
credit it carries, the organiser answered, verbatim:

> Just don't show credit at all

Then, verbatim:

> Ok, make sure to land the changes to remote and we'll plan to release the post
