The Third Embedded Meetup in Bosnia and Herzegovina took place on November 28, 2025, at the National Theatre in Tuzla. The event was organized by three companies specializing in the development of embedded devices: Prosafety d.o.o. from Sarajevo, and Resonate d.o.o. and Semblie d.o.o. from Tuzla.

Embedded BiH is a growing community of experts, professionals, enthusiasts, and students working in all areas of embedded systems — including hardware, firmware, software, PCB design, and testing. This year’s EmbeddedBa meetup received strong support from the University of Tuzla, with professors from the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering joining the event.

With more than 100 engineers, students, and industry professionals in attendance, the meetup was one of the most well-attended tech events of the year. The program featured eight speakers presenting insightful topics rooted in their extensive real-world experience.

The foundation aims to transform Bosnia & Herzegovina into a prosperous nation that can respond to the challenges of the 21st century. It does this by empowering the youth through non-formal education, technology access and development of world class leadership skills.
