# Speaker: Predrag Radenkovic

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-23 before his sessions
have slots or titles; he agreed to a public profile and to promotion on
social media (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md).
His public profile is the record `speakers/predrag-radenkovic.md`, built
into `site/speaker-predrag-radenkovic.html`. This page records only what
the sources say and what bears on GoatConf.

## Scope

- **Who:** "CTO at *codeplain turning modular specifications into validated agentic workflows"; "A two-time founding engineer, he has helped guide startups from their early stages to unicorn valuations and acquisitions, leading technical teams at Silverfin, Toptal, and FIS." His World Congress page adds "15 years of experience" (source: sources/web/speakers/wearedevelopers-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md).
- **His company:** *codeplain was founded in Ljubljana in early 2025 by Dušan Omerčević, its CEO, and generates code from specifications written in Plain, its open-source spec language. Its thesis, in Omerčević's words: code "should be regenerated", and "it's the specs that you maintain." In 2026 it released plain-forge, open-source agentic skills that let coding agents draft and keep Plain specs (source: sources/web/speakers/thenewstack-codeplain-2026-10-23.md). The company's team page lists him second, after Omerčević (same source).
- **What he brings, Perceived:** the organiser expects his company to give two sessions, a talk and a workshop, on regenerative software; "probably", so neither is promised yet (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md).
- **Talks:** "Spec-Driven Development with Agentic Skills", a 120-minute session at WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2026 in Berlin on 8 July 2026 (source: sources/web/speakers/wearedevelopers-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md); a featured speaker at How to Web in Bucharest (source: sources/web/speakers/howtoweb-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md); on the AWS Startup Days Ljubljana 2026 speakers list (source: sources/web/speakers/wearedevelopers-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md).
- **Bio on the site:** his WeAreDevelopers profile, word for word, already in the third person (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md, sources/web/speakers/wearedevelopers-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md).
- **Role on the site and cards:** "CTO at *codeplain", as the organiser wrote it (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md).
- **Photo:** his How to Web speaker portrait, 2380 by 2380, resized to 800 by 800; not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision. His WeAreDevelopers photo carries the *codeplain mark, which the site cannot show (sources: sources/web/speakers/howtoweb-predrag-radenkovic-2026-10-23.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md).
- **How to support him:** the *codeplain blog and plain-forge (source: sources/web/speakers/thenewstack-codeplain-2026-10-23.md).
- **Order:** ninth on the speakers list, after Christoph Lipautz; his arrival added an eighth reserved main-hall slot, Friday 15:30, so each day holds four (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md).
- **LinkedIn:** the organiser gave linkedin.com/in/pedja-radenkovic, as his WeAreDevelopers profile does; the company's team page links a different address, linkedin.com/in/predrag-radenkovic-07512116 (source: sources/web/speakers/thenewstack-codeplain-2026-10-23.md). The site follows the organiser.
