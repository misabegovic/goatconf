Peace Flame House
Association Peace Flame House organizes and performs activities in the facility Peace Flame House. “Fred foundation“ from the Netherlands started the project in 1994 with philanthropic ideas and with assistance of sportsmen who took part in Winter Olympic Games in 1984 in Sarajevo, local community and many people of good will. This unique facility – Peace Flame House in Tuzla- is a great contribution to repatriation and development of culture in Bosnia and Herzegovina. In 2003 the Peace Flame House was constructed. Today the House is the home for associations which work and bring a message of peace and tolerance and which tend to widen the awareness of citizens about social needs through various workshops about social values, culture of living and education.
