Barok
Trg Slobode bb, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
https://www.pizzeriabarok.com/
A pizzeria, cafe and bar all in one, Barok’s moniker should give you a clue as to the architectural majesty of its location. Found in the beautiful Baroque stunner on Trg Slobode, Barok serves up some of the best pizza in town, along with other options for those not interested in the magical qualities of dough, cheese, tomato and the rest. The breakfast options are also pretty decent, making this a fine stop no matter the time of day.
