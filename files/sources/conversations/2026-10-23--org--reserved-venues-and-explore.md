Translated by the agent: "For the National Theatre and the church in
glass (find the real name), put that they check with us first, because
maybe we want to use these locations for the program's purposes. For
example dom mladih can be for parties, DJ sessions... I don't know...
There are surely more locations around Tuzla."

Decided or directed here:

- **Explore deeply:** research venues and how hosts are guided, as a
  research and drafting task.
- **Check with us first:** a host who wants the National Theatre or the
  "church in glass" asks the organiser before planning there, because
  the organiser may use those two places for GoatConf's own program.
- **Find the real name** of the "church in glass".
- **Dom mladih for parties and DJ sessions:** offered as an example
  ("Tipa", "Ne znam"), not a decision.
- **More places:** the list should go beyond the five named.

Not answered: whether events may run alongside sessions or only in the
hours off, the form of the guides, and whether venues are listed as
places to ask or arranged first. These stay open.
