Shown Paul Campbell's goat in ginger, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I see the goat cut off in the card you shared? Why don't we make the goat overlay?

and then, verbatim:

> I didn't mean that I want a sitting goat. Peeking goat is great and maybe all speakers should have a peeking goat. But the horns are cut off... They should not be

The agent found several goats whose horns, or the cook's toque, were cut flat where the generated sheets had been split on a grid: Amar Mahmutbegović's peeking and sitting goats, the waving and jumping goats, the cook's peek and sticker, and about ten Goatchi poses. It cut them again by figure, restored horn tips from the original sheets, and had ChatGPT repaint the eating cook, the jumping shy goat and the peeking cook whole; the organiser downloaded and sent the cook's and Amar's head turns from their phone when Chrome blocked further downloads. Shown before and after, and the goats on the pages, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I want all speakers goats to be peeking goats without having their legs cut off

Asked which kind of peek, they chose "Head and hooves over the edge (Recommended)", described as: like the plain peeking goat, only head, horns and two front hooves above the card. They added, verbatim:

> Also want them also looking up and down, following the mouse

and chose "9 directions + blink", described as: a 3 by 3 sheet of the goat looking in every direction and a blink, per speaker.

The organiser sent Paul Campbell's two gaze sheets from their phone, then wrote, verbatim:

> I see white spots between horns

They were right: the first cut filled the paper showing between and through the horns; it was cut again so bare paper stays see-through. Asked, verbatim:

> If there is no mouse movement or tapping, does the goat go back to its animations?

the agent made the goat look back at the visitor four seconds after the pointer stops, its own moves then resuming. Shown Paul's goat following the mouse in nine directions, and asked whether to paint the other seven speakers the same way, the organiser chose "Ship Paul first", described as: ship the horn fixes and Paul's new peek now, the others in a second pull request.

After the head-only peek went live (PR #226), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> So... I am not sure I like this that much over the old peeking goats. Too small, also too responsive and follows every movement. I want it to feel natural, also react on taps, have its animations... Also a bit more like the previous version in the way it peeks over

Offered to revert, repaint in the old style, or only calm it down, they chose "Repaint in the old style now", described as: a new nine-direction sheet in the old peek framing (head, chest and hooves over the card, the old size) and a calmer follow (a short delay, holding each look, only when the mouse is near). They sent the sheet and its blink from their phone. Shown a video of it on the Speakers page, they chose "Ship it (Recommended)".
