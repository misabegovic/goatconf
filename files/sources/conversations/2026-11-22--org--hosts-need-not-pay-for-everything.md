---
title: "A host does not have to pay for everything: people who come may pay for themselves"
date: 2026-11-22
kind: conversation
---

On 2026-11-22, with the site saying in several places that the host of an
added event pays for it, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I think we need to stress out that it's ok to propose an event where the organizer doesn't pay for everything but folks that come pay for themselves

Wording was then proposed for the Google Form that takes event proposals
and for the Luma event, with the site's host page to be reworked to
match. For the form, under "Who pays for it?": "You run it. You do not
have to pay for everything: it is fine if the people who come pay for
themselves, as long as the listing says what it costs.", with the help
text "You, the people who come, or both." For Luma: to remove "and the
goat hunt" and to add "A host does not have to pay for everything; at
some events people pay for themselves." The organiser answered, verbatim:

> Ok. Pls change luma and google forms too

and then, to the wording, verbatim:

> Sounds good. You should have access to chrome though? You also have access to my google drive

Read as: the site, the form and Luma say the same thing. A host runs the
event and answers for it, and GoatConf does not pay for it, as before
(the charter's fourth rule: the host carries its own costs and risks;
GoatConf lends its audience and its calendar, not money). What is new is
said plainly and stressed where a host decides: the host does not have to
pay for everything, since it is fine for the people who come to pay for
themselves, a dinner where each pays their own bill or a trip with a
ticket, as long as the listing says what it costs. An event in an hour
off stays free. The form and the Luma event are changed through the
browser by the session that talks to the organiser; the repository holds
the site. Because this is new wording in the organiser's name, the round
is shown to the organiser before it is published
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).
