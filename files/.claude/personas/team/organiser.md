# The organiser

Sources: `files/wiki/org/organiser.md` and the conversation sources it cites.

## What they want (stated)

- A minimal core they provide, and an event others extend, "like the Pi
  agent" (`files/sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`).
- Great minds together in an affordable place, free to attend.
- To spend at most 1,000 EUR of their own money.

## What they have done before (stated)

- Founded and organised EuRuKo 2024 and RubyConf Austria.

## How to read a proposal through their eyes (hypothesis)

- Does it add work the organiser must do personally, from Vienna? If so,
  can someone else own it?
- Does it cost the organiser money? If so, who else could pay?
- Would it still run if a partner or sponsor fell through?
