On 2026-11-22 the organiser was asked three questions with answers to
choose from, about the home page's sections, and chose one answer to each.
The chosen answers are quoted exactly.

1. Question: on a first phone visit the open speakers section is 42% of
   the home page and the free ticket sits 8.7 screens down while Tuzla,
   food and cost start closed; how should the first visit look on phones?

   Answer chosen: "Keep as it is"

   That is: the speakers open with every card in full, every other
   section closed, as decided earlier the same day
   (sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--sections-start-closed-speakers-open.md).
   An independent review had disagreed; the defaults do not change.

2. Question: the closing "See you in Tuzla" band can be collapsed but
   closing it saves only 140 px and hides one line; should it keep its
   collapse control?

   Answer chosen: "Make it fixed open (Recommended)"

   That is: "See you in Tuzla" loses its control and is always open, like
   the hero and "Get your free ticket". This amends the organiser's
   earlier "Only leave the free ticket section as one that can't be
   collapsed and the hero section"
   (sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--every-section-collapsible-and-remembered.md).
   What a visitor's browser already remembers about that section is
   ignored and breaks nothing.

3. Question: the fix for sections closed versus open (closed rows holding
   still, one tap opening a whole section, goats not covering text, the
   sideways phone menu that cannot scroll) is building now; how should it
   go out?

   Answer chosen: "Ship when ready (Recommended)"

   That is: the work recorded in
   sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--meals-section-weird-when-collapsed.md
   is published once it is built and the checks pass.
