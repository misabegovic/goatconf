After sessions got an area tag (engineering, design and product,
research, the business of software, the people side of the work), the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Let's also borrow from https://compassaitechsummit.com/ and have more topics we support, like AI/ML, UX/UI and Data

The Compass AI Tech Summit home page, read on 2026-10-19, lists five
topics as tags on its hero and speaker cards, each with one line: Data,
Leadership, Product, AI/ML and UX/UI.

Offered three sets, the organiser picked eight areas with Leadership:
Engineering, AI/ML, Data, UX/UI, Product, Research, Business of software
and Leadership. "Design and product" splits into UX/UI and Product, and
"the people side of the work" becomes Leadership, as Compass names it.
Not picked: keeping the people side of the work (which also covered
careers and communication), and a seven-area set with "UX/UI and
product" as one area.

They added, verbatim:

> But also have in mind that we need to group stuff for the program. Maximum I could think of planning for GoatConf is getting two more single track venues to also split topics and sessions.

So the areas should group into a few tracks for the program, at most IPI
Academy plus two more single-track venues. No grouping was decided.

Then, verbatim:

> These are the other two venues: https://maps.app.goo.gl/4i6512pQyDX8hBUa7?g_st=ac and https://maps.app.goo.gl/PwiQhrE2oQ9EWMqH9
>
> I still don't have them, but was thinking to explore potentially

The two links resolve, on 2026-10-19, to Google Maps places:

- Tuzla Public Theatre, Pozorišna 4, Tuzla 75000.
- Hrvatski kulturni centar "Sveti Franjo" Tuzla (the Croatian Cultural
