# The hiring partner

## Stated by the operator

- "If companies want to hire these folks they can reach out and help the
  event in that way, as a sort of agency maybe."

## Needs (hypothesis)

- Meeting strong people in a setting that is not a job fair.
- A clear, priced way to support the event and be visible for it.

## Constraints the attendee persona puts on them (hypothesis)

- Attendee data is never sold or handed over without explicit consent.
- Hiring is something attendees opt into, not the price of a free ticket.
