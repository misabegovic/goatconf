The organiser asked to work on the online presence: social posts, banners, a logotype, unified across, and a rework of what the site shows where. Asked about channels, the logotype, the order and review, the organiser chose: LinkedIn, X / Bluesky / Mastodon, and Luma and link previews; "New logotype from scratch"; "Brand kit, then shareables, then site (Recommended)"; "Show me at each step (Recommended)".

Shown three directions (a seal, a one-line goat, a pictogram), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I like the first one. maybe we should go in the "the one to feed the community" story? lets refine it a bit more visually

> I also like the foot stamps of goats on the website

Shown the refined seal with the Bosnian saying on the ring, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> "The one that feeds the community" ne CIJELA TUZLA JEDNU KOZU MUZE on this, I don't like that.

Offered two bottom lines for the ring, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> A . but there still more work to be done on the goat itself.... you have access here somewhere to EuRuKo 2024 on this device. we used a goat there already as a mascot.

> we also had t-shirts with the goat and stuff, went great

The goat was found in the organiser's local copy of the EuRuKo 2024 site, at 2024.euruko.org/assets/images/goat.png (394 x 449 px, white on transparent), and traced into vector outlines. Shown the seal with that goat, and asked whether the goat is the organiser's to reuse, whether to change it, and whether GoatConf's blue and yellow is the right split from EuRuKo's red, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Yes. Looks good.

Read as: the GoatConf mark is a round seal with the EuRuKo 2024 goat, unchanged, in a coin at the centre; a band of hoof prints around it for the community; THE ONE THAT FEEDS THE COMMUNITY across the top of the ring and GOATCONF · 2027 along the bottom; blue and yellow. The goat is the organiser's to reuse.
