Posted October 19, 2026 (updated October 20, 2026). Verbatim:

As part of the celebrations marking the major milestone of 50 years of the University of Tuzla, the University Hiking Excursion to Konjuh Mountain was successfully held today, 17 October 2026. The event was organised by the University of Tuzla Centre for Sport, Culture and Arts, in cooperation with the “Konjuh” Mountaineering Society.
Around 150 participants, including students and staff members of the University of Tuzla, spent a day enjoying nature, socialising and hiking together to the Javorje Mountain Lodge on Konjuh Mountain.
After completing the hiking trail, the participants were welcomed with traditional mountaineering hospitality, including hot herbal tea and bean stew, adding to the warm atmosphere and convivial spirit of the gathering.
One of the highlights of the programme was a tug-of-war competition, in which the team representing the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Tuzla emerged victorious.
There was also plenty of music, laughter and dancing, with participants joining together for the popular Macarena dance, demonstrating that health, recreation and good spirits are best brought together through shared activities.
We would like to express our sincere gratitude to the “Konjuh” Mountaineering Society for its excellent cooperation and hospitality, as well as to all students, staff members and participants whose presence helped make this day a memorable one.
