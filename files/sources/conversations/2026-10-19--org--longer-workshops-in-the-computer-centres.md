---
title: "Workshops in the computer centres can run longer than an hour"
date: 2026-10-19
kind: conversation
---

The review of what GoatConf invites found that one hour is short for a
hands-on workshop and that nothing offered longer. The organiser wrote,
verbatim:

> For workshops, within the computer centers, yes we can figure out how to make it work over multiple hours if someone needs it.

So a workshop in one of IPI Academy's two computer centres (20 and 26
seats) may run over more than one hour if its speaker needs it; the
speaker says how long in the proposal and the hours are worked out with
them. How the program shows such a workshop is not decided yet.
