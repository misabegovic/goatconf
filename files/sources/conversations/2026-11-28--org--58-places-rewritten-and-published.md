On Saturday 28 November 2026 the organiser asked, verbatim:

> What's today's post?

The agent gave week 7 of the drumbeat, "58 places to run yours", as it
stood on `wiki/org/outreach/drumbeat-2026.md`, still drafted, and said
the brain held no news since Tuesday that would take the Saturday. The
organiser then asked, verbatim:

> Should we rewrite the post?

The agent said yes, for three reasons. The LinkedIn text said "you ask,
you book and you pay", against the organiser's ask of 22 November that a
host need not pay for everything
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--hosts-need-not-pay-for-everything.md).
It was drafted before the organiser asked for posts that are "hypey and
introductionary"
(sources/conversations/2026-11-23--org--carmine-spotlight-hypey-and-introductory.md),
and the agent had read the approval of 24 November as covering every post
still to come, the Saturday ones too
(sources/conversations/2026-11-24--org--speaker-posts-approved.md). And
the short text named the page, "Make it yours lists 58 places", which
says nothing to a reader on X who has not seen the site, and gave no
date. The agent showed a rewrite of both texts, the facts read that day
from the Make it yours page (`site/extend.html`), with the short text
counted at 279 characters. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> We are 3 chars over the X limit with text

The agent's explanation: X counts every link as 23 characters whatever
its length, and `goatconf.com/extend` is 19, so a text of 279 characters
is 283 on X, three over 280, which is the figure the organiser gave. The
agent had counted the characters as written. It took "2027" out of the
first line, the year staying in the date at the end, which makes the text
274 characters as written and 278 as X counts them, and it counted every
other short text on the three drumbeat pages the same way: none is over
280 on X. Shown the shorter text and asked whether it and the LinkedIn
text were good to post, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Yes, ship it and I posted

Read as: both texts are approved as shown and the post is out, on
Saturday 28 November 2026. The organiser was composing it on X when the
count came up, so X is taken as one place it went; whether it also went
on LinkedIn, which card went with it, and whether the LinkedIn text went
out as given were not said.
