THE BEAUTIFUL CITY OF BUENOS AIRES HOSTED THE FIRST ETHEREUM WORLD’S FAIR FROM NOVEMBER 17 to 22, 2025.
Over 14,000 attendees from 130+ countries gathered at the iconic La Rural in Palermo for a touch and feel experience on Ethereum.
The first Ethereum World’s Fair brought over 80 exhibiting teams across eight dedicated districts for DeFi, Privacy, L2s, Decentralized Social, Hardware & Wallets, AI, Gaming, and Art.
The whole event was supported by more than 200 volunteers. Over 40 events took place inside La Rural, while 500+ community-run events took place across the city.
