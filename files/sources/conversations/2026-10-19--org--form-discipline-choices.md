After PR #131 widened the talk page's "Who we are looking for" (source:
sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--business-and-people-sessions.md),
the organiser was asked whether the proposal form's "discipline" choices
(engineering, design, product, founding a company, other) should match the
site. They picked "Match the site".

The agent changed the choices in the Google Form "GoatConf 2027: propose a
session", through Chrome, on 2026-10-19, to: Engineering; Design and
product; Research; The business of software; The people side of the work;
Other. The public form showed the new choices afterwards. The form had two
responses at the time; their stored answers are unchanged.
