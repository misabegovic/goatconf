From the organiser's review of the site, taken one fix at a time and each seen in the browser first (sources/conversations/2026-10-26--org--food-tags.md): on desktop, every inner page's blue head pushed the title to the bottom of its first row, leaving about 200px of empty blue above it, with the lede falling low beside the photo. The title and lede now sit together as one block, centred against the photo, and the head is shorter; phones are unchanged. Shown side by side with the live site on the register, travel and speakers pages, the organiser chose "Ship it".
