The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> For the speakers dinner, let's put that the organiser pays for that

Until now the speakers dinner was to be paid from online supporters'
money as an event cost, if the budget allowed, and the organiser's own
money was never to pay for it
(sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--speakers-dinner.md). The talk
page read "GoatConf pays for it if the budget allows."
(sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--dinner-wording.md).

Asked to choose, the organiser picked:

- **Promised:** "Yes, promised". The organiser pays for the speakers
  dinner, whatever the budget. The "if the budget allows" condition is
  dropped, and so is the rule that the organiser's own money never pays
  for it.
- **Wording:** "The organiser pays for it (Recommended)".

Built on 2026-10-18: the talk page's line reads "An invitation to the
speakers dinner, with the other speakers. The date is still to come. The
organiser pays for it." Who is invited (every accepted speaker) and the
date (still to come) are unchanged. The live talk form's description
only says accepted speakers are invited to the dinner, so it needs no
change.
