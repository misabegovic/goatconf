date: 2026-10-12
kind: conversation
---

Asked how many of the four slots on each track are invited keynotes and how many are open for applicants, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I don't know yet. the slots will be filled as we go, depending on interest and quality of ideas

Read as: the invited share is undecided. The slots are open and are filled as proposals come in, judged on interest and quality. Not yet stated: who judges quality (the organiser, or the blind panel from the CFP decision), how interest is measured, and when the filling stops. The calendar in the wiki has the call closing in late January and the full schedule in April, with no extension after publication.
