Location: Bingo City Center Tuzla, which OSM places at Mitra Trifunovića Uče 2 (44.53223, 18.65196), about 2 km straight-line west of IPI.

Restaurant KVIN is a newly opened restaurant located within Bingo City Center Tuzla.
Its pleasant and modern ambiance combines industrial and natural design elements, making it an ideal setting for friendly gatherings, romantic dinners, and corporate events. The restaurant is open daily from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. and consists of four separate sections: a café, a bar, a restaurant area, and a VIP hall.
Every meal at KVIN is prepared using carefully selected ingredients of controlled origin, thoughtfully balanced in terms of proteins, fats, and carbohydrates. In addition to a diverse selection of dishes and a dedicated vegan menu, the restaurant also offers a refined wine list featuring labels from various vineyards around the world.
