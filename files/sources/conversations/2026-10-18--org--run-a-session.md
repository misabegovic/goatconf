The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok, for the Give a talk section. We should also encourage something outside of a talk. Our cfp is open for more than talks... I don't think this section reveals the full picture

The call already takes a talk, a panel, a workshop or something else
that fits an hour (the form's formats; the slot format is open,
sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--day-chain-and-session-format.md),
but the home page section, the top bar and the footers said "Give a
talk". The organiser chose:

- **The name:** "Run a session", in the top bar, every footer, the
  session page's title and the home section. The page stays at
  goatconf.com/talk, so old links keep working.
- **The home section:** four format stickers, Talk, Panel, Workshop and
  Something else?, each opening the session page, under the steps.
- **Examples of "something else":** live coding or a live demo, a
  hands-on lab (the two computer centres have 20 and 26 seats), a
  fireside chat or an interview, a debate or an open discussion. They
  invite proposals; they are not promises.

The session page gains "What a session can be" with the four formats and
the examples, and "Not sure your idea fits? Propose it anyway". The home
page's cover link reads "Propose a session", and its open places read
"Your session" instead of "Your talk".
