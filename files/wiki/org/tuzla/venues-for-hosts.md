# Places in Tuzla where hosts could run an event

GoatConf's core is two days of sessions at IPI Academy; everything else is
added by the people who come
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--extendable-and-each-edition.md`).
On 2026-10-23 the organiser asked for guides for hosts and a list of
places "where they could do their magic", including between sessions,
not only around them (`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--host-guides-and-venues.md`),
asked for karaoke, contacts and photos, and ruled places of worship out
of the list except the "church in glass"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--extensions-page-search-guides-photos.md`).
This page is the research behind that: what each place is, how far it is
from IPI, how big it is, what it already hosts, how to ask, and whether a
photo GoatConf may use exists. Nothing here is arranged with any venue;
every listing is "a place you could ask", and the site may say only that
until the organiser arranges more.

The places now live as records in `places/venues.yml`, shown on the Make it
yours page once the organiser approves each
([the ADR](../adrs/make-it-yours-hub.md)); this page keeps the research
behind them.

## How to read this page

- **Distance** is a straight line from IPI Academy (44.5404, 18.6738),
  from OpenStreetMap
  (`sources/web/osm/tuzla-venues-2026-10-23.json`,
  `sources/web/osm/tuzla-venue-geocodes-2026-10-23.json`). A real walk is
  longer, often by a third. Where only the street was found, the
  distance is the street's and says so.
- **Fits an hour off** marks places under about 400 m in a straight
  line, roughly a five to eight minute walk, so a short event fits one of
  the hours off: Thursday 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00, Friday 10:30, 12:30 and
  14:30 ([what is known](../goatconf-2027.md)). Friday 12:30 is the hour for
  Džuma and lunch.
- **Contact** is what the venue itself or a business listing publishes.
  Mobile numbers appear only where the business publishes them as its own
  line.
- **Photo** says whether a freely licensed photo was found (each source
  file lists the candidates under "Photo candidates", with author and
  licence) or whether only the venue's own photos exist. On 2026-10-23 the
  organiser chose to use every good photo either way ("Use all photos that
  are good, disregard that",
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--hosts-hub-answers.md`), as with
  the food photos, which come down if an owner asks
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--food-photos-one-by-one.md`).
  Each photo still keeps a record of where it came from.
- **Confidence** of the page is low: the facts are cited, but capacities
  are mostly unpublished, several listings are undated, and no venue has
  been asked anything.

## Check with GoatConf first

The organiser may use these two places for GoatConf's own program, so a
host asks the organiser before planning there
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--reserved-venues-and-explore.md`).

- **Narodno pozorište Tuzla (the National Theatre).** JU Narodno
  pozorište Tuzla, Pozorišna 4, about 510 m. One auditorium of 290 seats,
  220 in the stalls and 70 on the balcony
  (`sources/web/venue/nptz-pozoriste-sala-2026-10-23.md`). It rents the
  hall: it publishes a hall booking request form ("Zahtjev za korištenje
  sale") that covers outside and commercial events, with stage, seating,
  lighting and sound plans; the rental rulebook it refers to is not online
  (`sources/web/venue/nptz-zahtjev-za-koristenje-sale-2026-10-23.md`).
  Contact: nptz@nptz.ba, produkcija@nptz.ba, +387 35 251 645, office hours
  09:00 to 15:00; the real site is nptz.ba
  (`sources/web/venue/nptz-kontakt-2026-10-23.md`). The adjacent Arlekin
  building is under renovation in autumn 2026, with no closure dates
  (`sources/web/venue/nptz-arlekin-obnova-2026-10-23.md`). Photo: one
  exterior on Wikimedia Commons, by Sebleouf, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **The "church in glass": Hrvatski kulturni centar "Sveti Franjo".**
  No source uses the phrase "crkva u staklu"; the organiser confirmed the
  match on 2026-10-23 ("Yes, that's it",
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--hosts-hub-answers.md`). HKC "Sveti
  Franjo" stands in the old town, "Stari grad bb, behind the Chamber of
  Commerce of Tuzla Canton", on the site of the old Catholic church taken
  down in 1987; its main hall is built as "the silhouette of the old
  Catholic church" and described as "open, transparent", with white glass
  doors, and a guide describes its "modern glass surfaces"
  (`sources/web/venue/hkc-sveti-franjo-fst-news-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-klix-2018-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-go-bosnia-guide-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-tztz-2026-10-23.md`). It is a cultural centre run by
  the Franciscan friary, not a church in use: a multifunctional hall with
  a stage and loose chairs, plus a small lecture hall, rehearsal rooms, a
  café, a library and a lawn
  (`sources/web/venue/hkc-sveti-franjo-fst-official-2026-10-23.md`). It
  hosts concerts, plays, a book fair and a fair run by outside organisers
  in 2026 (`hkc-sveti-franjo-rtvslon-koncert-violine-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-rtvslon-predstava-casa-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-kultura-ba-sajam-knjige-2026-10-23.md`,
  `hkc-sveti-franjo-rtvslon-sajam-djulistan-2026-10-23.md`). No seat count
  or rental terms are published; a 2017 concert drew about 240 people
  (`hkc-sveti-franjo-klix-2018-2026-10-23.md`). Contact is the friary,
  Franjevačka 26 (`hkc-sveti-franjo-fst-official-2026-10-23.md`). A 2023
  article says the Croatian Consulate General was to move into its side
  building; not checked since
  (`hkc-sveti-franjo-hrvatski-glasnik-konzulat-2023-2026-10-23.md`).
  Distance: not measured; the friary on Franjevačka is about 320 m from
  IPI. Photo: only the guide's and the tourist board's own photos.

## Cultural houses

- **Dom mladih Tuzla** (the organiser's example for parties and DJ
  sessions). JU "Dom mladih" Tuzla, Dragiše Trifkovića 2, about 1.1 km, now
  a unit of JU Centar za kulturu Tuzla; the tourist board calls it the
  Center of Youth Culture (`sources/web/venue/czk-o-domu-mladih-2026-10-23.md`,
  `tztz-dom-mladih-tuzla-bs-2026-10-23.md`). Any association or informal
  group of young people may use its resources; it lists concerts of
  classical and popular music, band rehearsals, festivals, conferences and
  "cultural and entertainment events"
  (`czk-o-domu-mladih-2026-10-23.md`), and the Centar leases out its
  premises (`czk-procedura-prijema-stranaka-2026-10-23.md`). It has a
  "Kamerna scena" (chamber stage) (`tuzlainfo-dom-mladih-kamerna-scena-2026-10-23.md`).
  No seat counts, and no party or DJ night found in 2025 or 2026. Contact:
  the head of Dom mladih, rukovodilacdommladih@centarzakulturutuzla.ba,
  +387 35 362 620 (`czk-kontakt-2026-10-23.md`). Photo: none found.
- **Kuća plamena mira (Peace Flame House).** Šetalište Slana banja bb, in
  Slana banja park, about 0.9 to 1.2 km, run by the citizens' association
  of the same name. It organises concerts, plays, celebrations and
  workshops, and has hosted choirs and art-music concerts in 2024 to 2026;
  a 2025 post describes the house as having "no right angles" and good
  acoustics. No room list,
  capacity or rental terms published. Contact: kucaplamenamira@gmail.com,
  +387 35 276 330, kpm.ba (`sources/web/venue/kpm-home-2026-10-23.md`,
  `kpm-koncert-umjetnicke-muzike-2026-2026-10-23.md`,
  `kpm-ethno-bih-2026-2026-10-23.md`). Photo: none of the house.
- **Međunarodni atelje "Ismet Mujezinović" (the Atelier).** Klosterska 19,
  about 680 m. A memorial room, an exhibition and workshop space and a
  garden in the centre that hosts concerts, exhibitions, book launches,
  conferences and workshops; summer garden concerts in 2025 and 2026
  (`sources/web/venue/czk-atelje-ismet-mujezinovic-2026-10-23.md`,
  `tuzlainfo-atelje-musica-bosnae-basta-2025-2026-10-23.md`,
  `rtv7-atelje-price-koje-pjevaju-2026-10-23.md`). Capacity not published.
  Ask through the Centar za kulturu, +387 35 252 002
  (`czk-kontakt-2026-10-23.md`). Photo: none found.
- **Dom književnosti (House of Literature).** A unit of the Centar za
  kulturu, about 320 m (OSM), fits an hour off
  (`sources/web/venue/czk-dom-knjizevnosti-2026-10-23.md`). Spaces and
  seats not published.
- **Međunarodna galerija portreta (International Portrait Gallery).**
  2. tuzlanske brigade 13, about 1.2 km. Exhibitions, concerts and round
  tables; two collections are a national monument, so expect limits on
  food, drink and noise (`sources/web/venue/czk-medunarodna-galerija-portreta-2026-10-23.md`,
  `czk-koncert-galerija-portreta-2023-2026-10-23.md`).
- **Kaleidoskop.** A former cinema hall of the Centar za kulturu that
  needs renovation (`sources/web/venue/czk-kaleidoskop-sala-2026-10-23.md`);
  OSM places "Kino Kaleidoskop" about 530 m away. Not usable as far as
  known.
- **BKC (Bosanski kulturni centar Tuzlanskog kantona).** Mije Keroševića
  Guje 3, about 1.7 km. A 603-seat large hall, the Blue Hall (127 fixed
  seats, with sound and projector), a 30-seat congress room, two galleries
  and a 150-seat chamber stage built on risers; 200 to 300 events a year
  (`sources/web/venue/bkc-o-bkc-u-tuzla-2026-10-23.md`). It publishes 2026
  prices: the Blue Hall 390 KM for up to three hours or 650 KM a day, the
  congress room 150 KM or 300 KM, the large hall from 1,000 KM for three
  hours. Non-profit programmes under a signed cooperation protocol with
  BKC pay no fee, which a free conference's host could ask about
  (`sources/web/venue/bkc-cjenovnik-usluga-2026-10-23.md`). Contact:
  info@bkctuzla.ba, +387 35 282 296 (`bkc-kontakt-2026-10-23.md`). Photo:
  none found.

## Karaoke

No Tuzla venue with a confirmed, current karaoke night was found, after
searches in English and Bosnian (`sources/web/venue/karaoke-tuzla-search-extract-2026-10-23.md`,
`allevents-tuzla-karaoke-2026-10-23.md`). Two leads are search extracts
only: a review mentions "karaoke nights" at Club Viva (OSM places a "Club
VIva" about 1.5 km away), and an Instagram profile, @zags_caffe, lists
karaoke on Friday and Saturday with no address. Until a local confirms
one, a karaoke host books a bar or a room that takes private events (the
Pivnica Taverna, Nardi Pub) and brings the songs, which is what the
propose an event page already says (`site/add.html`). The organiser's
direction on 2026-10-23: "Imas kafica blizu, tipa na trgu" (there are
cafés nearby, like on the square), so the cafés on and around Freedom
Square, below, are the first places to ask for a karaoke evening
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--hosts-hub-answers.md`).

## Cafés and restaurants near Freedom Square

- **On the square, under 200 m, fit an hour off:** Caffe Bar Central and
  Barok (both in the Barok building), and Coffee Zone, Fra Grge Martića 30,
  with a summer garden. None lists events or rooms
  (`sources/web/venue/iyp-caffe-bar-central-2026-10-23.md`,
  `iyp-barok-2026-10-23.md`, `iyp-coffee-zone-2026-10-23.md`). A meetup in
  one is a matter of telling the café ahead.
- **Restoran Zlatnik**, by the Slana banja promenade and the lakes, about
  900 m by the street: up to 200 guests, a large garden, seminars and
  events, +387 35 261 225 (`sources/web/venue/tztz-restoran-zlatnik-2026-10-23.md`,
  `iyp-zlatnik-2026-10-23.md`).
- **Heartland**, Kazan mahala 10, about 630 m by the street: 120 seats,

## Rules a host should know

The agent's reading of Tuzla Canton's law on public gathering, not legal
advice. Gatherings for cultural, entertainment and similar interests that
do not aim to earn income are not notified (Article 35), unless their
character or numbers call for extra security, when the police are told at
least five days ahead (Article 36); a public event that does aim at
income ("javna priredba") is notified to the police at least seven days
ahead (Article 31) (`sources/web/legal/tk-zakon-o-javnom-okupljanju-1-12.md`).
The 08:00 to 22:00 window added in 2015 applies to peaceful assemblies,
not to every event (`sources/web/legal/tk-zakon-o-javnom-okupljanju-izmjene-11-15.md`).
Draft laws in 2024 would change the notice to five days by post, fax or
email; whether they passed is (unknown, needs source)
(`sources/web/venue/paragraf-tk-javno-okupljanje-nacrt-2026-10-23.md`).
An event inside a café, a hotel or a cultural house sits under that
venue's own rules. Using Freedom Square or another public space for an
event needs the city's rules, which are (unknown, needs source).
