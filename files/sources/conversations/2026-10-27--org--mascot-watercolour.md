The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I also want to explore alternatives to the goat mascot... I want it to be more animated and more similar to the enola one if that makes sense. I want to see different versions for me to select of the mascot

Shown four enola-style versions, animated, the organiser answered: "the animation aspects are a nice progress... but I want to explore wildly different concepts of the mascot". Shown six concepts (pixel, bean, the mark alive, doodle, climber, the hidden goat), then six more drawing styles (rubber hose, kilim, shapes, letters, moods, paper), the organiser chose "None yet, more ideas" and "Still none, keep going". Asked what was missing, they chose all four offered: "More character, less icon", "More detail and depth", "Funnier, weirder", "More Tuzla in it". Shown three goats with a story (the salt smuggler, the floater, the hungry goat), they wrote: "lets first focus on the final base look of the mascot and then we can do the different moods". Shown one goat six ways (plush, toy, kid, shaggy, two-tone, the Bosnian goat), they wrote:

> these are cute... but they are all the same, same style, same art... maybe you dont know how to illustrate? should we use some other tool?

Told that the agent draws by writing SVG shapes, so its versions share one construction, and offered an image generator, an illustrator or both, the organiser chose "Generator, then illustrator (Recommended)", asked the agent to run the prompts in their Chrome, and left the choice of generator to the agent ("idk, you choose"). In ChatGPT, signed in on the organiser's own account, one image showed the same goat in nine styles; the organiser liked eight (all but rubber hose). Offered the goat multiverse as one goat across art styles with one base style for the site, they chose "Yes, but another base", then "5. Watercolour". Shown a watercolour character sheet (sitting, peeking over an edge, standing, waving, asleep, jumping), they chose:

> Yes, illustrator finalises (Recommended)

described as: download the sheet into a reference folder and write a brief for the organiser's illustrator to draw the final goat and its poses, owned by GoatConf; the site keeps today's goat until then. The sheet, the nine-style image, the prompts and the brief are on the organiser's computer, in Downloads/goatconf-mascot; generated images are kept out of this repository.
