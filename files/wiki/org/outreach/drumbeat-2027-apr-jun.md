### Tue 6 Apr 2027, week 26: Eat and drink, with or without meat

- **State:** drafted
- **Image:** `post-with-or-without-meat-square.png` (LinkedIn, Mastodon), `post-with-or-without-meat-wide.png` (X, Bluesky)
- **Before posting:** No photo on this card: the food photos on the site are not freely licensed.
- **LinkedIn:**

  > Bosnian food is meat-heavy, and it is good. There's plenty without meat too.
  >
  > Try zeljanica (spinach and cheese pie), sirnica (cheese pie) and krompiruša (potato pie), often vegan. Ask for posno: it means made without meat, and often without eggs or dairy too, so check if you're vegan. Finish with a Bosnian coffee or tufahija, a poached apple with walnuts.
  >
  > The travel page maps places near IPI with vegetarian dishes on their menus: goatconf.com/travel

- **Short** (179 characters):

  > Eating at GoatConf in Tuzla, with or without meat: ćevapi, but also zeljanica, sirnica and krompiruša. Ask for posno: made without meat. Places near the venue: goatconf.com/travel

### Tue 11 May 2027, week 31: Before you travel

- **State:** drafted
- **Image:** `post-before-you-travel-square.png` (LinkedIn, Mastodon), `post-before-you-travel-wide.png` (X, Bluesky)
- **Before posting:** Recheck visa and roaming news the week it goes out.
- **LinkedIn:**

  > Coming to GoatConf from abroad? A short checklist.
  >
  > Papers: EU, UK and US citizens need no visa for up to 90 days. Check your own country's rules.
  > Phone: Bosnia and Herzegovina is not in the EU roaming area, so a local SIM or eSIM avoids extra charges.
  > Health: EHIC and GHIC cards don't work here. Get travel insurance.
  > Money: the convertible mark (KM) is fixed to the euro. Carry some cash.
  >
  > More on the travel page: goatconf.com/travel

- **Short** (182 characters):

  > Coming to GoatConf from abroad? No visa for EU, UK and US citizens (up to 90 days), no EU roaming (get a local SIM), no EHIC (get insurance), and carry some cash. goatconf.com/travel
