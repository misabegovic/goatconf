Scope: the whole site, rebuilt from scratch on 2026-10-12; the Korzo walk is replaced. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: the would-be attendee first, then would-be speakers and people who want to add something.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GoatConf is a sticker album. The organiser sticks in the core facts; every other numbered slot is printed empty and waits for whoever brings a talk, a workshop or a walk. It refuses the dark tech-conference hero with a speaker grid.

OWN-WORLD: album-cover blue (#0b3d91) as the ground, printed with faint cloven-hoof marks; white album pages with dashed printed slot outlines, each empty slot carrying a faint printed goat silhouette and a yellow-ringed printed number; glossy stickers with a white die-cut border, a soft lift and a diagonal gloss; the goat itself as one die-cut yellow sticker (white edge, no foil); signal yellow (#ffd400) for numbers, the big facts and the registration status; red (#e30613) only for the "Yours?" stamp

STORY: The visitor reads the filled stickers first (Free, Tuzla, June 2027, IPI Academy), sees the empty numbered slots, understands the program fills as people join, and takes one of three doors.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the album opened flat on a blue spread. Left page: the die-cut goat sticker, GOATCONF 2027, and the lede "A free conference in Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina, in June 2027", so Free, June and Tuzla are on the first screen on phones too. Right page: four filled stickers (FREE, TUZLA, JUNE 2027, IPI ACADEMY, with TUZLA lettered at the same yellow scale as FREE and JUNE) and two empty slots ("the exact days", waiting on IPI; "your talk", stamped Yours?). Under the cover text, the yellow status "Free registration opens when the dates are confirmed" and the links "Propose a talk" and "Add something".

FORM: Sticker album, my own list position 1 (Impeccable's pick), seed key 7707c262. Code-led. Raised by the type specimen: scale contrast carries hierarchy. Raised by the provenance ribbon: decided facts are stuck-in stickers, undecided ones are empty printed slots. Signature interaction: stickers peel and settle once into their slots as they enter view; reduced motion shows them stuck.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
