# IPI Academy (IPI Akademija Tuzla): host and venue

GoatConf's partner and, "for now", its venue
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--venue-ipi.md`). It agreed to
partner and host (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`).
Site: [ipi-akademija.ba](https://ipi-akademija.ba/).

## What it is

- "Higher education institution of contemporary business, information
  technology and market communications 'International business-information
  academy' Tuzla (IPI Academy) was founded in 2014 and it is the first
  local private higher education institution in Tuzla"
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md`).
- Programs: three-year degrees in Information Technology, Market
  Communications, and Contemporary Business and IT Management; four-year
  degrees in Informatics and Computing (and, in the Bosnian text,
  Accounting and Finance) (same source). Its slogan is "studij po mjeri
  poslodavca", study made to the employer's measure (same source).
- Address: Kulina bana br. 2 (Skver), 75000 Tuzla
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-kontakt.md`).
- The organiser holds a BSc in Information Technology from it
  (`sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-about.md`), and has visited it "many
  times this year already" (2026)
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`). Its
  rooms are set out below.

## What it brings beyond rooms (from its own pages)

- **Students and a student club:** "an active student association,
  Student Club of the IPI Academy" (`ipi-akademija-about.md`). A natural
  source of volunteers and of local attendees.
- **Student HUB @ipia:** "a designer space intended for students, where
  student can get mentoring support and they can develop and implement
  their ideas and business plans for free" (same source). A candidate
  second room or a hallway-track space.
- **Employer ties:** "a number of collaborations with employers" for
  student placements (same source, Bosnian text). Relevant to the hiring
  idea.
- **App Challenge:** a student mobile-app competition; the 2026 edition
  took topics until 15 June 2026 and full apps until 15 September 2026,
  with the best "presented at the DIEC 2026 conference"
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-app-challenge.md`). IPI therefore
  already runs or hosts a conference, DIEC, and a June deadline sits in
  its calendar.

The largest hall's seats are not settled. The June message, written on the
visit day, says 150. On 2026-10-07 the organiser picked "under 120" and,
asked for a number, said "120 i think"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).
The June figure records what was seen on the visit; the October figure is a
recollection, so the June message is the likelier of the two. Neither has
been confirmed by IPI in writing, so the core-day plan uses about 120 seats
until one of them is.

Also from the visit notes (same source):

- **Every room has HDMI and presentation support.** There is no sound
  system, and none is needed except for recording content.
- **Recording is not possible** without an extra engagement.
- **Duration:** IPI expects a conference on its premises to run two to
  three days.
- **June constraint:** the event fits the first or the second week of
  June; in the second week, the faculty's rooms are available only until
  15:30.
- **Breaks:** lunch and the long refreshment break would probably be
  outside the faculty, in town; a coffee break for 150-200 people on site
  is unclear, maybe through cooperation with the hotel if it has a
  restaurant.
- **Marketing:** the faculty will take part in promoting the event
  through its marketing department.
- **Hotel rooms** for attendees are being clarified in the weeks after
  the visit.
- **Scale the rooms suggest:** a program of two to three talk tracks,
  with segments of four to six parallel workshops; over two to three full
  days that is about twelve workshops and thirty-six to forty
  presentations, in the organiser's own estimate.

And from the organiser's answers on 2026-10-07
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`):

- **Equipment for a full room.** Asked about projector, sound and Wi-Fi, the
  organiser answered yes and added "no microphone afaik and no recordings".
  The June message says there is no sound system and none is needed except
  for recording. Both agree there is no microphone. Whether the halls have
  speakers is not confirmed.
- **Several other rooms** are free for workshops while talks run.
- **Wheelchair access:** "The main hall and the first floor yes". On 2026-10-12 the organiser added that
  anything not on the ground or first floor is not wheelchair accessible, that guide dogs cannot come in,
  and that accessible toilets probably exist but need verifying (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--accessibility.md).
  Computer centre 1 is on the first floor and is reached by the lift (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--computer-centre-1-lift.md).
  Computer centre 2 is not step-free (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--computer-centre-2-not-step-free.md).
- **Saturday or a weekday** both work.

## Dates and rooms for 2027

- **Dates accepted:** IPI Academy has accepted Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June
  2027 as GoatConf's core days and Saturday 5 June as the community day
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`). The organiser reported it on 2026-10-12; no written copy is in
  the brain.
- **All rooms:** GoatConf has access to all seven rooms in the table below
  on those days (same source). The main hall's seat count stays as set out
  below.

## What the rooms allow

Arithmetic from the table above, not a cap on attendance:

- **Talk tracks at once:** the three largest rooms, 150, 100 and 34 seats, or
  254 seats if the hall holds 120. That matches the two to three talk tracks
  in the June message.
- **Workshop rooms** if those three are talk tracks: computer centre 1 and 2,
  the top-floor room and the former student club, 13 to 26 seats each. Four
  parallel workshops fit in those four rooms. Five or six need the 34-seat
  room, which would then not be a talk track.
- **Wheelchair-accessible seats** in the rooms marked yes: 270, or 240 if the
  hall holds 120.
- **All seven rooms together:** 367 to 368 seats. That is the sum of the rooms,
  not how many people one session can hold. Each session is capped by its own
  room.
- **Second week of June:** the rooms close at 15:30.
