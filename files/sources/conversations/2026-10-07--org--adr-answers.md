After approving the epic and the eleven PRDs, the organiser asked to be
asked "one by one". Each question below was put on its own, with options;
the answer is the option the organiser picked, verbatim, or the words the
organiser typed instead of an option.

1. **Which tool for free registration?** (pretix hosted, Tito, Luma, a
   plain form.) Answer: "Luma".
2. **Which form tool for the call for talks, and for extension submissions
   too?** (Tally or Google Forms.) Answer: "Google Forms".
3. **How should an extension be written down in this repository?**
   (Markdown with frontmatter, plain text, a structured data file.)
   Answer: "Markdown with frontmatter (Recommended)".
4. **On the core day, how should the long breaks be arranged?** (Two
   breaks of one to two hours, one long midday break, decide with IPI.)
   Answer: "Two breaks (Recommended)".
5. **What should the pages at muhamed.at/goatconf be built with?** (Plain
   HTML and CSS, whatever muhamed.at uses, a static site generator.)
   Answer: "Plain HTML and CSS (Recommended)".
6. **Where should the goat appear in the design?** (A mark and a small
   story, a mark only, everywhere and playful.) Answer: "Everywhere,
   playful".
7. **Where should the email list live?** (Luma's own updates, a separate
   newsletter tool, no list.) Answer: "Luma's own updates (Recommended)".
8. **How should talk proposals be reviewed?** (Anonymised with a small
   panel, the organiser alone anonymised, open.) Answer: "Anonymised,
   small panel (Recommended)".
9. **How should an attendee say they are open to work?** (An opt-in Luma
   question, a badge mark only, both, neither.) Answer: "Opt-in Luma
   question (Recommended)".
10. **What shape should the public budget ledger take?** (A CSV in
    goatconf rendered as a page, a Markdown table, a public Google
    Sheet.) Answer: "A CSV in goatconf, rendered as a page (Recommended)".
11. **Who runs the town-wide goat hunt?** (Hand it to a host, the
    organiser, skip it in 2027.) Answer: "I run it myself".
12. **Which code of conduct should GoatConf start from?** (The Geek
    Feminism policy or the Contributor Covenant 3.0.) The organiser typed
    instead: "Take from RubyConf Austria".
13. **How should conduct reports reach the two contacts?** (Phone plus
    email, email plus in person, the same as RubyConf Austria.) Answer:
    "Same as RubyConf Austria".
14. **Fixed calendar dates now, or steps anchored to the day IPI
    confirms?** Answer: "Anchored steps (Recommended)".

Read as: these answers settle the "Decision needed" of every PRD, and the
ADRs are written from them for the organiser's approval. RubyConf Austria
is the organiser's own conference; its code of conduct is the Contributor
Covenant 1.4 adapted for a conference, with reports by email to the CoC
team (`sources/web/coc/rubyconf-austria-code-of-conduct.md`).
