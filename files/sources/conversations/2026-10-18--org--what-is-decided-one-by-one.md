After the first pass (two columns of tighter stickers, merged as PR #96),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Maybe order one by one? And optimize for size?

Asked to choose, the organiser picked:

- Where: phones only. Tablets and desktop keep the two-column grid.
- Fact stickers (Free, 3–5 June, Tech stack agnostic): title above text,
  as before, full width.
- Photos (Tuzla, IPI Academy): keep them tall.

Built as, below 48rem: the six stickers stack in one column, in order 1
to 6. Each is only as tall as its content needs. The photos are square at
full width, the facts all use the same title size, and the column is at
most 26rem wide, centred, so wider phones do not get oversized photos.
The words did not change.
