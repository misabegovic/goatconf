# More goat animations: tap to pet, ears and tail that move at rest, and more tricks

## Objective

Every goat on the site, and the goat kept in Goatchi, feels alive: it can be petted, it flicks an ear or wags its tail
while it waits, and it has more tricks, all painted rather than drawn in code.

## Background

The organiser asked for more animations "not only for goatchi" and bet on the whole pitch on 2026-10-31
(sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--more-goat-animations.md,
sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goat-animations-bet.md; [the pitch](../pitches/goat-animations.md)).

## Affected personas

- [Attendee](../../../.claude/personas/users/attendee.md), owning the need: a site that rewards a tap and stays alive
  while they read.
- [Speaker](../../../.claude/personas/users/speaker.md), affected: their goat gains moves, published with a change on
  request as before.

## Now / Perceived / Target

- **Now.** Three tricks on a tap, five moves while waiting, a blink, and for speakers a look around; in Goatchi no
  blink and nothing on a tap.
- **Perceived.** The goats feel a little stiff between moves.
- **Target.** A tap pets first, then plays a trick; ears and tails move now and then; four more tricks; Goatchi blinks.

## Scope

- Tap to pet, everywhere: a heart, a happy lean with eyes closed, a line.
- A peek set of painted frames for every goat that peeks (the plain goat, six roles, eight speakers): ears flicked,
  petted, a yawn, a sneeze, a hoof raised to scratch, a wink.
- A whole set for every goat seen whole (Goatchi's seven goats): ears flicked, tail wagging, petted, a stretch with a
  yawn, a sneeze, a scratch behind the ear with a hind hoof.
- The new tricks on a tap and among the moves while waiting; Goatchi blinks.

## No-gos

- No goat upright on two legs; nothing sent anywhere; nothing over a food photo or the when, where and how to register.

## Rabbit holes

- About 30 to 40 sheets; the plain goat's sets come first as the reference.
- Weight: frames load after the page, only for goats on it.
- Too busy: moves stay rare and slow; reduced motion keeps only the blink.

## Appetite

Medium, about one to two weeks, much of it art.

## Success metrics

- Tapping any goat once pets it; a second tap plays a trick.
- Every goat on the site has its six peek frames, and every Goatchi goat its six whole frames, with whole horns.
- The scripted goat audit shows no console errors, and each goat page weighs no more than about 150 KB more.
- The organiser plays with the goats on their phone and approves.

## Decision needed

How the frames are made, named and loaded, and how petting and the new moves fit the companion and Goatchi.
