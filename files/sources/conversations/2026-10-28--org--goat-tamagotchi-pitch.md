---
title: "The goat tamagotchi: a free game on the site, not a paid app, shaped as a small pitch"
date: 2026-10-28
kind: conversation
---

The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> How would a tamagochi game work with the goats? As part of the website... Or as a separate mobile game? I could sell maybe €0.99 to support the conference?

The agent sketched a game on the site: adopt a speaker's goat or the plain one in the browser, with no account; feed it Bosnian food, play with its tricks and let it sleep; it never dies, only sulks; it perks up when its speaker's session is read, the visitor registers or shares a card; its trip to Tuzla counts down to 3 June; QR codes at the event give treats. It advised against a paid app: the app stores cost about 99 US dollars a year (Apple) and 25 once (Google) and keep about 15% of a sale, so a 0.99 euro sale leaves about 0.65 euro and about 150 sales a year only cover Apple's fee; the charter says GoatConf's "content is never for sale"; the speakers' goats depict real people, who would have to agree; and money paid to the organiser's Austrian business is for the adviser to place. It suggested an optional "buy your goat a burek" support link instead.

Asked what next, the organiser chose "Shape a web pitch (Recommended)". Asked for the appetite, they chose "Small (Recommended)": about a week, mostly the agent's work with the organiser's review, the QR treats at the event and the burek link left for a second bet in spring. Shown who it is for and its boundaries (the attendee persona owns the need; each speaker is asked before their goat can be adopted, and until then only the plain and role goats can be; no paid app or in-game purchases; no accounts, tracking or server; the goat never dies and never nags; nothing on the site promises the game before it is built; rabbit holes: payments from individuals to the business, a slow connection, a game that guilt-trips), they chose "Write it (Recommended)".
