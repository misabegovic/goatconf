# Who each supporter is and how to support them, on their profiles

Each supporter's profile on goatconf.com can say who they are ("Who they
are", the body of their record in `supporters/`) and how people can
support them ("How to support them", the record's `support_them:`). These
texts are written from each supporter's own public pages, cited below.
The organiser approved them, and they are on the profiles since
2026-10-18 (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--supporter-descriptions.md,
sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--show-support-parts.md). The note
below still goes to each supporter, so they can correct or replace
their part.

Each profile also offers two pairs of cards, square and wide: from
2026-10-21 a "Proud supporter of GoatConf 2027" card for the supporter to
post as their own, with their logo, their supporter line, when and where,
and their profile's address, the same headline for every supporter, the
host included; then GoatConf's thank-you card to them. A link to a
profile still previews with the thank-you card (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-21--org--proud-supporter-cards.md).

## Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures Foundation

**Who they are:**

> Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures Foundation empowers young people in Bosnia and Herzegovina through non-formal education, access to technology and leadership development. Through its Futures Scholarship, Researchers, and Entrepreneurs & Innovators programs, students build globally relevant careers at home, with mentoring from Bosnian and Herzegovinian professionals around the world. It aims to develop 10,000 STEM leaders by 2030.

Every claim is on the foundation's own pages: the mission, the three
flagship programs, careers at home, the network of professionals and the
10,000 STEM leaders by 2030 (source: sources/web/supporters/bhff-get-involved-2026-10-18.md).

**How to support them:**

- Partner as an organisation: scholarships, internships, mentors and speakers: https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-organisations
- Get involved as a professional: host a student, mentor, host a networking event: https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-professionals
- Join as a student: https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-students
- Become a monthly donor, from 5 euro a month: https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/donate

(source: sources/web/supporters/bhff-get-involved-2026-10-18.md)

## IPI Academy

**Who they are:**

> IPI Academy, the International Business-Information Academy, was founded in 2014 as the first local private higher education institution in Tuzla. It teaches three- and four-year programs in information technology, market communications, contemporary business and IT management, and informatics and computing, with practical teaching and a mandatory professional placement. At its Student HUB @ipia, students get a space, equipment and mentoring to build their ideas and business plans for free.

(source: sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md)

**How to support them:**

- Study at IPI Academy: https://ipi-akademija.ba/ (enrolment is on the home page; source: sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-home.md)
- Offer students a placement, as an employer: https://ipi-akademija.ba/kontakt (IPI works with employers on students' professional placements; sources: sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md, sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-kontakt.md)

## Meister

**Who they are:**

> Meister builds digital productivity tools that make it easy for anyone to go from ideas to action. MeisterTask is a work management platform that gives teams one place for projects, tasks and documentation, and MindMeister is a mind mapping tool used by millions of students, teachers and professionals. Founded in 2006 by Till Vollmer and Michael Hollauf, Meister is based in Vienna, Austria, with a team across Europe.

(source: sources/web/supporters/meister-about-and-jobs-2026-10-18.md)

**How to support them:**

- Try MeisterTask: https://www.meistertask.com
- Work at Meister: https://www.meistertask.com/pages/jobs

(source: sources/web/supporters/meister-about-and-jobs-2026-10-18.md)

## wroclove.rb

Drafted on 2026-10-21 from wroclove.rb's own site for the organiser to
check, and on its profile once the organiser approves it (source:
sources/conversations/2026-10-21--org--wroclove-rb-community-partner.md).

**Who they are:**

> wroclove.rb is a relaxed, single-track and not-for-profit Ruby conference in Wrocław, Poland, run by the local Ruby community: a place for Ruby professionals to confront ideas in a friendly atmosphere and mutual respect, where deep topics meet a receptive audience. Its 2027 edition runs from 23 to 25 April at the University of Wrocław's Institute of Computer Science, with talks and workshops on Friday, talks on Saturday and social activities on Sunday.

Every claim is on the conference's own pages: the description in its
own words, the dates, the three days and the venue (source:
sources/web/supporters/wroclove-rb-about-2026-10-21.md). "Poland" is added, as Wrocław is in Poland.

**How to support them:**

- Come to wroclove.rb, 23 to 25 April 2027 in Wrocław: https://wrocloverb.com/participants
- Watch their past talks: https://www.youtube.com/user/wrocloverb
- Join their newsletter: https://wrocloverb.kit.com/0430aa4d7d

(source: sources/web/supporters/wroclove-rb-about-2026-10-21.md)

## *codeplain

Drafted on 2026-10-23 from *codeplain's own site and The New Stack, when
it was listed for Predrag Radenkovic's travel (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--codeplain-supporter-and-home-speakers.md).

**Who they are:**

> *codeplain is an AI-powered code generation platform that turns specifications written in ***plain, an open-source specification language, into production-ready software. When something needs to change, the spec is edited and the code regenerated from it. Founded in early 2025 in Ljubljana, Slovenia, it works on spec-driven development for software written with AI.

The first sentence follows the home page's own words, adding that ***plain
is an open-source specification language (source:
sources/web/supporters/codeplain-logo-and-site-2026-10-23.md); the rest,
editing the spec and regenerating the code, and its founding in Ljubljana
in early 2025, are from The New Stack (source:
sources/web/speakers/thenewstack-codeplain-2026-10-23.md).

**How to support them:**

- Try *codeplain: https://www.codeplain.ai/
- Read the *codeplain blog: https://blog.codeplain.ai/

(source: sources/web/supporters/codeplain-logo-and-site-2026-10-23.md; its careers page has no open positions, so there is no jobs link)
