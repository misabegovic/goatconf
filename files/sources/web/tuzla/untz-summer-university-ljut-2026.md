The 31st Summer University Tuzla (LJUT 2026) was officially opened with a message encouraging students to remain open to new knowledge and ready for cooperation that transcends national and disciplinary boundaries.

Over the coming weeks, the University of Tuzla will host students, professors, and experts from Bosnia and Herzegovina and abroad, continuing more than three decades of international academic exchange, dialogue, and collaborative learning.

During one of the University’s most recognizable events, LJUT 2026, taking place from June 1 to June 25, 2026, students, professors, researchers, experts, and citizens will have the opportunity to participate in numerous educational, scientific, cultural, and sports activities, said the Rector of the University of Tuzla, Prof. Dr. Amir Karić.

“LJUT is a meeting place for professors, students, and people from different cultures and identities. In this time of conflicts, violence, aggressive rhetoric from world political leaders, and wars, it is very important to insist on and persevere in civilized communication, dialogue, and understanding of diversity,” Karić stated.

For more than three decades, the Summer University Tuzla has served as a space for academic exchange, knowledge sharing, and cooperation among participants from Bosnia and Herzegovina and abroad. Organizers emphasize that the event aims to provide opportunities for acquiring new knowledge and skills, promoting scientific research, activism, and international cooperation, alongside activities that encourage creativity, dialogue, and networking.
