Accessibility

What to expect, and what we are still checking. We would rather tell you plainly than promise what we cannot keep.

Checked with the organiser on 12 October 2026.

Wheelchairs and step-free access

  • Step-free: the ground floor and the first floor of IPI Academy. That is the main hall (ground floor, about 120 seats), the first-floor hall (100 seats) and computer centre 1 (first floor, 20 seats). The first floor is reached by lift.
  • Not step-free: computer centre 2, the second-floor room, the top-floor room and the old student club.
  • Being confirmed: the accessible toilets. We believe there are accessible toilets, and we are checking with IPI Academy. This page will say when we know.
  • Sessions stay in their rooms. The program says which room each session is in, so you can see which ones are step-free. Tell us early if a session you need is upstairs; we will move it only where that makes sense, and cannot promise it.
  • The breaks are on Freedom Square, a pedestrian square a few minutes away. The old town around it is paved with stones.

Deaf and hard of hearing

  • No microphones. The rooms have no microphones, so in a big room it can be hard to hear.
  • No recording. GoatConf does not record the talks, so there are no captions afterwards.
  • Slides carry the main points. We ask every speaker to put their main points on their slides and to share the slides afterwards.
  • No sign language or live captions yet. A supporter can fund them. If one does, this page will say which sessions have them.

Blind and low vision

  • This site works with screen readers. The program is also available as plain text (llms.txt) and as data (program.json).
  • Speakers describe their slides. We ask every speaker to say out loud what is on their slides.
  • A volunteer can meet you at the entrance and walk you to your rooms. Tell us before the event.
  • Guide dogs cannot come into the venue.

Tell us what you need

Email info@muhamed.at or use the contact form, before the event or on the day. We will tell you honestly what we can do.

GoatConf 2027, a free conference in Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina, 3–5 June 2027. Organised by Muhamed Isabegović, with IPI Academy as host and partner.
