Wine & Whiskey Bar
Đorđa Mihajlovića 4, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
It does exactly what it says on the tin, in short. Wine, whiskey, bar. You know what to expect but try to remember that this is Bosnia & Herzegovina — best leave those presumptions at the door. Plenty of live music keeps visitors entertained during the weekend, while the extensive drink selection keeps the mood up. Wine & Whiskey is another excellent spot in summertime, with a terrace that stands up to any other in the city.
