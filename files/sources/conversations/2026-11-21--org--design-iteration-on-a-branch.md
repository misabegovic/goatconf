On 2026-11-21 the organiser asked for an analysis of how the design of
goatconf.com can improve, verbatim:

> Ok. Let's analyze how we can improve the design of https://goatconf.com

The agent ran a design critique of the live site at 1440 and 390 pixels
wide, with a separate detector scan, and reported five priority issues:
the phone home page runs to about 35 screens with no register action in
the sticky bar; registering takes two pages and the register page shows
its button below the first screen; what the ticket means is unclear
beside the button; the top bar and the hero carry too many competing
asks; and the red stamp and the slot outline sit under the contrast the
album asks for, with six fact stickers unnumbered.

Asked which to take first, how much home page content may move, and how
many issues to cover, the organiser gave no preference on any of the
three. Shown the proposed order, the organiser answered, verbatim:

> Ok. Show me visually, I don't care for text
> Iterate on it on a separate branch, never deliver without my approval and show me locally here through screenshots

The work is done on the local branch `site/phone-first-and-one-ask`. It
is not pushed, not merged and not published until the organiser approves
it.
