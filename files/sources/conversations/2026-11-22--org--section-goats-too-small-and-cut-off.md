On 2026-11-22, about the live site, after the speakers section was
published starting closed
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--ship-speakers-start-closed.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Goats seem to small now and the text and the goat can be cut off by the section it is in apparently?

and then, verbatim:

> Maybe it's on mobile only? Check

Read as: the goat that stands on each section of the home page, placed
there on the organiser's words of the same day
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--meals-section-weird-when-collapsed.md),
is too small to read as the site's goat, and the goat and what it says
must never be cut by the section it stands on. The second message asks
whether the fault is on a phone only.

It is not on a phone only. Measured on the published page the same day,
light theme, the goat called to each row in turn:

- On a phone 390 wide the goat on a section's row is 45 by 48 pixels; on a
  window 1440 wide it is 63 by 68. The same goat on a slot of the cover is
  83 by 87 on that phone, and before the change of that day the goat on a
  heading was 86 wide on a phone and the cook 112.
- While a section is open, the goat on its top edge is cut whole by the
  section itself: the section's page clips what leaves its rounded
  corners, and the goat and its speech bubble stand above the top edge,
  outside it. That is so at 390 and at 1440, on every section drawn as an
  album page (nine of the ten that open and close). While a section is
  closed nothing clips the goat or the bubble; the goat is only small.
- A row whose top is just below the sticky top bar keeps its goat, whose
  head is then under the bar.

Not the organiser's words, and for the organiser to confirm: the size the
goat is given, the room kept between two sections for it, and where its
speech bubble opens.
