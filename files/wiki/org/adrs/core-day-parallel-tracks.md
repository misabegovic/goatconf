# Core days: every available room in parallel, four one-hour slots each, on one shared timetable

Accepted 2026-10-12 by the organiser
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`,
question 1).

## What

On each core day the program runs in parallel in all available rooms at
IPI Academy, seven at most
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--all-available-rooms.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--parallel-rooms.md`). Every room
follows the same timetable: slots at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00, and
breaks at 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00 (see
[Dates: two core days](two-core-days-first-week.md)). A slot is a talk,
a panel or another one-hour format
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--day-chain-and-session-format.md`).
The talks are the conference's keynotes
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--keynotes-and-first-week-planning.md`),
and slots are filled as proposals come in, on interest and quality
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--slots-filled-as-we-go.md`).

## Why

The organiser asked for parallel rooms and every available room. IPI's
own notes already suggested two to three talk tracks with parallel
workshops (`sources/conversations/2026-06--org--ipi-visit-notes.md`). A
shared timetable means every break lines up, so people move between
rooms and all meet on Freedom Square at the same time.

## How

The rooms are IPI's, as listed on the [IPI Academy](../ipi-academy.md)
page: the ground-floor hall (planned at about 120 seats), the first-floor
hall (100), the second-floor room (34), the two computer centres (20 and
26), the top-floor room (24) and the former student club (13 to 14)
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).
Each session is capped by its own room. Registration is one free pass
per day, not per room; seats in a room go to whoever is there first, and
the door volunteer closes a full room.

The program page shows every room and slot for both days. A slot is
empty until a proposal is accepted; each accepted session becomes a
record in the repository and the page is rebuilt from the records, as
the [website decision](goatconf-ba-and-google-forms.md) sets out.

**All seven rooms.** IPI Academy has given GoatConf access to all seven
rooms on both core days, as the organiser reported on 2026-10-12
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`). The program page lists all seven.

## Alternatives

- **One track per core day.** Rejected by the organiser in favour of
  parallel rooms.
- **Two or three parallel rooms.** Rejected: the organiser chose all
  available rooms.
- **Registration per room.** Rejected: one pass per day keeps a single
  form and lets people move between rooms.

## Consequences

- **Closes:** the one-track rule, "no parallel talks", the four-talk
  limit and the single core-day cap of about 120.
- **Opens:** up to 28 slots a day and 56 across both days, filled as
  proposals arrive.
- **Costs:** small rooms fill first; more sessions to judge; speaker
  travel stays unpaid unless a supporter funds grants.

## Amendments

- **2026-10-12, Friday starts at 09:30 for Džuma.** Friday 4 June keeps four one-hour slots but starts half an hour earlier: welcome 09:15, slots at 09:30, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30, and the end at 16:30, so the midday hour off, 12:30 to 13:30, covers Džuma prayer (podne in Tuzla is at 12:44 that day) and lunch. Thursday keeps 10:00 to 17:00. The organiser wants it kept easy to change; each day's times live in one place in the program generator (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--friday-dzuma-schedule.md, sources/web/tuzla/vaktija-tuzla-2027-06-03-04.md).
