---
title: "The Banja Luka train: two passenger trains a day, not one"
date: 2026-11-02
kind: conversation
---

On the travel page's draft line "From Banja Luka, one train a day reaches Doboj, leaving at 04:32", the organiser asked, verbatim:

> The time when the train leaves... Is it morning or afternoon? Are you sure it's a passenger train?

The agent had taken the line from a timetable aggregator (redvoznje.ba). Checked against ŽRS's own timetable and passenger fare list (sources/web/travel/zrs-banja-luka-doboj-2026-11-02.md), two passenger trains a day run each way: Banja Luka 04:32 to Doboj 06:38 and Banja Luka 15:38 to Doboj 17:45; back, Doboj 04:15 and 15:30. The fare is 10.40 KM.

Done on 2026-11-02, on the same pull request: the travel page names both trains, says the afternoon one meets a bus to Tuzla (about 4 hours in all, about as long as the bus all the way), and that the timetable for June 2027 is not out yet.
