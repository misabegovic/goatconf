Pick a goat

Your goat lives in this browser only: nothing about it is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser's data lets it go. You can swap it for another at any time.

How it works

Goatchi is a little game like a tamagotchi. Your goat gets hungry, bored and sleepy over about a day and a half. Left alone it sulks and says so, but it never dies: feed it, play with it or let it sleep, and it is glad you came back.

A speaker's goat can be kept once that speaker has said yes. The goats, the name GoatConf and Goatchi are all rights reserved.
