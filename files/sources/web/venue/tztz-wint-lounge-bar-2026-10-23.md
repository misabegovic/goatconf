Combining a warm and inviting atmosphere, exceptional service, an extensive selection of beverages, and outstanding music, WINT Lounge Bar captures attention from the very first moment and invites guests to explore an interior where everyone can find their perfect spot to relax.
Inspired by the elegance of traditional Viennese cafés, WINT Lounge Bar features a sophisticated blend of luxurious materials such as velvet and marble, ambient lighting, and carefully selected golden details that create a unique setting. The result is an atmosphere that transports guests to another era while offering all the comforts of a modern lounge experience.
Renowned for its premium service, impressive drink selection, and creative cocktail menu, WINT Lounge Bar has earned a reputation that extends far beyond Tuzla, making it one of the most recognizable lounge destinations in Bosnia and Herzegovina and the wider region.
WINT Lounge Bar is located at:
ZAVNOBiH-a 13, Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina
(within the former Hotel Tuzla building)
