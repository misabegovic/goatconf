Park Pub is one of the best-equipped venues of its kind in Tuzla, offering visitors everything a modern pub should provide.
It is a place of daily live music performances, yet still set just on the edge of the city’s bustle, close to the Central City Park and only a hundred meters from the Panonian Lakes.
True beer enthusiasts will especially appreciate the atmosphere, as the pub offers a variety of draft beers. To make the stay and socializing even more enjoyable, guests can also enjoy a rich gastronomic selection.
