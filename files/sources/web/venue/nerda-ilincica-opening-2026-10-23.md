Opening Ceremony for New Tourist Attractions at Ilinčica
01.11.2024 11:50
Today, in the courtyard of the Nature House at Ilinčica, an opening ceremony was held for new tourist attractions that were realized through the project "New Outdoor Locations for the Purpose of Improving the Quality of Tourism Products - Open-Air Everywhere".
As part of the Ilinčica park, the project has created a recreational-adrenaline park that includes a zip-line course, an adrenaline route, a recreational park for children, and an artificial climbing rock. In addition to improving tourism infrastructure, the Project had a special focus on creating conditions for sustainable management of targeted tourist locations and improving the quality of services in the tourism sector. As part of these activities, joint cross-border training sessions were organized on the topics of branding tourist destinations and communication tailored to the needs of different groups of tourists.
The training sessions were intended for representatives of relevant public institutions involved in the tourism sector and service providers in this sector.
