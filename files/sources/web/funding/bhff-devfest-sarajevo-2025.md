We’re proud to share that the BH Futures Foundation supported three of our scholars in attending DevFest Sarajevo 2025, one of the most inspiring technology conferences in Bosnia and Herzegovina.
DevFest isn’t just a tech event — it’s a global learning and networking experience that brings together communities from around the world to connect, learn, and build the future together.
Organized by Google Developer Group (GDG) Sarajevo the conference gathered leading voices from Google DeepMind, the United Nations, and experts across Europe to explore how AI, Cloud Computing, and Data Science are transforming industries and societies.
By attending, our scholars gained hands-on experience, fresh insights, and the chance to connect with innovators from around the world — experiences that will continue to inspire their academic and professional journeys.
Because empowering young talent to learn, grow, and lead the future is what we stand for — here’s to their bright futures and the impact they will create.
