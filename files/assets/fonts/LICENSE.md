# Fonts

Both faces are served from this folder and licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which allows bundling them with a
website.

- **Bricolage Grotesque** by Mathieu Triay, from Google Fonts
  (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque), variable weight
  200 to 800 and width 75 to 100 percent, latin and latin-ext subsets.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** by the Braille Institute of America, from
  Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible+Next),
  variable weight, latin and latin-ext subsets, plus latin italic.

Downloaded on 2026-10-07 from fonts.gstatic.com.
