other choices. The organiser picked "Engineering and Data": the talk's
ontology, identity across repositories and measured facts against
inference are data-modelling ideas.

Both speakers had picked Engineering, the one choice the form then
allowed. Their talks now show Engineering and Data (Dejan Menges) and
Engineering and AI/ML (Christoph Lipautz).
