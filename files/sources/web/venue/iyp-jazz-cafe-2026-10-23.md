Jazz Cafe
VI Bosanske brigade 39, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
It should be law for every city to have a top notch jazz cafe, and even better if it can be called ‘Jazz Cafe’. All boxes ticked in Tuzla on that front, and the frequent live music events and excellent furniture only adds to the experience. Jazz Cafe is also one of the best spots in Tuzla for anyone looking to drink Bosnian craft beers, with Brčko’s Laufer occasionally available. There is a real living room fell to the place too.
