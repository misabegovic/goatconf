The supporters

The companies and communities that help keep GoatConf free, and how to join them.

Who makes it possible

GoatConf is free to attend. Companies and communities help keep it that way. Supporters cannot buy talks, stage time or the attendee list, and who speaks is never decided by who supports. See the charter.

Support GoatConf online

Reach the 120 to 300 engineers, designers, product people and founders who come to Tuzla. One level, the same for every supporter: 500 euro, plus VAT where it applies.

  • Your logo and a link on this page and on the home page.
  • One post about you on GoatConf's social channels.
  • A mention in the emails to everyone registered.

It is online only: no booth, no banner and nothing at the venue. The money pays the event's costs.

You can also pay a supplier directly for one named thing, such as the printing or a round of coffee, or give something in kind.

If you speak at GoatConf and your company covers your travel, your company is listed here as a supporter too, with its logo and link, the same as online supporters.

If you or your organisation fund and organise an event we accept as part of GoatConf 2027, you are listed here as a supporter too, if you agree, with your logo and link, the same as online supporters.

To support GoatConf, reach Muhamed, the organiser, through the contact form or at info@muhamed.at.
