# Speaker: Roy Tomeij

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-20 before his session
has a slot or a topic; he asked to be listed as "Co-founder of AppSignal"
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--roy-tomeij.md). His public
profile is the record `speakers/roy-tomeij.md`, built into
`site/speaker-roy-tomeij.html`. This page records only what the sources say
and what bears on GoatConf.

## Scope

- **Who:** "Founder · Operator · Advisor · Investor"; "I’ve built and operated companies myself, including co-founding AppSignal ($22M Series A)." He now works "with founders and leadership teams on customer experience, operations, and growth" (source: sources/web/speakers/roytomeij-com-2026-10-20.md).
- **AppSignal:** an "APM and Logging solution for Ruby on Rails" whose founders "have been part of the Rails community since 2005" and went full-time on it in 2013; it joined the Rails Foundation as a contributing member in April 2023, with a quote from him. In May 2025 it closed a USD 22 million growth investment, and AppSignal's post titles him "Chief Customer Officer & Co-Founder", focused "on our customers and the communities we serve" (source: sources/web/speakers/appsignal-roy-tomeij-2026-10-20.md).
- **Talks:** nine on RubyEvents, from Amsterdam.rb in 2011 and EuRuKo 2012 to RailsConf 2014, 2016 and 2018 ("Empathy through Acting"), mostly on front-end code (source: sources/web/speakers/rubyevents-roy-tomeij-2026-10-20.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet.
- **Writes about:** SaaS, operations and building companies, on roytomeij.com, including "Written With AI, Not by AI" (April 2026) (source: sources/web/speakers/roytomeij-com-2026-10-20.md).
- **Bio on the site:** his own site's introduction, retold in the third person, on the organiser's choice (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--roy-tomeij.md).
- **Role on the site and cards:** "Co-founder of AppSignal", as he asked (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--roy-tomeij.md).
- **Photo:** the portrait on his own site, 1024 by 1024, resized to 800 by 800; not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision (sources: sources/web/speakers/roytomeij-com-2026-10-20.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--roy-tomeij.md).
- **Order:** second on the speakers list, after Paul Campbell; his arrival added a seventh reserved main-hall slot, Thursday 16:00 (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--roy-tomeij.md).
