The agent asked the seven open questions of the AI-suggested PRD
`wiki/org/ai-suggestions/prds/hosts-hub-guides-and-venues.md` one at a
time, as the organiser asked ("Ok, ask me in order"). The organiser's
answers, the chosen option's label quoted, or their own words verbatim:

1. **Events between sessions.** Asked what hosts may run on Thursday and
   Friday while the core days are on, the organiser chose "Hours off only
   (Recommended)": short, free, open events near IPI during the hours off,
   never at the same time as sessions, with Friday 12:30 kept free for
   Džuma and lunch.
2. **The church in glass.** Asked whether "crkva u staklu" is Hrvatski
   kulturni centar "Sveti Franjo" in the old town, the organiser chose
   "Yes, that's it".
3. **Editions.** Asked how firmly the site should say that each edition
   takes its shape from who joins, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

   > It only shapes the current edition, every edition could  be different based on who joins

4. **Pages.** Asked whether to have one hub page or keep "Events people
   add" and "Propose an event", the organiser chose "One hub
   (Recommended)": one page with what people are adding, the guides (how,
   what, where), the places with filters, then the form, with the old
   addresses redirected.
5. **Arrangements with venues.** The organiser chose "List only for now
   (Recommended)": every venue reads "a place you could ask"; the agent
   may draft emails the organiser could send later.
6. **Photos.** Asked whether to ask venues for permission or use only
   freely licensed photos, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

   > Use all photos that are good, disregard that

7. **Karaoke.** Asked whether anyone local knows a karaoke place, the
   organiser wrote, verbatim:

   > Imas kafica blizu, tipa na trgu

   Translated by the agent: "There are cafés nearby, like on the square."
   Read as: a karaoke host can use a café near IPI, for example on Freedom
   Square, rather than a bar with a standing karaoke night.
