Read on 2026-10-13 from the two conference sites' repositories on the organiser's machine, which are the public sites' sources.

- **EuRuKo 2024:** https://2024.euruko.org, Sarajevo, "SEPTEMBER 11-13 2024", at Hotel Hills. The site has 46 speaker pages, including Yukihiro "Matz" Matsumoto (closing keynote), José Valim (keynote), Dave Thomas (keynote), Xavier Noria (opening keynote), Koichi Sasada, Jean Boussier and Obie Fernandez. Logo: the red hexagon with the goat (`assets/images/logo.svg`).
- **RubyConf Austria 2026:** https://rubyconf.at, Vienna, Friday 29 May 2026 at Haus der Musik and Saturday 30 May 2026 at Das MuTh; the only edition so far. Its 21 speakers include Chad Fowler (opening keynote), Dave Thomas (closing keynote), Obie Fernandez (keynote), Charles Oliver Nutter (workshop), Bozhidar Batsov, Andrzej Krzywda, and José Valim and Armin Ronacher on the AI panel. Logo: the red piano mark (`assets/images/logo.svg`, mark only).
- **Photos:** RubyConf Austria 2026's 85 event photos are public at https://rubyconf.at/photos/, each watermarked "© Alessandro Bruckheimer". The organiser said they have the rights to reuse them on GoatConf (sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--past-conferences-on-the-site.md). No EuRuKo 2024 event photos were found.
