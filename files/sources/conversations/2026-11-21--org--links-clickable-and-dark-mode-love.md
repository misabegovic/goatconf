`site/less-blue-minimal`
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--minimal-chosen-phone-hooves-light-and-dark.md),
and told that two supporter logos nearly vanish in the dark theme and that

> Ok, let's improve them... Let's improve how we show links in general on the site and footer links etc... let's make sure that what's clickable looks clickable with taste... Also the dark mode needs more love. Let's start improving everything

Read as: the supporter logos and the maps are fixed in the dark theme;
links get one small, consistent system across the site, the footer
included, so that a reader can tell at a glance what is clickable and what
is not, and nothing that is not a link looks like one; the dark theme is
made beautiful and not only working; and where the light theme is weak it
is improved too.

Nothing is pushed, merged or published before the organiser approves it
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).

Later in the same round the organiser added, verbatim:

> Ok. Also maybe optimize in the hero section and remove the "an excuse to..." Text to remove noise?

Read as: the paragraph "An excuse to hang out with like-minded people and
learn from folks sharing their current best." leaves the home page's
cover, and the cover is tightened as a whole. The organiser asked only
about the hero, so the same sentence stays wherever else it is used until
they decide.
