## Area pitches

- [Website and registration](website-and-registration.md): plain HTML pages written here and published under muhamed.at, pretix's free quota for registration, collect only what is needed. Appetite: small.
- [Design with impeccable](design-with-impeccable.md): PRODUCT.md from the brain, one visual world, the surfaces in order, a host kit. Appetite: small.
- [Social presence and the drumbeat](social-presence-and-drumbeat.md): an email list, three channels, one public reason every week, things people can share. Appetite: small.
- [CFP and speakers](cfp-and-speakers.md): an honest self-funded CFP, invited names first, a sponsor-funded grant pool, an anonymised committee. Appetite: medium.

- [Dates, format and schedule](dates-format-and-schedule.md): six date options for IPI (first or second week of June 2027; a Saturday core, weekday lecture days, or all midweek); one core day wrapped in workshop, fringe and lake days; three to four talks with coffee and lunch on Freedom Square, one to two hours each. Appetite: medium.
- [The budget](budget-a-goat-and-a-menu.md): a 1,000 EUR base (half of it reserve), a menu of stretch goals supporters fund by paying suppliers directly, no money through the organiser, and a public ledger from the first payment. Appetite: small.
- [Activities](activities-the-lake-and-the-town.md): the cheap city as the gift, a lake day, a town-wide goat hunt, every other activity an extension. Appetite: small.
- [Operations](operations-people-rules-and-the-day.md): run from Vienna without a co-organiser, IPI students and community volunteers who each own a domain, the code of conduct, the gathering and data rules, the day, after. Appetite: medium.
- [The road to June 2027](the-road-to-june-2027.md): one dated calendar for every area, starting this month. Appetite: small.

## How to bet

Read the umbrella first, then any area. Betting on a pitch means saying so in your own words; it then graduates into committed work (the umbrella into an epic, each area into a child of it), and this page marks it superseded. Each pitch ends with its open questions; answering them is the fastest way to sharpen it.
