# Tuzla for GoatConf

The facts about the city that the pitches build on, as found on
2026-10-07. Prices move; each one carries its source and date, and the
public travel page should say "last checked" with a date, as Bansko Nomad
Fest does. Amounts in KM convert at the fixed rate of 1.95583 KM to the
euro (`sources/web/tuzla/cbbh-exchange-rate-2026-10-07.md`).

The city's history, the goat legend and statue, the Kapija, and a walk
through the old town are on their own page:
[Tuzla's history, the goat and the old town on foot](tuzla/history-and-old-town.md).
In one line: a salt city since the Neolithic, named from the Turkish
*tuz* (salt), industrial under Austria-Hungary, multi-ethnic by habit,
and fond of a goat that one family hid when goats were banned
(`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia-bs.md`,
`sources/web/tuzla/mondo-tuzlanska-koza-legenda.md`).

## Getting there

- **Tuzla International Airport (TZL)** is at Dubrave Gornje, Živinice,
  "about 13 km from Tuzla" by the airport's own count; the tourist board
  says 15 or 16 km (`sources/web/travel/tzl-kako-do-nas.md`,
  `tztz-tuzla-airport.md`).
- **Wizz Air** reopened its Tuzla base on 2025-12-12 and added a second
  aircraft on 2026-03-29 (`sources/web/travel/tzl-wizz-base-reopening-2025.md`,
  `tzl-wizz-second-aircraft-2026.md`). Launch fares from 40.99 KM (about
  21 EUR) were announced to Cologne, Maastricht, Malmö, Berlin, Larnaca
  and Paris Beauvais, with Göteborg, Hamburg, Frankfurt-Hahn and
  Bratislava also announced (same sources). The live board on 2026-10-07
  showed Berlin, Göteborg, Memmingen, Basel-Mulhouse, Maastricht, Köln
  and Frankfurt-Hahn (`sources/web/travel/tzl-flight-board-2026-10-07.md`).
  The June 2027 schedule is (unknown, needs source); a search on
  2026-10-07 found no summer 2027 announcement.
- **The airport itself** is the second largest in Bosnia and
  Herzegovina, a former military field, with a 1998 terminal sized for
  350 passengers in the peak hour, one gate and two check-in desks; it
  is open to airlines from 03:30 to midnight
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tzl-airport-wikipedia-history.md`). It has ATMs, an
  exchange office and a café, and posts an accessibility notice for
  passengers with reduced mobility; its address is Gornje Dubrave bb,
  75270 Živinice (`sources/web/tuzla/tzl-airport-home-2026-10-07.md`).
  Wikipedia lists Wizz Air to Basel-Mulhouse, Berlin, Bratislava,
  Cologne, Dortmund, Hahn, Hamburg, Maastricht, Malmö and Memmingen,
  seasonal Gothenburg and Larnaca
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tzl-airport-wikipedia-destinations.md`), plus a
  weekly Pegasus flight from
  Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen announced in 2024; Wizz Air first based an
  aircraft here in 2015 and Lumiwings' base lasted from December 2023 to
  February 2024 (`sources/web/tuzla/tzl-airport-wikipedia-history.md`). Wizz Air closed its earlier base in
  September 2023 and added Vienna to Tuzla four times a week from 15 June
  2025 (`sources/web/tuzla/exyu-wizz-vienna-tuzla-2025.md`); whether it still
  does is (unknown, needs source). Bases have come and gone here, so
  the public travel page should not promise a route before it is on
  sale.
- **Airport to the city:** a private transfer at 25 EUR for a car, about
  12 minutes (`sources/web/travel/easybalkan-tzl-transfers.md`); a taxi
  estimated at 13 to 17 USD (`sources/web/travel/rome2rio-tzl-airport-tuzla.md`).
  A public bus from the airport is (unknown, needs source).
- **From Sarajevo:** the airport is 122 km away, about 2 h 10 min by
  private transfer from 27 USD per person
  (`sources/web/travel/daytrip-sjj-airport-tuzla.md`); buses from the
  Sarajevo bus station take about 3 to 3.5 hours at about 23 to 39 KM
  (`sources/web/travel/obilet-tuzla-sarajevo-bus.md`,
  `rome2rio-sarajevo-tuzla.md`).
- **From neighbouring capitals by bus** (undated aggregator estimates):
  Belgrade about 4 h 20 min, Zagreb about 5 h 30 min, Osijek about 4 to
  5.5 hours with a change (`sources/web/travel/rome2rio-belgrade-tuzla.md`,
  `rome2rio-zagreb-tuzla.md`, `rome2rio-osijek-tuzla.md`).
- **Long-distance buses:** the tourist board says weekly buses run from
  Tuzla to Sweden, the Netherlands, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and
  Slovenia, and regular lines to the bigger cities of Serbia and Croatia
  (`sources/web/travel/tztz-how-to-reach-tuzla.md`). Times and fares from
  Vienna are (unknown, needs source).
- **In town:** a city bus ticket is 2.50 KM (above), and the tourist
  train, Nextbike bike share and free city Wi-Fi are on the
  [old town page](tuzla/history-and-old-town.md#getting-around-the-centre)
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-tourist-train.md`, `tztz-nextbike.md`,
  `tztz-wireless-city.md`).

## Money, phones and papers

- **Money.** The convertible mark (KM, BAM) is fixed to the euro (above).
  The airport has ATMs and an exchange office
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tzl-airport-home-2026-10-07.md`). How widely cards
  are taken in cafés and at the lakes is (unknown, needs source).
- **Phones.** Bosnia and Herzegovina is outside the EU's "Roam Like at
  Home" area, which today covers the EU, the EEA, Moldova and Ukraine.
  The Council authorised talks to extend it to the Western Balkans on
  2026-06-04, but each country must first align with EU roaming law and
  the Commission must confirm it before charges go
  (`sources/web/tuzla/ewb-eu-wb-roaming-talks-2026-06.md`). Whether that
  happens before June 2027 is (unknown, needs source); the travel page
  should assume EU visitors pay roaming and point to a local SIM or eSIM
  (suggestion).
- **Visas.** EU, UK and US citizens need no visa for up to 90 days in
  six months; EU citizens, among others, can enter with an ID card
  (`sources/web/tuzla/visa-policy-bih-wikipedia.md`). The UK government
  lists cultural events among visa-free purposes, asks for a passport
  issued less than 10 years before arrival and valid 90 days after
  leaving, and advises getting it stamped in and out
  (`sources/web/tuzla/fcdo-bih-entry-requirements.md`).
- **Registration.** All foreigners must register with the police within
  72 hours; hotels usually do it for their guests, and people staying
  elsewhere must do it themselves (same source). Speakers and attendees
  in private flats need telling (suggestion).
- **Coming home through Schengen.** The EU Entry/Exit System can add time
  at Schengen borders (`sources/web/tuzla/fcdo-bih-overview-ees.md`).
- **Health cover.** The EHIC and GHIC do not cover Bosnia and
  Herzegovina, so travel insurance matters; pharmacies may not accept
  foreign prescriptions (`sources/web/tuzla/fcdo-bih-health.md`).

## Staying and eating

- **Hotels:** a metasearch showed averages of about 62 to 112 GBP a night
  for central hotels on 2026-10-07, changing daily
  (`sources/web/tuzla/hotelscombined-tuzla.md`); a 2026 travel guide puts
  mid-range Tuzla hotels at 40 to 65 EUR
  (`sources/web/tuzla/easybalkan-bosnia-costs-2026.md`).
- **No hostels** are listed on Hostelworld
  (`sources/web/tuzla/hostelworld-tuzla.md`). The tourist board lists 18
  hotels, motels and guest houses and about 36 rooms or apartments,
  without prices (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-accommodation-hotels.md`,
  `tztz-rooms-apartments.md`). Apartment prices are (unknown, needs
  source).
- **Where to book:** the metasearch aggregators (Booking.com,
  HotelsCombined and similar) carry the stock, about 457 properties in
  Tuzla on 2026-10-07, bookable there or directly with the hotel
  (`sources/web/tuzla/hotelscombined-tuzla.md`); the tourist board's
  lists point at the smaller guest houses and private rooms
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-accommodation-hotels.md`,
  `tztz-rooms-apartments.md`). Booking about six weeks ahead is the
  aggregator's own savings advice (44 days ahead showed about 25 GBP a
  night). Whether IPI arranges a block of hotel rooms for attendees was
  being clarified after the June 2026 visit
  (`sources/conversations/2026-06--org--ipi-visit-notes.md`); if it does,
  the site should point attendees at that block first.
- **Food and coffee** (Numbeo, 2026-10-02, 16 contributors): an
  inexpensive meal 9 KM (about 4.60 EUR), a cappuccino 3.25 KM (about
  1.66 EUR), a draft beer 4.50 KM, a local bus ticket 2.50 KM
  (`sources/web/tuzla/numbeo-cost-of-living-tuzla.md`). The travel guide
  gives a restaurant dinner at 10 to 16 EUR and a coffee at about 1 EUR
  (`sources/web/tuzla/easybalkan-bosnia-costs-2026.md`).
- **Taxis:** 3 KM to start plus 1.61 KM per mile; three large companies
  (`sources/web/tuzla/numbeo-taxi-tuzla.md`, `tztz-taxi.md`).

## Food and where to eat

- **Ćevapi** are the national grill dish: small rolls of minced meat,
  usually beef, with onion and spices, served in lepinja or somun with
  chopped onion and often kajmak or yoghurt, in portions of five, ten or
  more at a ćevabdžinica (`sources/web/tuzla/cevapi-wikipedia-bs.md`).
  What makes Tuzla's ćevapi Tuzla's is (unknown, needs source): a search
  on 2026-10-07 summarised food sites as describing a mutton, beef and
  lamb mix served in lepinja dipped in bone broth, but the pages were
  blocked and are not snapshotted. Burek and the best-known local grills
  are (unknown, needs source). The organiser's 2023 pitch promised
  vegan and vegetarian options (`sources/web/tuzla/rubyevents-city-pitch-tuzla.md`).
  A menu search on 2026-10-13 (`sources/web/tuzla/vegetarian-places-research-2026-10-13.md`) found no fully vegetarian or vegan
  place near IPI, but menus with meat-free dishes at Pizza Bar, Pekara Hukić,
  Mozaik (which also sells sirnica, krompiruša and zeljanica), Caffe Pizzeria
  Old School Pub and Zmaj od Bosne (a vegetarian platter), all on the travel
  page's list; the one labelled vegan dish nearby, a sandwich at Zdravljak
  Bosna, was not listed by the organiser's choice (`sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--more-vegetarian-and-vegan.md`).
- **What dishes cost.** Prices read on 2026-10-13, mostly from Tuzla menus on Korpa.ba (`sources/web/tuzla/dish-prices-2026-10-13.md`): grah 6 to 13 KM, begova čorba 6 to 9 KM, ćevapi 7 to 10 KM for five and 12 to 15 KM for ten, tufahija 3.5 to 5 KM, hurmašice 1.7 to 4 KM; pita (zeljanica, sirnica, krompiruša, burek) about 3 to 5.5 KM for 250 g, with one Tuzla menu (Mozaik, 1.7 KM per 100 g) and the rest Bosnia-wide; sataraš and Bosnian coffee only from Sarajevo; no price found for đul pita, which the home page replaced with uštipci (10 KM at Pizza Bar, with cheese and kajmak; `sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--ustipci-instead-of-dul-pita.md`). Punjene paprike costs 6 to 14 KM on three Tuzla menus (`sources/web/tuzla/punjene-paprike-prices-2026-10-13.md`) and replaced sataraš on the home page (`sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--punjene-paprike-instead-of-sataras.md`). Sarma costs 6 to 14 KM on the same three Tuzla menus (`sources/web/tuzla/sarma-prices-2026-10-13.md`) and replaced uštipci on the home page (`sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--sarma-instead-of-ustipci.md`). The home page shows these in euro (`sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--food-prices.md`).
- **Beer.** Pivara Tuzla was founded in 1884 as the Erste Dampf
  Bräuerei, brews a Czech-style pilsner with water piped from Konjuh, and
  makes Tuzlanski pilsner, Erster, a radler and Tuzlanski kiseljak
  mineral water (`sources/web/tuzla/pivara-tuzla-wikipedia-bs.md`). Its
  own pub, Pivnica Taverna, serves unfiltered beer at Maršala Tita 163
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-pivnica-taverna.md`). Tuzla Beer Days fall in
  May (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`), clear of June.
- **Cafés near the squares.** The tourist board lists Sloboda caffe bar
  at Solni trg 10 on Salt Square, a café that hosts bands and DJ nights
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-sloboda-caffe-bar.md`), and pages for
  Restoran Čaršijska česma, Citta del Sale, Restoran Zlatnik, Restorani
  Sezam, Park Pub, Old Story Pub, St James's Irish Pub, Nardi Pub, The
  Loft terrace bar, Dolce Vita, Cafe Cafe, Coffee Zone, Fabrika Coffe and
  Caffetteria Intermezzo, among others (names read from the page slugs)
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-sitemap-restaurants-cafes.md`). Which of them
  face Freedom Square, their seats and their prices for a group are
  (unknown, needs source); the breaks list should be built by walking
  the square (suggestion).
- **Central market** for fruit, vegetables and local products, in the
  centre since 1952 (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-central-market.md`).

## The salt lakes and the park

- **The Pannonian Lakes (Panonika)** are three artificial salt-water lakes
  in the city centre, filled from underground salt wells, opened in 2003,
  2008 and 2012, with salt waterfalls, an archaeological park and water
  slides; they won the UNWTO Ulysses award in 2012
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-pannonian-lakes.md`, `tuzla-wikipedia.md`). A
  36-seat tourist train runs from Freedom Square
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`).
- **Season opens on 1 June.** The organiser confirmed on 2026-10-13 that the lakes' season opens on 1 June, so they are open for swimming during GoatConf (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--lake-season-opens-1-june.md). Earlier reports varied: one says swimming
  "traditionally starts at the beginning of May", weather permitting
  (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-sezona-kurir-2026-04.md`); the tourist
  board's calendar lists the "Summer Season Opening" in June
  (`tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`); in 2019 the season opened on 7 June
  (`sources/web/tuzla/panonika-n1-2019.md`). The 2026 season closed on
  2026-09-13 with more than 300,000 visitors
  (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-kraj-sezone-kurir-2026-09.md`).
- **Tickets** in peak season 2026: adults 7 KM on weekdays, 9 KM at
  weekends (about 3.60 and 4.60 EUR)
  (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-cijene-kurir-2026-08.md`).
- **Slana banja park** sits next to the lakes, with paths, sports courts,
  cafés, views, war memorials and the Peace Flame House
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-salty-spa-park.md`,
  `sources/web/venue/tztz-peace-flame-house.md`).
- **An everyday hangout, not a single lake day.** The organiser's
  direction on 2026-10-08: the conference can go to the lake and chill
  every day, not only on one designated day after; and "the day after can
  be whatever", including a trip to Lukomir or somewhere else magical in
  Bosnia (`sources/conversations/2026-10-08--org--site-and-program-direction.md`).
  The season opens on 1 June (see Season above).

## June

- **Weather** (WMO normals 1991 to 2020): June highs average 26.1 °C, lows
  13.2 °C; June is the wettest month, 115.4 mm over about 11 rain days
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia.md`). An indoor core and a rain
  plan for activities follow from that.
- **Holidays:** Kurban Bajram 2027 falls on 16 or 17 May
  (`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-calendarlabs.md`, `holidays-2027-qppstudio.md`),
  clear of June. The Islamic New Year falls on Sunday 6 June 2027 and is
  listed as a regional holiday by one source
  (`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-officeholidays.md`) but not in the
  Federation's list of non-working days
  (`sources/web/tuzla/neradni-dani-fbih-2026-paragraf.md`); unverified.
  No other public holiday falls between 1 and 14 June 2027.
- **Local events in June** (tourist board calendar): the lakes' season
  opening, Astronomy Days (June to July), the Lege Artis choir
  championship, Summer at Pannonica, and others
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`). The Džumbus
  festival's 2026 edition ran from 5 June to 29 August
  (`sources/web/tuzla/dzumbus-festival-festivalfinder.md`). Their 2027
  dates are (unknown, needs source).
- **The Summer University (LJUT)** is the closest overlap: the
  University of Tuzla's 31st edition ran from 1 to 25 June 2026 with

## The rules

- **Gatherings (Tuzla Canton).** The canton's law on public gathering
  (Službene novine TK 1/12, amended 11/15) defines "other forms of
  gathering" as gatherings for economic, religious, cultural,
  humanitarian, sporting, entertainment and other interests "koji nemaju
  za cilj ostvarivanje prihoda" (not aimed at earning income), and says
  they "ne prijavljuju se" (are not notified), Article 35. Article 36 is
  the exception: the organiser must notify the police station at least
  five days ahead if the character or expected number of participants
  calls for additional security measures
  (`sources/web/legal/tk-zakon-o-javnom-okupljanju-1-12.md`). Fines for a
  required notification not made run from 3,000 to 9,000 KM for a legal
  person (Article 38, same source). A free, indoor, registered conference
  reads as an "other form of gathering"; this is a reading, not legal
  advice. New canton laws on public events and peaceful assembly were
  drafted in 2024 and listed as proposals due in June 2026
  (`sources/web/legal/tk-nacrt-zakona-o-javnim-priredbama-2024.md`,
  `tk-program-rada-skupstine-2026.md`); whether they passed is (unknown,
  needs source).
- **Personal data.** Bosnia and Herzegovina's personal data protection
  law (Službeni glasnik BiH 12/2025), aligned with the GDPR, has applied
  since early October 2025 (`sources/web/legal/bih-zakon-o-zastiti-licnih-podataka-12-25.md`,
  `paragraf-zzlp-primjena-2025-10-06.md`). It requires telling a person,
  when their data is collected, who the controller is, the legal basis,
  the purpose, how long it is kept, and their rights (Article 15, as
  summarised by the research helper).
