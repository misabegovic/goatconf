# The attendee

## Stated by the operator

- The goal is "to get great minds together in an affordable
  environment, hang out, spend time together, learn, share experiences".
- Attending is free; registration is probably required.
- Tuzla is cheap, and the event should make that a benefit; the salt
  lake is an attraction.

## Needs (hypothesis until sources say otherwise)

- To know the total cost of coming (travel, a bed, food) before
  committing, since the ticket is free but the trip is not.
- To get to Tuzla without friction: which airport, which bus, how long.
- Talks and workshops worth the trip, and time between them to meet
  people.
- A simple registration that does not ask for more than the event needs.

## What would make them not come (hypothesis)

- Uncertain dates late into the spring, which blocks cheap flights.
- A free event that feels like a sales funnel for sponsors or recruiters.
