After the speaker run was set for Tuesdays from 10 November
(sources/conversations/2026-11-02--org--speakers-one-by-one.md) and a Tuzla
post, "Named after Tuzla", was drafted for Saturday 19 December
(sources/conversations/2026-11-02--org--do-what-feels-best.md), the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I want to start announcing speakers starting from tomorrow, that post you prepared now doesn't sound relevant as much for the future of the event

The agent asked which post was meant and whether the pace stays weekly.
The organiser chose:

- **The post:** "Named after Tuzla" (19 December). It is dropped, with its
  cards.
- **Pace:** weekly on Tuesdays. Paul Campbell goes on Tuesday 3 November
  2026, and each speaker after him one Tuesday earlier than planned:
  Roy Tomeij 10 November, Carmine Paolino 17 November, Dejan Menges
  24 November, Amar Mahmutbegović 1 December, Cristian Planas
  8 December, Christoph Lipautz 15 December, Predrag Radenkovic
  22 December.

"7,000 years of salt", which held Tuesday 3 November, moves to Saturday
19 December, the slot the dropped post leaves. From Tuesday 29 December
2026 Tuesdays are news and Tuzla again. The registration post stays on
Saturday 9 January 2027, after the last speaker.
