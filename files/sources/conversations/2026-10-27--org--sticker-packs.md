Right after the speaker goats shipped, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I want these goats downloadable as sticker packs... we should have sticker packs for goatconf that can be downloaded... not only goats, just stickers in general for the conference

Asked one question at a time, the organiser chose:

- What the packs are: "Digital and printable (Recommended)", described as one set of stickers offered two ways: a zip of transparent PNGs for chats and slides (and a Telegram pack added with one tap), and a print-ready PDF sheet to print and cut or take to a print shop.
- What goes into the first pack, all four offered: "The goat's roles" (the mascot as speaker, host with fildžan, coder, pirate, hidden goat, cook and plain), "The speaker goats" (each speaker's own goat), "The mark and words" (the seal, the coin, "Yours?", "I'm speaking", "Tuzla 3–5 June 2027", "The one that feeds the community", hoof prints) and "Tuzla things" (salt crystal, the Pannonian sea, a fildžan, ćevapi, burek, the goat statue, the salt lakes).
- Where people get them: "A Stickers page (Recommended)", described as a new page, stickers.html, showing every sticker with the downloads (zip, print sheet, Telegram), linked from the footer and the brand page, with each speaker's page also offering their own goat sticker.

Shown the proposed ADR (wiki/org/adrs/sticker-packs.md) and asked "Do you approve the sticker packs ADR?", the organiser chose, verbatim:

> Approve, start building (Recommended)

described as: mark it accepted, merge it, then build step one (the Stickers page with the goat's roles and the mark and words, the zip and the print sheet) and show it in the browser before it ships.
