The program

Two days of talks in every room of IPI Academy, then a community day on Saturday. Every empty slot is waiting for someone.

How a day runs

Thursday 3rd and Friday 4th June 2027 are the core days, at IPI Academy, Kulina bana 2, Tuzla. Each day runs on one timetable for every room, so you can change rooms at every break.

Every slot is one hour: a talk with time for questions, a panel, or a workshop. After each slot comes a full hour off, in the cafés on Freedom Square under five minutes away. That is when you talk to the person you just heard.

Thursday runs from 10:00 to 17:00. Friday runs from 09:30 to 16:30.

GoatConf does not record the talks. You may bring a camera or a podcast and record, and podcasts are welcome in some of the rooms.

The slots fill as proposals come in and are accepted. Propose a session, or register for free to come and listen.

The rooms

  • Main hall, ground floor, about 120 seats
  • First-floor hall, 100 seats
  • Second-floor room, 34 seats
  • Computer centre 1, 20 seats
  • Computer centre 2, 26 seats
  • Top-floor room, 24 seats
  • The old student club, 13 seats

Closer to June, you will also register for the sessions you want, because each room has only so many seats. Everyone with a ticket gets an email with how. Seats nobody registered for go to whoever is at the door when a session starts.
