## https://carlosframework.com/

> An architecture for building and shipping uncompromising apps in the agentic age

> What makes CARLOS different?
> By combining GenAI, old school computer science principles, and embracing modern tech, CARLOS rejects both monoliths and microservices, in favour of many small full-stack instances.
> Better: 10× lower running costs from reducing AWS resources; 87% less JavaScript by rendering pages on the server
> Faster: 12× faster to ship by building with agents on CARLOS principles; 4.5× faster first render; 5× faster responses
> Stronger: 1–2s backup latency rather than nightly

> With CARLOS: Cloud provider, on-prem, or hybrid; CARLOS Platform; Single-process apps each with their own embedded database

> Numbers based on real data from Tito. The agents insisted on measuring everything.

> Who is using CARLOS?
> CARLOS is built by the people behind Tito and Jelly, serving real customers, and extracted from the lessons learned in the process.
> Tito: 12× faster to ship. Using the CARLOS principles, Team Tito build their latest release that combined their 10 year old Rails app and their most recent Pro app. CARLOS allows building new features at a velocity not seen before, and allowing customers to locate their data in any one of 7 regions.

> CARLOS is an AI-enabled project, and all CARLOS software to date has been entirely produced using a combination of LLM-based coding products from Anthropic, OpenAI, and open-weight alternatives.
