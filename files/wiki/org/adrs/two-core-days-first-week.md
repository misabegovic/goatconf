# Dates: two core days, Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June 2027, with Saturday 5 June free for the community

Accepted 2026-10-12 by the organiser, who approved this decision and the
three beside it in one answer
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`,
question 1). Supersedes
[the one-core-day timing decision](dates-and-the-core-day.md).

## What

GoatConf 2027 has two core days, Thursday 3 June and Friday 4 June 2027,
in the first week of June, and Saturday 5 June is free for community
activity and the salt lakes
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--two-core-days-and-saturday.md`).
Each core day runs from 10:00 to 17:00, with a welcome at 09:45. One-hour
slots start at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00, and a one-hour break
follows each slot except the last, at 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`,
question 4; `sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--day-chain-and-session-format.md`).
A slot is a talk, a panel or another format of one hour, as the organiser
decides. How the slots run across rooms is set in
[Core days: parallel rooms](core-day-parallel-tracks.md).

## Why

The organiser chose the first week and two core days, with Saturday for
"community stuff, swimming in the lake etc."
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--two-core-days-and-saturday.md`).
The first week has no stated afternoon cut-off at IPI, while the second
week closes the rooms at 15:30 (`sources/conversations/2026-06--org--ipi-visit-notes.md`),
which a day ending at 17:00 would not fit. A one-hour break after every
slot keeps the original wish for a day that does not cram, with time to
hang out on Freedom Square
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--break-after-each-talk.md`).

## How

The site publishes the three days and the timetable. Breaks are on
Freedom Square, under five minutes from IPI; each person buys their own
coffee and lunch, or a supporter pays a café directly, and the organiser
pays for no food or drink
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
Saturday is not a core day: the organiser runs nothing on it beyond what
the activities decision already lists, and anyone may add an activity
through the add-something form. A lake day does not depend on swimming,
since the lakes' opening date varies year to year
(`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-sezona-kurir-2026-04.md`).

**IPI has accepted the dates.** IPI Academy accepted Thursday 3 and
Friday 4 June 2027, with Saturday 5 June, as the organiser reported on
2026-10-12 (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`). No written copy is in the brain.

## Alternatives

- **One core day, Friday 4 June,** the earlier option B. Rejected: the
  organiser wants two core days.
- **Second week of June.** Rejected: the rooms close at 15:30 there.
- **Keep the window open until IPI answers in writing.** Rejected: the
  organiser asked for the days to be shared now.

## Consequences

- **Closes:** the one-core-day rule in the dates PRD and the epic, and
  the earlier six-option list.
- **Opens:** two days of talks, a community day, and a published
  timetable that registration and the call can point to.
- **Costs:** IPI holds its rooms for two days; the first-week swim is
  less certain than the second week's
  (`sources/web/tuzla/panonika-n1-2019.md`).

## Amendments

- **2026-10-13, the lakes open on 1 June.** The lakes' swimming season opens on 1 June, as the organiser confirmed, so the first week of June no longer carries the swimming risk named under Costs (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--lake-season-opens-1-june.md).
- **2026-10-12, Friday starts at 09:30 for Džuma.** Friday 4 June keeps four one-hour slots but starts half an hour earlier: welcome 09:15, slots at 09:30, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30, and the end at 16:30, so the midday hour off, 12:30 to 13:30, covers Džuma prayer (podne in Tuzla is at 12:44 that day) and lunch. Thursday keeps 10:00 to 17:00. The organiser wants it kept easy to change; each day's times live in one place in the program generator (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--friday-dzuma-schedule.md, sources/web/tuzla/vaktija-tuzla-2027-06-03-04.md).
