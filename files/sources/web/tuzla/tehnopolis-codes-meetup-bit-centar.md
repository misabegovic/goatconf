We invite you to the meetup “Building the IT ecosystem: Community, support and opportunities” where we will talk about how a strong IT community is created and grows, and what opportunities exist for people who want to develop ideas.

Our guest is Nenad Stojanović, director of the DELi space for creative action from Niš – who has been helping organizations and teams for years to turn their ideas into projects and find support for them.

He is particularly dedicated to the development of the IT community and creating opportunities for new technological ideas.

crowdfunding platforms and other ways to get support for your idea

opportunities for networking, collaboration and project development

If you are interested in the startup world, IT ideas or want to meet people who think similarly to you – this is the right place for you.

The activity is organized within the CODES project, co-financed by the European Union through the Interreg VI-A IPA Programme Croatia – Bosnia and Herzegovina – Montenegro 2021–2027, and implemented by Intera Technology Park from Mostar, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center Tehnopolis (IEC Tehnopolis) from Niksic, BIT Center Tuzla, City of Zadar and the University of Zadar.
