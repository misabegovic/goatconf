Found on 2026-10-13 through Bing image search and the Korpa.ba menus of Tuzla restaurants, and chosen by the organiser from labelled sheets, one dish at a time (sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--food-photos-one-by-one.md). Recorded as found.

**These photos are not freely licensed.** They come from recipe sites, news sites, blogs, Pinterest and restaurant menus, and their owners hold the rights. They are used on the organiser's decision, who takes a photo down if its owner asks. The site shows no credits (sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--no-photo-credits.md). Each is cropped to 4:3 and resized to 1200 by 900; the begova čorba crop also cuts out the restaurant's signature.
