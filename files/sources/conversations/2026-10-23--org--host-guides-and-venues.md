The Bosnian part, translated by the agent: "I mean, people can organise

Read together:

- **Guides:** the organiser floats ("maybe") guides that teach people how
  to add something to GoatConf.
- **Venues:** a list of potential venues and locations, naming five: Kuća
  plamena mira (Peace Flame House), the Ismet Mujezinović atelier, "dom
  mladih", the National Theatre (Narodno pozorište) and "crkva u stakly",
  a typo for "crkva u staklu", literally "the church in glass", an
  informal name the agent could not yet match to a place.
- **Between sessions:** events people add may also happen between the
  sessions, not only around the conference. Today the site does not offer
  this: an event is anything a host runs from 2 to 6 June, and an hour
  slot at IPI Academy on Thursday or Friday is a session the organiser
  picks (`site/add.html`).
