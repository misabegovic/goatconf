# Speaker: Jan Grodowski

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-14 before his session
has a slot or a topic, and listed fourth, after Dejan Menges; he agreed to
a public profile and to promotion on social media (source:
sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--jan-grodowski.md). His public
profile is the record `speakers/jan-grodowski.md`. From 2026-10-15 he is
held from the site while his employer clears the engagement, which is
still open, so no page shows him until the record's `hold` line comes off
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-15--org--hold-jan-grodowski.md).
This page records only what the sources say and what bears on GoatConf.
He is not the historian Jan Grabowski.

## Scope

- **Held from the site:** from 2026-10-15, on the organiser's
  instruction, because "all talking engagements need to be cleared by
  Shopify for him" and his confirmation with the company is still open.
  Nothing is deleted: the record stays with its checks and its `order`
  (fourth, after Dejan Menges), the speakers and home pages, the files
  for agents and the sitemap leave him out, and his page
  `site/speaker-jan-grodowski.html` is removed on the next build. His
  "I'm speaking" cards stay in the repository. Removing the `hold` line
  publishes him again (source:
  sources/conversations/2026-10-15--org--hold-jan-grodowski.md).
- **Who:** "Staff software engineer at Shopify. Salzburg, Austria.", on the Database Platform team, "building tools for schema migrations and data partitioning across their global infrastructure" (source: sources/web/speakers/grodowski-com-2026-10-14.md).
- **undercover:** the Ruby gem he wrote, "Actionable code coverage - detects untested code blocks in recent changes", at 32,381,761 downloads on 2026-10-14 (version 0.8.5, April 2026); and UndercoverCI, the GitHub App he runs on it, which now pitches coverage checks for AI-written pull requests (source: sources/web/speakers/grodowski-com-2026-10-14.md).
- **Talks:** a workshop, "Test Coverage for Agentic Coding with Undercover", at RubyConf Austria 2026; "Rails Migrations at Scale: How Shopify Moved from Trigger-Based Online Schema Changes to gh-ost" at Vienna.rb in September 2026, about thousands of schema changes a year on a 1.5 PB sharded MySQL database; "Balancing Quality and Speed of Testing with the Undercover Gem" at Vienna.rb in March 2025; a lightning talk at wroclove.rb 2016 (source: sources/web/speakers/grodowski-com-2026-10-14.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet.
- **Bio on the site:** the text on grodowski.com word for word, without its emoji, plus one sentence added on the organiser's choice: "The undercover gem has been downloaded over 32 million times." (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--jan-grodowski.md). The download count will grow; the sentence says "over".
- **Role on the site and cards:** "Staff Software Engineer at Shopify, author of undercover" (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--jan-grodowski.md).
- **Photo:** his GitHub avatar; not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision (source: sources/web/speakers/grodowski-com-2026-10-14.md).
