The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Nice. Now do it for Tuzla, the host town photos, also for Eat and drink, with or without meat section and food photos

"Do it" refers to the earlier phone changes on the home page (What is
decided, the speakers, the supporters and the session formats), which
list stickers one by one. At 390 pixels wide, Tuzla's six photos were
two small columns, about 120 pixels tall each. The twelve food stickers
were two columns too, with uneven heights and prices that wrapped
("1.50–2.50 € a / portion").

Asked to choose, the organiser picked:

- Tuzla photos: square, like the speakers.
- Food: a big photo on top.

Built as, below 48rem: both lists stack in one column, at most 26rem
wide and centred. Tuzla's photos are square at full width, still before
the text (as decided earlier the same day). Each food photo is 4:3 at
full width, with a larger dish name, tag and price below it, so the
prices fit on one line. Tablets and desktop keep their grids. The words
did not change.
