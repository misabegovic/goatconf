title: "The organiser judges extension listings too"
date: 2026-10-12
kind: conversation
---

Asked whether "I judge everything" includes extension listings, which the charter says are listed, not curated, the organiser answered, verbatim:

> yes

Read as an answer to that question: the organiser judges extension listings as well as program proposals. That changes the charter's rule that a listing is checked against the charter and the code of conduct, never for taste, and the accepted extension decision that rests on it. Neither is changed yet. The charter was approved on 2026-10-10 and changes through its own approved change. This is not read as approval of the drafts, which is still open.
