# The drumbeat: the weekly posts, from launch to June 2027

The calendar of posts that [the social drumbeat decision](../adrs/social-drumbeat.md) asks for: one public reason to care about GoatConf a week, drafted here and published only by the organiser, or a volunteer the organiser names, because anything that reaches the public is the organiser's call (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--goatconf-will-be-public.md). Every post below is a draft until its state says published.

## How it runs

- **When:** one post every Tuesday, from Tuesday 20 October 2026 to Tuesday 1 June 2027 (33 posts), then one on each of the three event days and a thank-you on Tuesday 8 June 2027. Week 1, "GoatConf is happening", is already out; the date it went out was not recorded (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--social-plan-to-june.md).
- **Where:** GoatConf's own LinkedIn page (linkedin.com/company/goatconf) and X account (@goatconf), with Bluesky and Mastodon later, and Luma updates to registered guests for the practical news (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-15--org--announcing-on-linkedin-and-x.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--brand-seal-and-euruko-goat.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--registration-on-luma.md).
- **What each post has:** a LinkedIn text, a short text of at most 280 characters for X, Bluesky and Mastodon, and an image in `site/share/` made by `tools/shareables.py`: square for LinkedIn and Mastodon, wide for X and Bluesky. Links point at goatconf.com, so each post also shows the link preview.
- **Photos:** the post images use only photos given for GoatConf, with the credit their giver asked for ("Photo: Grad Tuzla"). The food photos on the site are not freely licensed, so no post image uses them (source: sources/web/images/food-photos-chosen-2026-10-13.md).
- **Before posting:** some posts carry a note (a price to recheck, a decision still to come). Check it, mark the state published here, and keep the link to the post if you like.
- **Speakers and supporters:** each speaker has a spotlight with their own "I'm speaking" card, and each supporter a thank-you with their card, so they can share them. Supporters get one post about them, as the supporters page promises (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--online-supporters-through-the-business.md).

The texts are on three pages: [October to December 2026](drumbeat-2026.md), [January to March 2027](drumbeat-2027-jan-mar.md) and [April to June 2027](drumbeat-2027-apr-jun.md).

## When something new happens

Some news can't be dated in advance. When it comes, it takes the next Tuesday, or goes as an extra post the same week, and the planned post moves down the list:

- **A speaker is confirmed:** a spotlight like the others, with their card (`speaker-<name>-square.png` and `-wide.png`), and a nudge for the speaker to share it.
- **A supporter joins:** a thank-you naming exactly what they fund, with their card (`supporter-<name>-square.png` and `-wide.png`).
- **An event is accepted:** a post with its card (`event-<name>-square.png` and `-wide.png`), and the host listed as a supporter if they fund and organise it and agree.
- **The program fills up:** a post about the full program, in place of the last call.
- **Jan Grodowski's hold comes off:** his spotlight, with his card.
