### Tue 23 Feb 2027, week 20: Support GoatConf online

- **State:** drafted
- **Image:** `post-support-goatconf-square.png` (LinkedIn, Mastodon), `post-support-goatconf-wide.png` (X, Bluesky)
- **LinkedIn:**

  > GoatConf is free to attend. Companies help keep it that way.
  >
  > There is one level, the same for every supporter: 500 euro, plus VAT where it applies. You get your logo and a link on the site, one post about you on GoatConf's channels, and a mention in the emails to everyone registered. It's online only: no booth and nothing at the venue, and the money pays the event's costs.
  >
  > Supporters never buy talks, stage time or the attendee list. How to support: goatconf.com/supporters

- **Short** (201 characters):

  > GoatConf is not a crammed schedule.
  >
  > Every session is one hour, and after each of the first three there is an hour off, in the cafés on Freedom Square, under five minutes' walk from IPI Academy. Coffee, lunch, and time to talk to the speaker you just heard.
