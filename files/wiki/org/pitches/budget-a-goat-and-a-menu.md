# The budget: a base under 1,000 EUR and a menu of stretch goals others fund


> **Bet on and graduated, 2026-10-07.** The organiser bet on this pitch; it continues as [budget-and-public-ledger.md](../prds/budget-and-public-ledger.md). This page is kept as the pre-bet record.

Area pitch under [One goat feeds the town](one-goat-minimal-core-and-extensions.md).
Pre-bet, shaped in auto mode on 2026-10-07.

## Problem

The organiser will put in "Up to 1,000 EUR" of their own money, "truly
minimal: the venue is the partner's, everything else volunteer or
sponsored" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`,
question 8), for a free event with no ticket income
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`). The
guide's method is a base budget, "the minimum required to run the event",
and stretch goals that unlock only when money arrives: "If we sell this
many tickets, we will have t-shirts... breakfast... we will record the
conference" (`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Base budget and stretch goals). GoatConf has the same method with a
different trigger: stretch goals unlock when a supporter funds them, not
when tickets sell.

It also expects that "you are likely to lose money on your first
conference, or barely break even" (guide, § Money). Here, the most the
organiser can lose is the 1,000 EUR, by design.

## Appetite

**Small:** one spreadsheet-sized page, kept current from November to
June, owned by the organiser.

## Solution

### The base (the goat): what it costs, and who covers it

The organiser's money is a fallback, not a budget: "Just in case there
are any expenses I need to cover. Normally, I pay for nothing also"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).
So the base is covered in kind or by supporters paying suppliers, and
the 1,000 EUR is spent only on what nobody else covers. What is known to
cost nothing or almost nothing, with sources:

| Item | Cost | Source |
|------|------|--------|
| Main hall and rooms | In kind from IPI Academy (terms unknown, needs source) | [IPI Academy](../ipi-academy.md) |
| Registration (pretix hosted) | 0 EUR: 500 free tickets a year | `sources/web/tooling/pretix-hosted-pricing.md` |
| Website hosting | 0 EUR (GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) | [Website pitch](website-and-registration.md) |
| Domain, `.com` | about 10 USD a year | `sources/web/web/cfdomainpricing-com-price.md` |
| Domain, `.ba` | about 41 EUR first year, 15 EUR to renew (2021 figures, unverified) | `sources/web/web/namepros-ba-thread-2021.md` |
| CFP tool | 0 EUR: a simple form, reviewed in a shared sheet (organiser's choice) | `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`, `sources/web/tooling/tally-pricing.md` |
| Code of conduct | 0 EUR (a CC0 policy, see [Operations](operations-people-rules-and-the-day.md)) | |

The rest of the base is **things somebody has to cover**: the first ask
is a supporter or in kind, and the amounts below are the most the
organiser's fallback would spend on each if nobody does. They are
allocations, not quotes, until a Tuzla price comes in.

| Allocation | Amount | Why |
|------------|--------|-----|
| Printing: badges, signs, the code-of-conduct poster | 250 EUR | Needed at the door; quote from a Tuzla printer. |
| Food and drink, for anyone | 0 EUR, never | "I am not paying for food or drinks... Nothing. Just leaving space either for others to cover or for folks to pay themselves" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`). Food and drink are on the supporters' menu below, or each person pays their own. |
| Small supplies (tape, pens, a spare cable) | 50 EUR | Day-of essentials that are not food or drink. |
| Tools | 50 EUR | A margin for a paid tool; no domain is needed, since the site lives under muhamed.at (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--talks-team-and-site.md`). |
| Reserve | 500 EUR | The guide's emergencies: something will go wrong (guide, § Emergencies). |
| **Total** | **850 EUR allocated, 1,000 EUR at most, 0 EUR if all is covered** | |

The reserve is half the budget on purpose. If the reserve is unspent by
the end, it seeds the next edition, the way FOSDEM's charter keeps
savings against the next event (`sources/web/funding/fosdem-charter.md`).

### The menu (the milking): stretch goals a supporter funds

Each item is a support extension in the umbrella's terms. Costs are
computed from cited Tuzla prices where they exist, at 1.95583 KM to the
euro (`sources/web/tuzla/cbbh-exchange-rate-2026-10-07.md`); they are
estimates, not quotes.

- **A round of lunch on a supporter.** About 552 EUR for the core day's
  cap of about 120 at an inexpensive meal's 9 KM
  (`sources/web/tuzla/numbeo-cost-of-living-tuzla.md`), paid by the
  supporter to the cafés on the square. Optional: lunch and coffee happen
  in town anyway, each person paying their own
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`).
- **Volunteers' lunch on a supporter.** About 92 EUR for about 20
  inexpensive meals at 9 KM (same source), paid to a café on the square.
  If nobody funds it, volunteers pay their own like everyone else; the
  organiser's fallback never covers food or drink
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
- **A round of coffee on a supporter.** About 199 EUR for about 120
  cappuccinos at 3.25 KM (same source), paid to the café.
- **Speaker grant pool.** 100 EUR per speaker the organiser mentioned, so
  about 1,000 EUR for ten
  ([CFP and speakers](cfp-and-speakers.md)).
- **Speaker dinner.** About 120 to 190 EUR for twelve at 10 to 16 EUR a
  dinner (`sources/web/tuzla/easybalkan-bosnia-costs-2026.md`).
- **The lake for everyone.** About 1,380 EUR for 300 weekend tickets at
  9 KM (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-cijene-kurir-2026-08.md`).
- **Video.** Volunteer-run with free OBS software
  (`sources/web/tooling/obs-project.md`) costs the equipment, which is
  (unknown, needs source); a hired crew runs about 1,500 to 4,000 USD a
  day at US rates (`sources/web/tooling/videographer-rates-2026-ruah.md`).
  The guide treats video as a stretch goal (§ Video recording).
- **The after-party, shirts, lanyards:** priced when a supporter asks.

For scale, Balkan Ruby's entry tier was 500 EUR and its Food and Coffee
items 1,500 EUR each (`sources/web/funding/balkanruby-sponsorship.md`);
EuRuKo 2024 took "Anonymous support" from 500 EUR
(`sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-sponsoring.md`). GoatConf's menu prices
sit in the same range and can go lower for individuals and local
companies.

### No money through the organiser

The organiser decided: "Sponsors can provide no money directly to me. No
money goes though me" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`).
So GoatConf never receives, holds or invoices money:

- **A supporter pays the supplier.** A company that funds coffee pays the
  café; one that funds printing pays the printer; one that funds video
  pays the crew. The supplier invoices the supporter, not GoatConf.
- **Or gives in kind:** the coffee itself, the equipment, a room, people's
  time.
- **The organiser's own contribution** of up to 1,000 EUR is the
  organiser paying suppliers directly for the base, with the same
  record kept.

This also settles what the guide raises about legal structure ("a clean
entity for sponsors to pay", § Legal structure and insurance): with no
money to receive, GoatConf 2027 needs no company, association or bank
account.

### Every euro in the open

The organiser also decided: "We also need to be transparent about the
budget" (same source). The budget is public, from the first payment:

- **One ledger, a budget page at muhamed.at/goatconf** (organiser's
  choice, `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`),
  written here and copied over like the rest of the site, and kept in this
  brain too. Each line: what it
  is, who paid (the organiser, a named supporter, or in kind from whom),
  the amount or an in-kind estimate, the date, and whether the supplier's
  receipt is in hand.
- **Updated as it happens,** not at the end, so a supporter can see their
  line before the event and an attendee can see what the event cost.
- **A final account** within two weeks of the event, as the guide
  advises settling finances (§ Settle the finances), published in full.
- **Anonymous support is not hidden spending:** a supporter who wants no
  name shown appears as "an anonymous supporter" with the item and amount
  still listed.

Transparency also makes the menu honest: a stretch goal shows as funded
only when its line is in the ledger.

## Rabbit holes

- **Allocations read as prices.** The table must be updated with real
  quotes; a 250 EUR printing line could be far off.
- **A stretch goal promised before it is funded.** The guide's rule:
  announce it when the money is there.
- **Suppliers who cannot invoice a foreign company,** or supporters who
  cannot pay a small local supplier. Whether a Tuzla café or printer can
  invoice an Austrian or German company is (unknown, needs source); in
  kind is the fallback.
- **A supporter offering cash** "to make it simple". The answer is always
  the supplier's invoice or in kind.

## No-gos

- No spending beyond 1,000 EUR of the organiser's money, and none at all
  while a supporter or in-kind route is still open.
- No stretch goal announced before it is paid for.
- No food or drink paid from the organiser's money, for anyone.
- No money from anyone to the organiser or to GoatConf, ever.
- No spending, of anyone's money, that is not on the public ledger.

## Choices made in auto mode

- **A 500 EUR reserve.** Because the base has no ticket income to absorb
  surprises, and a free event cannot raise prices.
- **Lunch and coffee as stretch goals, not base.** Because the guide
  calls catering an expensive mistake and Tuzla is cheap to eat in.
- **The ledger is public from the first payment, not only at the end.**
  Because the organiser asked for transparency, and an early ledger also
  shows supporters their line before they are thanked on stage.

## Open questions for the organiser

- What does IPI provide in kind: rooms only, or also AV, Wi-Fi, cleaning,
  staff time?
- Is the 1,000 EUR firm, or is a second personal contribution possible
  if a stretch goal falls short?
