# The organiser: Muhamed Isabegovic

GoatConf's organiser, by their own statement
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--organiser.md`). Public site:
[muhamed.at](https://muhamed.at/). This page records only what the site
says and what bears on the conference.

## What bears on GoatConf

- **Has organised conferences before:** "Founder and organiser @ EuRuKo
  2024 and RubyConf Austria" (`sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-about.md`).
  "Community building and conference organising" is listed among their
  specialties (same source). That is a network of Ruby speakers,
  sponsors and attendees, and a track record a CFP and a sponsor deck
  can point to.
- **An alumnus of the host:** "BSc in Information Technology, IPI
  Akademija Tuzla" (same source). The partnership with IPI Academy is
  with the organiser's own school.
- **Runs a travel guide to the country:** Usput.ba is described as "my
  own travel guide to Bosnia and Herzegovina", built from real place data
  (same source). It is a ready source of places, itineraries and audio
  tours for attendees who stay longer.
- **Based in Vienna**, working remotely across Europe; Bosnian native,
  English at full professional level
  (`sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-home.md`, `muhamed-at-about.md`).
  The conference is organised at a distance from Tuzla, which makes local
  help (the host, volunteers) load-bearing.
- **Works in AI-native engineering, Ruby and Rails**, and builds
  open-source tools (pi-brain, enola, tabula) (same sources). These are
  communities GoatConf can reach first, while its audience stays open and
  multi-discipline (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`).
