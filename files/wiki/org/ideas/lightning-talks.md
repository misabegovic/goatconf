# An hour of lightning talks

The organiser asked on 2026-10-18 whether GoatConf should give one hour
to lightning talks, and chose "Not now": the idea is kept here, and
nothing on the site says it (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--lightning-talks-not-now.md).

## What

One one-hour slot on a core day, in one room, of short talks back to
back. If it happens, the organiser leans towards talks of 5 minutes,
about 8 in the hour, proposed ahead through the same talk form as other
sessions and picked by the organiser, with the slides collected on one
laptop beforehand. The day, time and room are to be decided later
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--lightning-talks-not-now.md).

## Why interesting

- It is the lowest bar for a first-time speaker, the same people the
  30-minute option is meant for (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--thirty-minute-sessions.md).
- The format is familiar from conferences the organiser knows: EuRuKo
  lists lightning talks next to keynotes, talks and workshops (source:
  sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-home.md), and Jan Grodowski, one of the
  confirmed speakers, has given one at wroclove.rb (source:
  sources/web/speakers/grodowski-com-2026-10-14.md).
- The core days have many open one-hour slots, so one hour costs little
  (unverified, 2026-10-18: inferred from the program page, not from a
  source).

## Maturity

An idea only, not a decision. To go ahead it needs the organiser's yes,
a day, time and room, and the talk page and form to say that a lightning
talk can be proposed. Until then the site does not mention it.
