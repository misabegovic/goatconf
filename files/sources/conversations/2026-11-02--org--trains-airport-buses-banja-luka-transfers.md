The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Let's add the train options to the travel page. Also any information we have about busses from airports... Also maybe mention Banja Luka airport as an option... Also transport from airport options, there are companies that do that.

The agent read the railways' and timetable sites, Tuzla and Banja Luka airports' own pages, the bus operator GIPS's timetable for line 34, travel sites and transfer companies (sources/web/travel/trains-to-tuzla-2026-11-02.md, tzl-bus-line-34-2026-11-02.md, tzl-airport-to-town-2026-11-02.md, banja-luka-airport-2026-11-02.md, airport-transfers-2026-11-02.md). It found that no passenger train runs to Tuzla, and that a city bus (line 34) has served Tuzla airport since 18 June 2026, where the travel page still said a public bus was unknown. Asked four questions, the organiser chose:

- **Trains:** "Honest note + combos (Recommended)": say plainly there is no train to Tuzla, then list the train and bus combinations (Banja Luka to Doboj by train, then bus; Zagreb or Osijek by train to Slavonski Brod or Vinkovci, then bus), times marked as estimates.
- **Transfer companies:** "Research and list several (Recommended)": find the companies serving Tuzla, Sarajevo and Banja Luka airports to Tuzla and list them, as the car-rental block does.
- **Banja Luka:** "Own bullet in Getting here (Recommended)": who flies there (checked on 2026-11-02), the shuttle into town, and the bus or car on to Tuzla.
- **Delivery:** "Push and open a PR".

Done on 2026-11-02: the travel page's "Getting here" gains a Banja Luka bullet, the Sarajevo airport bus into town, and a "By train" bullet; a new "From the airport to town" block covers bus 34, the shuttle vans, taxis and the transfer companies (Easy Balkan Transfers from Tuzla airport; Balkan Transfer, Daytrip and Easy Balkan Transfers from Sarajevo airport; none found for Banja Luka airport, with a taxi estimate instead); "Renting a car" gains Banja Luka airport (Avis and Carwiz); "Good to know" names Banja Luka as a third airport. The old line "We don't yet know whether a public bus runs from the airport" is gone.

Notes from the agent's reading on 2026-11-02:

- The GIPS timetable page carries injected spam links, so the travel page links the airport's own notice for bus 34 instead.
- JAM Transfer's Tuzla page (it says the airport is 1 km from town) and tuzlatransfer.ba (no readable content) were left out; Funky Tours and Balkan Transfer's Tuzla airport route go to Sarajevo, not into Tuzla.
