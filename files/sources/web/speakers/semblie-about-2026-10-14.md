Semblie is an electronic hardware development company based in Bosnia and Herzegovina. We are a team of passionate hardware and software developers always looking to outperform ourselves.

Our Story
2012-2014, Team: It was the thirst for knowledge and passion for creating something out of nothing that connected two, then students of electrical engineering in what started just as a hobby, but became what we have today.
2015-2017, Founding: To ensure better security and status, we decided to register the company in Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina. That's when Semblie d.o.o. Tuzla was founded.
2018-2020, Expansion: After years of working in a garage and/or smaller quarters, due to the expansion of work and the need for more space, and general company image we moved into the new office space.
2021-2023, Manufacturing: In response to the growing demand for small-batch manufacturing, we've expanded our facilities to include a PCB assembly line and our 3D printing capabilities with resin printing technology.
