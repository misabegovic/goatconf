On 2026-11-22, while the proposal that closes the Tuzla photos and the
dishes on a phone was being built
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--food-and-tuzla-open-and-close-on-phones.md)
and before seeing it, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> We should probably have every section as collapsable? Also probably should store latest preference in localstore so folks keep last state?

Told in summary that every section below the hero could then be closed by
the visitor, the organiser answered, verbatim:

> Only leave the free ticket section as one that can't be collapsed and the hero section

Read as:

- On the home page two things have no control to close them: the hero
  (the cover with "What is decided") and "Get your free ticket". Every
  other section can be closed and opened by the visitor, on a phone and
  on a wide screen: the days, the speakers, Tuzla, the food, propose a
  session, make it yours, support GoatConf, why a goat, how we treat each
  other, Goatchi and "See you in Tuzla".
- The last state of each section and each list is kept in the browser
  (localStorage), which replaces the persona study's "no memory between
  visits". It is never a trap: a link or a text fragment into something
  closed opens it for that visit; storage that is missing or cleared gives
  the first-visit page; without the script everything is open, and the
  lists still open and close on their own. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- That a section can be closed is not that it starts closed. What a first
  visit shows was, at this point, still what the persona study decided.
  The organiser decided it differently later the same day:
  sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--sections-start-closed-speakers-open.md.
