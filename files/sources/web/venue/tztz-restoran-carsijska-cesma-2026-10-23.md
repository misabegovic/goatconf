At “Čaršijska česma,” guests can enjoy a variety of traditional Bosnian dishes, as well as coffee or boza, and sweeten their experience with desserts from Bosnian cuisine.
“Česma,” as locals from Tuzla call it, is fully designed in the Bosnian traditional style. The exterior resembles an old Bosnian house, while the interior is filled with details and objects once commonly used in everyday life, offering visitors a brief journey into the past.
This restaurant is often the first stop for tourists visiting the city. Its unique Bosnian ambiance, traditional food, coffee, and sevdalinka music make it a true attraction for foreign visitors.
It is also interesting that many guests, inspired by the atmosphere, have donated various antique items that further enrich the interior, contributing personally to the restaurant’s distinctive character.
Another notable feature of “Česma” is that alcoholic beverages are not served on the premises.
