Host an event during Devconnect!
💡 TL;DR: Just do it! No permission needed. 
Embracing the spirit of decentralization, each event is independently hosted, meaning there is no main Devconnect event. We (the Devconnect team) are hosting a coworking space throughout the week, but beyond that, all events are independent. This means you, the event organizers, make Devconnect what it’s supposed to be 🙂
## How to host an event during Devconnect 🗓️
1. Set the goals for your event, determine your target audience, capacity and format
1. Choose your date using the Devconnect calendar to minimize overlaps with similar topics
1. Find a suitable venue in Istanbul
1. Announce it on Twitter (tagging @EFDevconnect) and in the official Telegram group!
1. Submit your event via this form once it is confirmed
📝 If the event aligns with the Devconnect philosophy (see below), we will list your event on the official Devconnect calendar.

## Devconnect Philosophy 🌟
What makes Devconnect successful is the participation of various teams, who self-organize events, guided by the expertise of domain experts and the interests of attendees. You, as domain experts, know best what the Ethereum ecosystem needs to achieve and which problems must be addressed in your field.
Our goal in initiating Devconnect is to create a week dedicated to progress. This progress can manifest as concrete technical advancements, innovative ideas, or theoretical breakthroughs. It can also involve teaching people about already solved issues or introducing more newcomers to Ethereum research and industry.
## How to align with the Devconnect philosophy ✨
- Organize Ethereum and education-focused events 
- Look for other companies/teams in your domain and collaborate on organizing a relevant event around an Ethereum topic. You can coordinate in the Devconnect Telegram group. 
- Aim for full-day events (not just evening events)
- Choose a date during Devconnect (Nov. 13-19, 2023)
- Target a specific audience to keep discussions relevant and enable in-depth understanding
- Organize events that tackle newcomer problems
## Support by the Devconnect team 🤲
We will be supporting events taking place during Devconnect in three ways:
  1. Signalling towards a place (Istanbul) and a time (November 13-19) for in-person collaboration among our global distributed Ethereum community
  1. Organizing a Coworking Space throughout the week open to all, and
  1. Helping with publicity through Devconnect.org calendar, twitter, etc. for events aligned with the Devconnect philosophy:

## Finding sponsors 💳
- You can obviously look for sponsors for your event! To align with the Devconnect philosophy, try to find Ethereum-aligned sponsors, that means sponsors that you connect with, not just random for-profit entities. 
Don’t hesitate to ask in the Devconnect IST TG group if anyone is interested in sponsoring, or offer if you want to sponsor. 
- There is no guaranteed funding from the Ethereum Foundation, however, if you’re organizing an event that is developer or education focused, you can apply via the Ecosystem Support Program grants.
