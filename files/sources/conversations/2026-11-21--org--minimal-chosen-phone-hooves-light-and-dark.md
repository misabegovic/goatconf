title: "Minimal is chosen, with better hoof marks on a phone and light and dark themes"
date: 2026-11-21
kind: conversation
---

On 2026-11-21, shown the calm and the minimal variants side by side after
their fifth round
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--hooves-stay-and-more-improvements.md),
the organiser chose, verbatim:

> Let's do minimal and improve hooves on a phone. Also let's make sure to properly support light and dark themes

Read as three things. Minimal is the way forward: the work goes on only on
the branch `site/less-blue-minimal`, and it is the chosen look, no longer a
variant; the calm and the phone-first branches stay as they are. The hoof
marks are made properly present on a phone, by design and not by turning
their strength up. And the site supports a light and a dark theme: it
follows the visitor's system setting, a visitor can also choose, and the
dark theme is designed as the album at night, not inverted.

Earlier the organiser had said that the share cards and the brand files
are part of the change "once we have a way forward"
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--continue-iterating-round-four.md).
There is now a way forward, so they are in scope: a card is an image and
has one look, the light one.

Nothing is pushed, merged or published before the organiser approves it
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).
Cards already posted elsewhere are not changed by this work.
