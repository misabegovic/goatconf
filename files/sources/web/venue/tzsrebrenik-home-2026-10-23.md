The Srebrenik tourist board's site (Bosnian). Translation (not verbatim): the old town of Srebrenik, a 14th-century fortress on a steep rock above the Tinja valley on the slopes of Majevica, about 500 m above sea level, first mentioned in 1333; the board suggests a quick day trip (the old town, the panorama, the town park and a local lunch). The fortress itself was not found in OSM by name on 2026-10-23; Nominatim's Gornji Srebrenik village point is 44.7023484, 18.5398252 (node 1715277345), about 20.9 km from IPI Academy straight line, a village point, not the fortress.

Stari grad Srebrenik smješten je na strmoj i krševitoj stijeni na obroncima Majevice. Njegov položaj i fortifikacijska struktura učinili su ga važnim geostrateškim središtem šireg područja Usore.
