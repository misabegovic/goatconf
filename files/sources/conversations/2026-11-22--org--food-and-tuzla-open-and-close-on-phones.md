On 2026-11-22, with the phone home page still about 29 screens long, the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I'm also thinking to have the food section collapsable and extendable by default? So folks that want to learn can open/close sections like food and Tuzla the host town? Idk if that makes sense or if it stupid. Could work for all sections probably below the hero section

The answer recommended keeping the reasons to come open and closing only
the "learn more" sections. The organiser answered, verbatim:

> Figure it out with personas

A study was then made with the five users PRODUCT.md names and two more
drawn from recorded sources (a local attendee, and a phone visitor who
arrives from a shared speaker card). It is kept outside the repository.
Its verdict: on a wide screen nothing closes; on a phone the six photos
of "Tuzla, the host town" and the twelve dishes of "Eat and drink" are
closed until a visitor opens them, with the text, the prices, the travel
link and the cook goat left open; every other section stays open, among
them the speakers, as the organiser decided on 7 November, the ticket,
the supporters' logos and the code of conduct.

Read as: the organiser asks, in their own words, for two lists to open
and close. That reopens, for those two lists on a phone only, the
decision of 7 November that every card, dish and goat stop shows on a
phone (sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-goats-stay.md). It
does not reopen it for anything else, and it does not say the goats may
go behind the control: so as many goat stops as can honestly be kept are
kept while a list is closed, and the count is reported. This is built as
a proposal on a branch and shown by images; the organiser has not
approved publishing it
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).
