After the home page's "Give a talk" sentence was changed to "the organiser may refund some or all of it" (sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--refund-some-or-all.md), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> But also maybe not have that on the homepage? Just on the page where folks apply

Done on 2026-10-14: the home page's "Give a talk" drops "Speakers pay their own travel. If the budget allows, the organiser may refund some or all of it, but this is not promised." and keeps "Sessions are picked for their quality, never for who is paying." On the site, speakers' travel terms are now only on the talk page, where people apply ("What you get, and what you pay"). The supporters page keeps its note to companies that one covering its own speaker's travel is listed as a supporter. The facts file for agents, `llms.txt`, keeps its one line on travel, since it is read by tools answering questions, not shown to visitors.
