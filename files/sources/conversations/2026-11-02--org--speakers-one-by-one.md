On Monday 2 November 2026 the organiser asked, verbatim:

> What is the next post for tomorrow?

The agent answered from the drumbeat pages: Tuesday 3 November 2026,
"7,000 years of salt", drafted, with its news-first note. It also noted
that "Registration is open, and free", planned for after Goatchi's post
(published 2026-10-31), was still marked "drafted, next" with no record of
it going out, and that Saturday 7 November carried "The first speakers are
here", one post naming all eight speakers. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> No, we can make that registration post a bit later. For speakers, let's announce one by one? To spread it out?

The agent asked three questions and the organiser chose:

- **Pace:** "Tuesdays, weekly". One speaker each Tuesday from Tuesday 10
  November to Tuesday 29 December 2026, in the Tuesday news slot. The
  Tuzla posts that held those Tuesdays (Meet at Meša and Ismet, 58 places
  to run yours, Salt Square) move to the Saturdays the speakers free, and
  the round-up post "The first speakers are here" is dropped.
- **Order:** "Site order". The organiser's standing order from the speaker
  records: Paul Campbell, Roy Tomeij, Carmine Paolino, Dejan Menges, Amar
  Mahmutbegović, Cristian Planas, Christoph Lipautz, Predrag Radenkovic.
  Jan Grodowski stays out while his hold is on.
- **Registration:** "After all speakers". "Registration is open, and free"
  goes once the speaker run is done.

Tuesday 3 November 2026 keeps "7,000 years of salt".
