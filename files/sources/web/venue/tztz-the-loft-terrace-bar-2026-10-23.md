The Loft Terrace Bar is a unique event venue characterised by elegant design and an urban aesthetic.
It features a multifunctional terrace with both a closed conference area and an open event space, making it suitable for weddings, birthday celebrations, and other private parties, as well as corporate events such as promotions, conferences, seminars, and trainings. The venue is equipped with modern technical facilities and offers food and beverage service provided by professional and friendly staff. The terrace has a capacity of 100 seats, while the indoor area accommodates up to 70 guests.
The Loft Terrace Bar is located in Tuzla, on the ninth floor of Hotel Tehnograd in Slavinovići, at Žarka Vukovića Pucara bb.
