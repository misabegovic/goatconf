Caffe Bar Central
Trg Slobode bb Zgrada Barok, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
Is it Bosnian law that every city must have a ‘Central Caffe’? Maybe so, maybe not, but who cares when you can get Tuzlansko on tap and a decent shot of espresso in the morning. The location is as central as the name suggests, with a darling terrace onto the main square that is perfect for people-watching in the warmer months.
