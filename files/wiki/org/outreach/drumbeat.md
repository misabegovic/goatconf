# The drumbeat: the weekly posts, drafted, from launch to June 2027

The calendar of beats that [the social drumbeat decision](../adrs/social-drumbeat.md) asks for: one public reason to care about GoatConf a week, drafted here and published only by the organiser, or a volunteer the organiser names, because anything that reaches the public is the organiser's call (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--goatconf-will-be-public.md). Every draft below is a draft. Nothing here has been posted.

## Channels and images

The organiser chose the channels on 2026-10-12: LinkedIn; X, Bluesky and Mastodon; and Luma with link previews (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--brand-seal-and-euruko-goat.md). Registration runs on the Luma event, so registered guests can get Luma updates (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--registration-on-luma.md).

Each post has a LinkedIn text and a short text of at most 280 characters for X, Bluesky and Mastodon. Each names its image in `site/share/`, made by `tools/shareables.py`; square images suit LinkedIn and Mastodon, wide ones suit X and Bluesky. Links point at goatconf.com, so each post shows the link preview.

Weeks count from the week goatconf.com goes live (week 1). The organiser fills in the real date when posting and marks the state: drafted, approved or published.

## The launch sequence

The story in week 5 follows the EuRuKo 2024 account of the goat (source: sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-why-the-goat.md).

### Week 1: GoatConf exists. Save the date

- **State:** drafted
- **Image:** `post-save-the-date-square.png` (LinkedIn, Mastodon), `post-save-the-date-wide.png` (X, Bluesky)
- **LinkedIn:**

  > GoatConf is coming to Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina, on 3 to 5 June 2027. It is free.
  >
  > Thursday and Friday are two days of one-hour talks, panels and workshops in all seven rooms of IPI Academy, with a full hour off after each session to talk with the people you just heard. Saturday belongs to the community: the salt lakes, a goat hunt through town, walks and meetups.

  > The goat is from a Tuzla story. When the town was ordered to get rid of its goats, one man hid his, and that one goat ended up feeding everyone. GoatConf works the same way: a small core, and everything else added by the people who come.

  > Save the date: goatconf.com

### Week 5: Why a goat

- **State:** drafted
- **Image:** `header-3x1.png` or the seal, `brand/seal-1024.png`
- **LinkedIn:**

  > Why is it called GoatConf?
  >
  > When Austria-Hungary ruled Tuzla, it ordered every goat gone, to protect the forests. One man hid his. Word got around, and soon the whole town was buying milk and cheese from that one goat.
  >
  > Our seal says it: the one that feeds the community. The goat on it was the mascot of EuRuKo 2024, the first Ruby conference in Bosnia and Herzegovina.
  >
  > goatconf.com

## Later beats, to draft when their time comes

Each later beat gets the same treatment: a LinkedIn text, a short text and an image.

- **Week 6, Tuzla on a budget.** What a week costs: a simple meal about 4.60 euro, a cappuccino about 1.66 euro, a lake ticket 3.60 to 4.60 euro (prices checked 2026-10-07, see [Tuzla](../tuzla.md)); link to the travel page.
- **Week 7, every euro in the open.** No money passes through the organiser; the public ledger (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md).
- **Week 8, fund a speaker's travel.** Supporters pay a named thing straight to the supplier; their logo goes on the supporters page and on the speaker's profile (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--speakers-and-supporters-approved.md).
- **When a speaker is accepted,** one post each, with their "I'm speaking" card (`speaker-<name>-square.png` and `-wide.png`), and a nudge for the speaker to share it.
- **When a supporter joins,** a thank-you naming exactly what they fund, with their card (`supporter-<name>-square.png` and `-wide.png`).
- **The program fills up.** The first full day; Friday starting at 09:30 (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--friday-dzuma-schedule.md).
- **Saturday, the community day.** The lakes, the goat hunt, walks, as hosts add them.
- **Travel.** How to get to Tuzla, where to stay, where to eat without meat.
- **The last call for talks**, when the slots are nearly full.
- **The week before; live from the day; thank you and photos afterwards.**
