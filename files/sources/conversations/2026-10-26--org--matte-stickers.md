---
title: "The stickers go matte: no shine, and short light shadows"
date: 2026-10-26
kind: conversation
---

The organiser asked for a review of the site, "not too glossy design for example" (sources/conversations/2026-10-26--org--food-tags.md). The review named the gloss: a 115-degree white sheen over every sticker (40% at the top-left corner, 18% at the far corner), which washed out photos and made the blue fact stickers look like gradient cards, and long, dark soft shadows on stickers, pages and buttons.

Shown a first version in the browser (the sheen at about a third, shadows lighter), the organiser chose "Go further". Shown the second version, with no sheen at all and shorter, lighter shadows on stickers, number pills, album pages, the spread, buttons and the seal, next to the live site, the organiser chose "Ship it", with DESIGN.md updated to match: the Gloss Rule becomes the Matte Rule.
