Tuzla taxi (15-17)
The “Tuzla Taxi” Association represents the city’s taxi operators and is a respected professional organization of drivers providing taxi services within Tuzla and the surrounding region. The association promotes, coordinates, and represents the common interests of its members, currently comprising 250 active taxi drivers.
They are known for speed, comfort, and safety, with vehicles reaching your location within five minutes. Proud of their flexibility and efficiency, they are available 24/7, 365 days a year, ranking them among the top taxi services in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Their services can be accessed at all taxi stands across Tuzla or by a simple phone call to 15-17, where courteous drivers will transport you safely, comfortably, and at affordable rates to your destination—whether within Tuzla, elsewhere in Bosnia and Herzegovina, or across Europe.
Gradski taxi (15-25)
Gradski Taxi 15-25 operates successfully within Tuzla and beyond. By calling 15-25, you can order a taxi within minutes. Gradski Taxi operates in 10 countries and nearly 150 cities worldwide .
Radio taxi non-stop (15-01)
Radio tax non-stop is not just an ordinary taxi service—it is an association of experienced taxi drivers who have been in the business for many years. They are committed to providing high-quality passenger transport. All their vehicles are regularly maintained, air-conditioned, and comfortable, allowing them to serve destinations both within the country and abroad. They are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Call at any time, and Radio Taxi Non-Stop will promptly reach your location and transport you safely to your desired destination.
