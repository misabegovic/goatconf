On 2026-11-22, after seeing the published fix for the sections closed and
opened
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--meals-section-weird-when-collapsed.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok. Let's have speakers section also collapsed by default?

Read as: on a first visit "Who is speaking" starts closed like every
other section that opens and closes, at every width. Only the hero, "Get
your free ticket" and "See you in Tuzla" are open, and those have no
control.

This reverses two of the organiser's own answers of the same day, which
stay on record as they were given: "Speakers section stays open?"
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--sections-start-closed-speakers-open.md)
and the choice "Keep as it is" for the first visit on a phone
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--first-visit-stays-see-you-fixed-open-ship-when-ready.md).

A visitor whose browser already remembers a choice for the speakers keeps
it. One whose browser remembers nothing for the speakers, which is anyone
who never pressed that heading or "Open all sections", gets the section
closed. The closed row carries the lineup on its own: the eight faces and
"View all speakers", on a phone too.
