# A tamagotchi of each speaker's goat, for people to keep alive

The organiser left this idea for later on 2026-10-27, while the painted
goats went onto the site: "tamagochi game as part of the conference, for
each speaker. folks can play and keep alive their favorite speaker"
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--illustrated-goat-on-the-site.md).
Nothing is decided and nothing on the site says it.

## What

A small game in which a visitor adopts the goat of a speaker they like and
keeps it alive by tending to it, the way a tamagotchi is fed and played
with. Every speaker already has their own painted goat, with a blink and a
head that turns, which such a game could start from
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--illustrated-goat-on-the-site.md).

## Why interesting

- It gives people a reason to come back to the site before June and to
  care about a speaker, which the organiser framed as keeping "their
  favorite speaker" alive (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--illustrated-goat-on-the-site.md).
- The pieces exist: each speaker has a painted goat that blinks and turns
  its head (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--illustrated-goat-on-the-site.md).

## Maturity

An idea only, parked by the organiser "for after". To go ahead it needs a
shaped pitch and the organiser's yes. Until then the site does not mention
it.

## Open questions

- What keeps a goat alive: visits to the site, reading the speaker's
  session, coming to the talk, or something done in Tuzla.
- Whether it lives on the site, in the visitor's own browser only, or in
  something shared, and what that means for privacy.
- Whether speakers agree to it before their goat is used this way.

## Shaped

On 2026-10-28 the idea was shaped into a small pitch: a free game on the
site, in the visitor's browser, not a paid phone app
([the pitch](../pitches/goat-tamagotchi-on-the-site.md); source: sources/conversations/2026-10-28--org--goat-tamagotchi-pitch.md).
