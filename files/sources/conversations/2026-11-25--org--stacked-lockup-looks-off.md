date: 2026-11-25
kind: conversation
---

On Wednesday 25 November 2026 the organiser sent a screenshot of the brand
page on a phone, showing the card "Stacked, with the line": the seal, GOAT
over CONF beside it, and "The one that feeds the community." under the two
words. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> This looks off

The agent drew the file at four times its size and asked which of four
things was meant: the goat's drawing (a hairline along the lower left of
the inner ring, a spur on the horn's tip, nicks on the collar), the
lettering (a notch through the crossbar of the "f" in "feeds"), the
layout (the line and the name each said twice, the text sitting low, the
right third of the card empty), or the goat itself reading like a big cat
at that size. The organiser answered in their own words, verbatim:

> Stacked words, then a sentence below

Asked how far to take the fix (the defects now and the layout as options
to choose from, the defects only, or all of it on the agent's judgement),
the organiser chose, verbatim:

> Fix all of it, your call

Read as: what is off is the arrangement of the lettering, the word broken
into two stacked words with a sentence under them, and the agent decides
the new arrangement and mends the defects it found, then shows the result
on the working branch.

What the agent did on the branch, for the organiser to see before
anything is published:

- The lockup keeps the seal and sets GOATCONF beside it on one line, with
  THE ONE THAT FEEDS THE COMMUNITY under it in the same capitals (weight
  800, 75% width), letter-spaced to the wordmark's exact width and centred
  with it on the seal's height. The sentence in mixed case, at weight 700
  and 85% width, is gone: DESIGN.md's own rule is that every caption is
  lettered at 800, condensed and in capitals. A vertical arrangement (the
  seal over the word over the line) was drawn too and set aside, since the
  seal alone already serves a square space.
- The hairline between the goat and its ring is closed in the seal and in
  the small form: the goat is now cut at the middle of the ring, so the
  ring covers the cut edge.
- The spur on the horn's tip and the nicks on the collar are gone: the
  curve handles of the traced outline no longer overshoot a short segment
  beside a long one, and points of the trace one pixel apart are merged.
  Laid over the old drawing, nothing else in the goat moves.
- The notch in the "f" was not a defect. The agent had called it one; set
  from the font itself the letter shows the same notch, so it is how
  Bricolage Grotesque draws it. The new line has no such letter.

The card on the brand page is now captioned "With the seal and the line".
The files keep their names. The brand kit's SVG, PNG and PDF files, the
favicon and the home-screen icon are built and rendered again. The share
cards, the stickers and the guides that show the seal or the small form
were rendered from the old drawing and are not rendered again here; they
take the mended goat the next time each is rendered.

The organiser has not yet seen the result.
