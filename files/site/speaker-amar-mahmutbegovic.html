Amar Mahmutbegović

Co-founder and Head of Engineering at SemblieSpeaking at GoatConf 2027 in Tuzla.

Who they are

Amar Mahmutbegovic is the co-founder and Head of Engineering at Semblie, where he leads the utilization of modern C++ for firmware development. He is passionate about demonstrating the advantages of C++ to the embedded community through his blog posts and LinkedIn activity. He is the author of C++ in Embedded Systems (Packt, 2025).

  • linkedin.com/in/amar-mahmutbegovic
  • semblie.com/modern-cpp
  • embeddedrelated.com/blogs-1/nf/Amar_Mahmutbegovic.php

How to support them

  • Read his book, C++ in Embedded Systems (Packt, 2025)
  • Build your hardware product with Semblie, his company
  • Have your boards assembled at Semblie in Tuzla, in small batches
