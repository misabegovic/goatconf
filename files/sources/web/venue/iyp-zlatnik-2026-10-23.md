Zlatnik
Setalište Slana banja bb, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
https://www.restoranzlatnik.com
If you’re after somewhere to eat within spitting distance of the Pannonian Lakes, you can do worse than giving Zlatnik a try. There is something inherently ‘Bosnian’ about it all, be that the extensive menu of great food or the intangible atmosphere that reminds you exactly where you are. There is a lot to love about Zlatnik before the plate makes it to your table, and what comes out of the kitchen is always a delight.
