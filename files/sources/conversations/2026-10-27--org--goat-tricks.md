date: 2026-10-27

---

The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I'm thinking that we should make it that when someone taps or clicks on a goat it produces an animation

Asked what the goat should do, they chose "A trick each tap (Recommended)": each tap plays the next of a few tricks, a jump for joy, a spin, a head shake and a small "Meee!" bubble. Told that on a computer the goat ducks when the pointer comes close, so nobody could click it, they chose "Look at the pointer (Recommended)": it stops ducking at the pointer and turns its head towards it, and still plays peek-a-boo on its own.

While it was being built, they added, verbatim:

> Also speed of how often the tricks are done on its own... Just slightly faster, just a bit

The pause between its own moves went from 4 to 8 seconds to 3 to 6 seconds. Shown it in the browser, with a tap on a speaker's card playing a trick instead of opening the speaker's page, they chose "Ship it (Recommended)".

Before shipping, the agent changed two tricks and asked again. The spin had shown the goat mirrored, against the rule that the goat is never mirrored or spun to turn, so a whole goat (on a photo or at "See you in Tuzla") does a somersault in the air instead. A goat peeking over a slot is painted only down to the slot's edge, so it bounces and ducks and pops up instead of jumping or flipping, and the end of the painting never shows. Shown both in the browser, the organiser chose "Ship it (Recommended)".
