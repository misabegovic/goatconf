After the contact page began inviting volunteers to write in through the
form (`sources/conversations/2026-10-20--org--contact-page-students-and-volunteers.md`),
the form's "What is it about?" question still offered only A question, A
code of conduct report, Supporting GoatConf, Press and My data. Asked
whether to add "Volunteering" to it, the organiser answered, verbatim:

> Yes

On 2026-10-20 the agent added "Volunteering" to the question in the
form's editor, after "My data" and before "Other"; the published form
showed it the same day. The form's description, "Questions, supporting
GoatConf, press, or a code of conduct report.", was left unchanged.
