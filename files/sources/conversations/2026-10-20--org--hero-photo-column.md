title: "The home page hero becomes a photo column"
date: 2026-10-20
kind: conversation
---

The organiser asked to explore a redesign of the home page hero "without
losing any context", using impeccable, with options and nothing committed
without approval. Over several rounds of live variants they chose the
photo column, with these steers, verbatim:

> I like #1 . lets optimize for mobile screens too.

> feels weird to read 2-4-3-1, make it in order

> do we need to list 3-5 June, Tuzla on the left section? why not keep text like in prod now?

> in the what is decided section, for cards 1 and 3, can we center the text and make it a bit bigger? the yellow section only, the white one can stay the same size.

> I meant 1 and 3 can stay left aligned

> lets also make tech stack agnostic pop

> lets go with #1

The hero keeps every fact, link and sentence. The cover keeps the live
text and puts the seal beside the title. "What is decided" puts the
Tuzla and IPI Academy photos two cells wide and two tall, with Free,
3–5 June and Tech stack agnostic in larger yellow lettering beside them,
read 1 to 6. The spread holds 53rem on wide screens.

Explored and not chosen: the date and place lettered at poster scale, a
Tuzla photo printed in album blue as the cover, and photo stickers that
page through more photos with arrows and dots. The only other photo of
IPI Academy on hand, the building front, is 400 by 300 pixels, too small
for a big sticker.
