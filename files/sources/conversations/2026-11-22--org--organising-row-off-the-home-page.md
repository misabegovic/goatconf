On 2026-11-22 the twelfth round was published: "Who's organising" became a
page of its own, and the home page kept one row in its place with a link
to it (sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--organisers-own-page-and-see-you-in-tuzla.md).
Seeing it live, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Who's organising section can be removed from yhe the homepage as such? Maybe just have it in the footer or something? Or as part of supporters text as a link to the page?

Read as: the row is removed from the home page altogether, with no
bordered section and no gap left behind. The footer already links "Who's
organising" on every page, which is the "in the footer" option. The home
page's supporters section gets the link too, beside its existing links,
labelled "Who's organising" from the page's heading, with no new sentence
and the supporters text as it is. The host goat's stop that stood on the
row stays on the home page, as decided on 7 November
(sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-goats-stay.md), moved beside
the new link; and the old address `#organiser` stays on the new link, so
a link shared before lands next to it. Which of the organiser's options
to build, and where exactly the link and the goat go, was decided by the
session that talks to the organiser and by the designer; it is shown to
the organiser before it is published.
