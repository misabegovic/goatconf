After Goatchi shipped, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> If I share the link to goatchi, what's the preview? Also, can we have some sort of percentage on the yellow bars? Or a way to get a feeling for how long it will hold?

The agent answered that a shared link showed the site's general card, the seal with the dates, and made Goatchi a card of its own: its name, "Keep a GoatConf goat. Feed it, play with it, let it sleep.", the goat eating burek and the plain goat saying "Baaa!". Each need's bar gained its percentage, and a line under the bars says how long the goat stays happy before the first need runs low, or, when it sulks, what to do. Shown both, the organiser chose "Ship it (Recommended)".
