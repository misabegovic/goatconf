The days

  • Thursday 3rd June 2027: talks, 10:00 to 17:00, IPI Academy, Kulina bana 2, Tuzla. Welcome at 09:45.
  • Friday 4th June 2027: talks, 09:30 to 16:30. Welcome at 09:15.
  • Saturday 5th June 2027: the community day. Each event there is run by its host, who says how to join. See the program.

Seats

Talks run in all seven rooms at once. Rooms have from 13 to 150 seats. Closer to June, you will also register for the sessions you want, because each room has only so many seats. Everyone with a ticket gets an email with how. Seats nobody registered for go to whoever is at the door when a session starts. All rooms keep the same times, so you can change rooms at every break.

Students and volunteers

Students are welcome, and the ticket is free for everyone. Want to help run GoatConf as a volunteer? Write to us and say so.

Using a wheelchair, deaf or hard of hearing, blind or with low vision? See accessibility for what to expect.

How it works

Registration runs on Luma. The button opens it over this page. It asks for your name and email address, and, if you like, why you want to come. Luma emails you when you are in, with a calendar invite. Registering for GoatConf gets you your ticket and tells us how many people to expect. By registering you accept the code of conduct.

Your data

Luma (luma.com) stores your registration for the organiser, under its own privacy policy. The organiser, Muhamed Isabegović, keeps your name, your email and anything you write about why you want to come, and uses them only to run GoatConf 2027 and to write to you about it, because you agreed. They are deleted after the feedback survey goes out, and never shared or sold. You can ask to see, correct or delete them through the contact form or at info@muhamed.at.
