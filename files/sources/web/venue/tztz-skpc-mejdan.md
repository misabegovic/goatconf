Sport, Cultural and Business Center ‘Mejdan’ Tuzla was constructed in 1984 to satisfy demand of citizens of Tuzla and its environment in regard to physical culture and sport and to improve and promote sport, culture and art and various business events.
The Center is located in the western part of the City, between the northern slopes of Ilinčica and southern city highway and along with transverse road to Slatina settlement. It is located close to the old settlement Mejdan, after which it is named.
Within SCBC ‘Mejdan’ there are three groups of facilities which can function independently, separately one from another or jointly as one unit for certain program activities. One of the groups consist of University hall (big hall) with additional equipment. The second group consists of sports hall (small hall) with its equipment. The third group consist of a bowling alley, business premises and premises for exhibitions, which are connected with a heated pedestrian path.
Complex SCBC ‘Mejdan’ with its 16.500 m2 of covered space and 5.000 m2 of plateaus started its operations on 2 nd October 1984 on the Day of the City of Tuzla. Mny domestic and international sort, cultural and business events were held in the Center and the first significant program activity happened in 1985 – European Championship in Basketball for Cadets.
SCBC ‘Mejdan’ Tuzla receives 1.000 visitors daily – sportsmen, businessmen, citizens i.e. 30.000 monthly and 360.000 annually. This means that every citizen of Tuzla visits this beautiful building two times a year on average.
