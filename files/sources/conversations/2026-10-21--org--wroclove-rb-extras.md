sources/conversations/2026-10-21--org--wroclove-rb-community-partner.md),
the agent offered two follow-ups: rewording the supporters page, which

not a company, and drafting a thank-you post for the drumbeat. The

  already having one.
- **Which week:** "Week 14, Tue 12 Jan 2027". "Thank you, wroclove.rb"
  takes Tuesday 12 January 2027, and that week's "Who's organising" post
