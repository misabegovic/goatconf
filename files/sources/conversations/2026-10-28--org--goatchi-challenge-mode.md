The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Should we let the  player decide if the goat goes into a permanent sleep or not if they don't take care of it?

The agent suggested letting players choose, but not a "permanent sleep", which reads as the goat dying, and noted that the accepted decision record says the goat never dies. Asked what should happen to a goat nobody looks after, the organiser chose "Opt-in: it wanders off (Recommended)": players choose a challenge mode when picking a goat; left with every need empty for about three days, the goat goes exploring the hills above Tuzla, and the page shows how many days it was kept; the default stays forgiving.

Shown it in the browser (the checkbox, off by default; the warning while a challenge goat sulks; the hiking goat and "You kept it for 7 days" when it leaves; picking another at once; "Kept for N days" on every goat), they chose "Ship it (Recommended)".
