# The call for talks: the organiser judges every proposal, with no strict deadlines

Accepted 2026-10-12 by the organiser
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`,
question 1). Supersedes
[the blind-panel CFP decision](cfp-and-speakers.md).

## What

The organiser judges every proposal to the call for talks and every
add-something submission
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--organiser-judges-no-strict-deadlines.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--extension-listings-judged.md`).
The call has no strict deadline and opens with the site on goatconf.com.
Slots are filled as proposals come in, judged on interest and quality
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--slots-filled-as-we-go.md`). The
call stays open until the program is full.

## Why

The organiser's words were "I judge everything" and "there are no strict
deadlines, I might open the call sooner also"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--organiser-judges-no-strict-deadlines.md`).
A single judge who fills slots as proposals arrive decides faster than a
panel with a fixed window, which fits a program whose size depends on
what comes in.

## How

Proposals arrive on the talk proposal Google Form
([website decision](goatconf-ba-and-google-forms.md)). The organiser
reads each proposal first in the response sheet with the name column
hidden, which keeps the charter's promise that the open call is read
without the name first, then decides with the name shown. Each proposer
hears back by email, whatever the answer. The criteria, published with
the call, are interest to the people who come and the quality of the
idea. An accepted proposal becomes a program record and appears on the
program page. When the program is full, the site says so and the form is
closed.

## Alternatives

- **The blind panel of two or three with a fixed window.** Superseded.
- **A second reader for conflicts of interest.** Not adopted; the
  organiser keeps the whole decision.
- **A panel for talks and the organiser for everything else.** Not
  adopted.

## Consequences

- **Closes:** the blind panel; the mid-December to late January window
  as a fixed period; the April schedule date as a deadline.
- **Opens:** a call that is open now, decisions as proposals arrive, and
  a program that grows with them.
- **Costs:** one person's time for every proposal; one judge is a single
  point of trust, which the published criteria and the public program
  record are there to offset.
