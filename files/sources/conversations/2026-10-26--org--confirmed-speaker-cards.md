The organiser asked, verbatim:

> for speakers... should we also have the images I need to use for social media when making posts? like to announce a speaker

The drumbeat's speaker spotlights used the speaker's own "I'm speaking" card, which speaks in the speaker's voice; supporters have a card in each voice (their "Proud supporter" card and GoatConf's thank-you card). Asked whether each speaker should get a second card in GoatConf's own voice for the posts announcing them, the organiser chose "Yes, and show it on the profile", over the agent's recommendation of the card without the profile section, or reusing "I'm speaking".

The agent's draft headline was "New speaker at GoatConf 2027". The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> shouldn't be "new speaker" lets maybe use "confirmed speaker" ?

So the card reads "Confirmed speaker at GoatConf 2027", then the speaker's name, role and session title, "Tuzla · 3–5 June 2027 · Free to attend" when there is a title, and the address of their own page. Square and wide, as `speaker-<name>-announce-square.png` and `-wide.png`; each profile shows it under "The announcement card", after "Your card: I'm speaking", and the drumbeat's spotlights use it.
