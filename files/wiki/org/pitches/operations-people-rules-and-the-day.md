# Operations: run from Vienna, volunteers who own a domain, the rules, and the day


> **Bet on and graduated, 2026-10-07.** The organiser bet on this pitch; it continues as [operations-and-the-day.md](../prds/operations-and-the-day.md). This page is kept as the pre-bet record.

Area pitch under [One goat feeds the town](one-goat-minimal-core-and-extensions.md).
Pre-bet, shaped in auto mode on 2026-10-07.

## Problem

The organiser lives in Vienna ("Based in Vienna, working remotely across
Europe", `sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-home.md`) and the event is in
Tuzla. The guide is blunt: "You cannot run a conference alone. You can
try, but you will make yourself miserable"
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Volunteers & Team), and on the day "as much as possible should already
be someone else's problem" (§ Before you arrive). With 1,000 EUR, nobody
is paid. Meanwhile the event has to meet local rules on gatherings and on
personal data, have a code of conduct that means something, and survive
whatever goes wrong.

## Appetite

**Medium:** recruiting volunteers from November, a
fortnightly call from February, a run sheet by May.

## Solution

### People

- **No co-organiser.** The organiser manages from Vienna with volunteers
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--talks-team-and-site.md`). The
  guide recommends one ("The burden is meaningfully lighter with two
  people", § Your co-organizer); here the volunteers' domain owners carry
  that weight instead, and IPI is the on-site contact for the building.
- **Volunteers, each owning a domain**, as the guide lists them: social
  and marketing; catering logistics (here: the lunch list and any funded
  coffee); design and swag; speakers and the CFP; the MC (guide, § What
  volunteers actually do). GoatConf adds two: **extensions** (the person
  who turns host submissions into listings and answers hosts) and **the
  door** (registration on the day).
- **Two pools, both used:** IPI's students, through "an active student
  association, Student Club of the IPI Academy"
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md`), and community volunteers
  from attendees and local tech people, on the organiser's choice
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--talks-team-and-site.md`). What
  volunteers get back (a certificate, a reference, a front-row seat, a
  lunch only if a supporter pays for it, since the organiser pays for no
  food or drink,
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`)
  is set with IPI and the volunteers.
- **A short call every two weeks** from February, so that on the day
  "people know how to communicate with each other" (guide, § Building
  the team).

### The code of conduct

- **Start from the Geek Feminism / Ada Initiative anti-harassment policy,**
  which is public domain: "no credit and no open licencing of your
  version is required", with short, medium and long versions and an
  internal staff procedure
  (`sources/web/coc/geek-feminism-anti-harassment-policy.md`,
  `ada-initiative-conference-policies.md`). The Contributor Covenant 3.0
  (CC BY-SA 4.0) is the alternative
  (`sources/web/coc/contributor-covenant-3-0.md`). A template is not
  enough by itself: one source says templates "should not be considered
  as enforceable" without the event's own reporting details
  (`sources/web/coc/confcodeofconduct-com.md`).
- **It applies to every extension** listed under GoatConf's name; a host
  agrees to it when submitting.
- **Two named contacts: the organiser and a staff member from IPI
  Academy** (organiser's choice,
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`),
  with a phone number, and ten minutes on the stage explaining it at the
  start of the core day (guide, § Code of conduct). Which IPI staff member
  is (unknown, needs source).

### The rules

- **Notifying the police.** Under Tuzla Canton's law on public gathering,
  gatherings for cultural and similar interests "koji nemaju za cilj
  ostvarivanje prihoda" (not aimed at earning income) "ne prijavljuju se"
  (are not notified), Article 35; the exception is when the character or
  expected numbers call for extra security, with notice to the police
  station at least five days ahead, Article 36
  (`sources/web/legal/tk-zakon-o-javnom-okupljanju-1-12.md`). A free,
  indoor, registered conference reads as not requiring notice. With up to
  300 people, a short courtesy notice five or more days ahead costs
  nothing; IPI may already know the practice. This is a reading of the
  law, not legal advice, and new canton laws on events were due as
  proposals in June 2026 (`sources/web/legal/tk-program-rada-skupstine-2026.md`);
  whether they changed this is (unknown, needs source).
- **Personal data.** Bosnia and Herzegovina's personal data protection
  law (Službeni glasnik BiH 12/2025), aligned with the GDPR
  (`sources/web/legal/bih-zakon-o-zastiti-licnih-podataka-12-25.md`),
  means the registration form must say who holds the data, why, on what
  legal basis, for how long, and what rights people have. GoatConf keeps
  the list until the feedback survey is sent and then deletes it, and
  shares it with nobody (the umbrella's charter, after FOSDEM's,
  `sources/web/funding/fosdem-charter.md`). Who the controller is (the
  organiser or IPI as host) is (unknown, needs source); it is a question
  for the registration tool's setup, since no money and no company are
  involved (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`).
- **Accessibility facts published:** the main hall and first floor are
  step-free (organiser, same source); an accessible toilet and seating
  are still to be checked (guide, § Find a venue).

### The day

- **A run sheet** with an owner for each part: the door, the stage and
  the MC, speakers (a quiet room, water, the next speaker ready), the
  hiring corner, lunch directions, first aid, and someone to call (guide,
  § On The Day).
- **Registration that never needs the organiser:** a printed list, pens,
  badges, a volunteer whose only job is the door until it is done (guide,
  § Registration).
- **IPI's hall records nothing and has no microphone**
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`);
  speakers project their voice, on the organiser's choice.
- **Video, only if a supporter funds it, run like FOSDEM's volunteers:** a camera, a mixer
  and a capture of the slides per room, with a checklist for before,
  during and after each talk (`sources/web/tooling/fosdem-video-equipment-overview.md`).
- **Breaks in town:** coffee and lunch happen on Freedom Square, under five minutes from IPI, one
  to two hours each (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`).
  A volunteer walks the square ten minutes before the hall reopens; the
  return time is said at every break and posted online.
- **A plan for what goes wrong:** a speaker stuck at the airport (the
  waitlist), a dead projector (a spare), a conduct report (the two
  contacts), rain on the lake day (the park cafés).

### After

Settle the money within two weeks, a feedback survey within a week, and
an honest note on what to keep and what to cut, which becomes the first
page of the kit (guide, § After The Conference).

## Rabbit holes

- **The organiser doing everything from Vienna.** The single biggest
  risk, taken knowingly without a co-organiser. The mitigation is that
  each volunteer domain has an owner who does not need the organiser on
  the day, and that IPI handles the building.
- **Volunteers without a domain.** Help that is not owned gets dropped.
- **A code of conduct nobody can enforce.** Without named, reachable
  people, it is decoration.

## No-gos

- No paid staff from the 1,000 EUR.
- No attendee data kept after the event's follow-up, or shared.
- No extension listed whose host has not accepted the code of conduct.

## Choices made in auto mode

- **The Geek Feminism policy over the Contributor Covenant.** Because it
  is written for in-person conferences, comes with a staff procedure, and
  needs no attribution.
- **A courtesy police notice even though the law does not ask for one.**
  Because at 300 people it is cheap insurance and builds trust with the
  city; it is the organiser's or IPI's to send.
- **Two new volunteer domains: extensions and the door.** Because the
  extension model creates work the guide's list does not cover.

## Open questions for the organiser

- Would IPI's student club take on volunteering, and what would they want
  in return?
- Which IPI staff member will be the second code-of-conduct contact?
