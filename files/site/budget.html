Every euro in the open

What GoatConf costs, who covered it, and whether the receipt is in hand.

No money passes through the organiser. A supporter pays a supplier directly, or gives something in kind, and the payment is listed here. If something is not covered, the organiser may pay up to 1,000 euro of their own money. That spending is listed here too, line by line. It is never used for food or drink.

The public budget is in ledger.csv, one line per payment or gift.

How to read it

  • Covered by says who paid: the organiser, a named supporter, or "an anonymous supporter".
  • In kind means a gift of something rather than money; the amount is an estimate.
  • Receipt says whether the supplier's receipt is in hand. The receipts themselves are kept by the organiser, not published.
  • A thing counts as funded only once it is listed here.
  • The supporters page shows each supporter's logo and exactly what they funded.

You can see every change ever made to the public budget, not only today's numbers. We will publish a final account within two weeks of the event.
