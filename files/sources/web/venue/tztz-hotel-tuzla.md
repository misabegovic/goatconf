Hotel Tuzla comprises 12 floors, with the first three floors having been fully renovated. The hotel offers both Deluxe and Standard rooms. There are 51 Deluxe rooms, including 8 suites, all furnished with double beds. Each room is elegantly appointed and equipped with modern amenities and technology to ensure a comfortable stay.
More than 170 Standard rooms are situated on the upper floors of the hotel. Guests have access to a contemporary restaurant with a seating capacity of 300. The restaurant’s stylish interior is enhanced by fresh flowers and exotic plants, creating a pleasant and welcoming atmosphere. It is an ideal venue for receptions, celebrations, weddings, formal luncheons, banquets, and other special events.
Hotel Tuzla also features the Crystal Hall, accommodating up to 300 guests, a Congress Hall designed in an amphitheatre style with seating for 120 participants, and a meeting room with a capacity of 35 seats.
In addition, the hotel offers a 25-metre indoor swimming pool.
