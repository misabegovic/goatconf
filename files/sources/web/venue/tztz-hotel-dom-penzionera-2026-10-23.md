OSM places "Dom Penzionera" on Obala Zmaja od Bosne (44.53459, 18.67917), about 770 m straight-line from IPI; the street address given here (Filipa Kljajića 22) was not found in OSM.

Hotel ‘Dom Penzionera‘ is situated in the very heart of Tuzla, within easy reach of numerous amenities required by business travellers, while also serving as an excellent base for exploring the city and enjoying the Panonian Lakes.
In a dedicated section of the property, guests can choose from comfortable single and double rooms equipped with air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, LCD televisions and other modern amenities designed to ensure a pleasant stay.
The hotel is also an ideal venue for hosting a wide range of events, including seminars, meetings, workshops, congresses, presentations and similar gatherings. Its four multifunctional halls are equally suitable for formal luncheons, dinners, graduation celebrations, birthday parties, weddings and other special occasions.
The hotel restaurant offers a selection of delicious traditional Bosnian and Herzegovinian dishes, while the hotel bar and city café provide a variety of beverages at very reasonable prices.
Free secure parking, together with the hotel’s proximity to the main bus station, taxi ranks and Dubrave Airport, makes ‘Hotel Dom Penzionera’ Tuzla a comfortable and convenient choice tailored to the needs of its guests.
