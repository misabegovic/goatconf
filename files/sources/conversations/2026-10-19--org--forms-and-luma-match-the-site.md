Shown eight edits, they picked "Yes, all 8". Made in Chrome on 2026-10-19 and
read back from the public pages:

1. **Propose a session form, description:** "Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June
   2027, IPI Academy, Tuzla. Every slot is one hour: a talk with questions, a
   panel, a workshop or something else, followed by a full hour off. Half an
   hour is fine too: we may pair two short sessions into one hour. We read
   every proposal, the session first and your name after. There is no strict
   deadline: slots fill as good proposals come in. You pay your own travel
   and stay; money from supporters that the event does not need goes first to
   refunding speakers who pay their own way, never promised. If your session
   is accepted, you are invited to the speakers dinner, which we pay for.
   Details: https://goatconf.com/talk"
2. **Propose a session form, travel question:** "Your travel. You pay your
   own travel and stay. Money from supporters that the event does not need
   goes first to refunding speakers who pay their own way, some or all of
   it, after the event; this is not promised. Your answer never affects
   whether your session is picked."
3. **Propose an event form, description:** "A meetup, a dinner, a community
   trip, a walk, a hike, a live podcast, a karaoke night or a hackathon, any
   day from 2 to 6 June 2027. You run it and cover its costs. If we accept
   it, it becomes part of GoatConf 2027: it goes on https://goatconf.com and
   the program, and we send people your way. We accept what follows the
   charter and the code of conduct and fits the program. Details:
   https://goatconf.com/add"
4. **Propose an event form, start question:** "Starts: date and time
   (between 2 and 6 June 2027)".
5. **Contact form, description:** "Questions, supporting GoatConf, press, or

   email out." It had said an IPI Academy staff member helps if you agree.
6. **Luma, Saturday:** "Saturday 5 June: the community's day, with our day
   at the salt lakes and the goat hunt, plus walks, meetups and other events
   people add."
7. **Luma, registration:** "Registering is free and asks for your name and
   email, and optionally why you want to come."
8. **Luma, the call:** "Propose a session: goatconf.com/talk", in place of
   "Give a talk".

Luma's "Approval Required" stays, since the organiser still approves each
registration.
