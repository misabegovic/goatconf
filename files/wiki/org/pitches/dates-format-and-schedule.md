# One core day, wrapped in days others fill, on dates agreed with IPI


> **Bet on and graduated, 2026-10-07.** The organiser bet on this pitch; it continues as [dates-and-the-core-day.md](../prds/dates-and-the-core-day.md). This page is kept as the pre-bet record.

Area pitch under [One goat feeds the town](one-goat-minimal-core-and-extensions.md).
Pre-bet, shaped in auto mode on 2026-10-07. Covers the venue's capacity,
the dates, the length and the shape of the day.

## Problem

The organiser left three things open: the first or second week of June
2027, how many days, and whether there will be more venues
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`,
`2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`, question 6). The guide's advice
pulls two ways. "For a first conference, one day is the safer choice",
lower in risk and easier to program; two days give "more room for the
hallway track" and make "the trip feel more worthwhile to people who have
traveled far" (`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ How many days). Most GoatConf attendees will travel: Tuzla is three to
five hours by bus from Sarajevo, Belgrade or Zagreb, or a Wizz Air flight
from a handful of cities ([Tuzla](../tuzla.md)). A one-day event is a
thin reason for that trip; a two-day core is more than one organiser
with 1,000 EUR can carry.

The venue is IPI Academy "for now"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--venue-ipi.md`), and its room
sizes are not published ([IPI Academy](../ipi-academy.md)). Everything
about capacity waits on one visit.

## Appetite

**Medium:** the date and the room have to be decided and signed first,
in October and November 2026 (the guide's "before you commit" rule); the
day's shape is drafted now and finished when the program is chosen.

## Solution

### The dates: options to propose to IPI, not a choice

The organiser asked to keep the dates flexible and "prepare options to
propose to IPI Academy" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--date-options-for-ipi.md`).
IPI can host on a Saturday or a weekday
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`),
so the options differ in what they give attendees, not in what IPI
allows. Every option keeps the same shape: **one core day** the organiser
guarantees, with the days around it left to extensions.

| Option | Days | Core day | Around it |
|--------|------|----------|-----------|
| **A. First week, weekend core** | Thu 3 to Sun 6 June 2027 | Saturday 5 June | Friday workshops and fringe; Sunday lake and city |
| **B. First week, weekday core** | Wed 2 to Sat 5 June 2027 | Friday 4 June | Thursday workshops and fringe; Saturday lake and city |
| **C. Second week, weekend core** | Thu 10 to Sun 13 June 2027 | Saturday 12 June | Friday workshops and fringe; Sunday lake and city |
| **D. Second week, weekday core** | Wed 9 to Sat 12 June 2027 | Friday 11 June | Thursday workshops and fringe; Saturday lake and city |
| **E. First week, midweek** | Tue 1 to Fri 4 June 2027 | Thursday 3 June | Wednesday workshops and fringe; Friday lake and city; the weekend free to stay on |
| **F. Second week, midweek** | Tue 8 to Fri 11 June 2027 | Thursday 10 June | Wednesday workshops and fringe; Friday lake and city; the weekend free to stay on |

The organiser asked for options where "all lecture days take place on a
weekday" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--weekday-lecture-days.md`).
B and D already do: workshops on Thursday, talks on Friday, only the
lake day on a Saturday. E and F go further and keep the whole event on
weekdays, so the weekend after is the attendee's own: a cheap Tuzla
weekend at the lake for those who stay, or home in time for those who
do not.

How they compare, on what is known:

- **The salt lake.** Swimming "traditionally starts at the beginning of
  May" in one report (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-sezona-kurir-2026-04.md`),
  the tourist board lists the season opening in June
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`), and in 2019 it
  opened on 7 June (`sources/web/tuzla/panonika-n1-2019.md`). The second
  week (C, D) is the safer bet for a swim; the first week (A, B) may still
  have one, and the lake day works without swimming.
- **Holidays.** Sunday 6 June 2027 is the Islamic New Year, listed as a
  regional holiday by one source and not by the Federation's list of
  non-working days (`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-officeholidays.md`,
  `sources/web/tuzla/neradni-dani-fbih-2026-paragraf.md`); it touches A's
  lake day only, and is unverified. Kurban Bajram falls in mid-May,
  clear of all four (`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-calendarlabs.md`).
- **Weekend core (A, C), weekday lectures (B, D) or all midweek (E, F).**
  A Saturday suits people who come on their own time and money, which is
  most of a free event's audience. Weekday lecture days suit people whose
  employer sends them, and speakers whose company pays their travel; the
  midweek options also leave the weekend after as a reason to stay. The
  cost of weekdays is that people taking leave to come need two or three
  days off. Which matters more is the organiser's judgement; the guide
  does not settle it.
- **IPI on weekdays.** Teaching may still be running in early June, so
  rooms on Tuesday to Friday are a question for IPI that a Saturday is
  not. It bears most on E and F, which need three weekdays.
- **IPI's own calendar.** The App Challenge's 2026 topic deadline was 15
  June and its finalists present at IPI's DIEC conference
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-app-challenge.md`); exams, defences
  or DIEC in early June would rule options out. Unknown until IPI says.
- **Other June events in Tuzla.** The Džumbus festival's 2026 edition
  started on 5 June (`sources/web/tuzla/dzumbus-festival-festivalfinder.md`);
  if 2027 starts the same week, it adds life to option A or B and
  competes for beds. Its 2027 dates are unknown.
- **Flights.** Which weekdays Wizz Air flies to Tuzla in June 2027 is
  unknown ([Tuzla](../tuzla.md), § Getting there); it may favour some
  arrival days over others once the schedule is published.

**What to ask IPI**, so its answer picks the option:

1. Which of these days are free of teaching, exams, defences and DIEC in
   June 2027? (Options E and F need three weekdays.)
2. Can the hall and several rooms be held for all of the option's days,
   or only for the core day?
3. Would IPI prefer a weekend or a weekday, given its own staff and
   students?
4. Can it confirm in writing by November 2026, so the CFP can open in
   December?

Whichever option IPI picks, a traveller gets three or four days worth
the trip while the organiser guarantees one. It is the guide's "one day is safer" and its
"two days... make the trip feel more worthwhile" held together, and it is
the umbrella's minimal core made literal.

### The room

- **The organiser knows the venue:** "I visited IPI many times this year
  already" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`).
  What the brain lacks is that knowledge written down, against the
  guide's checklist: the largest hall's seats, a second room for
  workshops, AV and Wi-Fi, set-up and tear-down time, what IPI staff
  handle, and the routes in and out with their barriers, for the
  accessibility page (guide, § Find a venue).
- **What the organiser knows** (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`):
  the largest hall seats about 120, with a projector, sound and Wi-Fi but
  no microphone and no recording; several other rooms are free for
  workshops; the main hall and first floor are step-free; Saturday or a
  weekday both work, so Saturday stands.
- **The registration cap equals the hall: about 120.** The organiser's
  words: "I would register per hall capacity". The core day stays in one
  room with a waiting list. The rest of the 120 to 300 the organiser
  hoped for can come through the extension days, since workshops, hubs
  and fringe events in IPI's other rooms and around the city have their
  own capacity.
- **No microphone:** speakers project their voice in a room of about 120,
  on the organiser's choice.
- **If demand far exceeds 120,** the organiser's choice is still the
  cap. A larger room would be a venue extension, not a core change. Hotel Tuzla's
  Crystal Hall accommodates "up to 300 guests"
  (`sources/web/venue/tztz-hotel-tuzla.md`); its price is unknown, and
  the guide warns hotels often come with room blocks and food minimums
  (guide, § Find a venue). That makes it a fallback, not a plan.

### The shape of the core day: few talks, long breaks in town

The organiser does not want "a huge schedule that cramps everything
together", but "space and time for folks to hang out". IPI is "only where
lectures take place"; coffee and lunch breaks happen "in the city on the
nearby town square", and "they can take 1-2 hours each"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`).

That goes further than the guide's 30-30 format, which pairs each
30-minute talk with a 30-minute break and fits six to eight talks in a
day (guide, § The 30-30 format). GoatConf keeps fewer talks and gives the
breaks to the city. The organiser chose **three to four talks**
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--freedom-square-and-few-talks.md`),
in short blocks, as an illustration (times to be set with IPI):

| When | What | Where |
|------|------|-------|
| Morning, under an hour | Doors, a short welcome, ten minutes on the code of conduct, one or two talks | IPI's hall |
| Late morning, 1 to 2 hours | Coffee | Freedom Square (Trg slobode), under five minutes' walk |
| Early afternoon, under an hour | One talk | IPI's hall |
| Afternoon, 1 to 2 hours | Lunch | Freedom Square |
| Late afternoon, under an hour | One closing talk "that sends people out the door with something to think about" (guide, § Pacing the day) | IPI's hall |
| Evening | Free, or fringe events and a social as extensions | The city |

Pacing still follows the guide: the densest talk first, something lighter
after lunch, a closer that sticks (§ Pacing the day). A prepared MC
announces when the hall reopens, since people are spread around the
square rather than in a foyer (guide, § The MC). Workshops on the
lecture day, if any, never run against talks.

What this needs, and what is unknown:

- **The square.** Freedom Square (Trg slobode), under five minutes' walk
  from IPI (same source); it is also where the tourist train to the salt
  lakes starts ([Tuzla](../tuzla.md)). How many people its cafés and
  places to eat can absorb at once is (unknown, needs source); about 120
  arriving together is the case to check.
- **Getting people back.** With breaks spread over cafés, the return to
  the hall needs a clear time, said at every break and posted on the
  website; a volunteer can walk the square ten minutes before.
- **Rain.** June is Tuzla's wettest month ([Tuzla](../tuzla.md));
  covered café terraces or IPI's own rooms are the fallback for a break.
- **Money.** Each person buys their own coffee and lunch, which in Tuzla
  costs little (an inexpensive meal about 4.60 EUR, a cappuccino about
  1.66 EUR, [Tuzla](../tuzla.md)); a supporter may pay a café directly
  for a round, under the rule that no money goes through the organiser.

### Things the calendar should not collide with

- IPI's own conference DIEC and its App Challenge, whose 2026 topic
  deadline was 15 June (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-app-challenge.md`);
  DIEC's date is unknown, and a joint moment (App Challenge finalists
  presenting at GoatConf) could be an extension.
- Other conferences in the region. Balkan Ruby is off in 2027
  (`sources/web/funding/balkanruby-blog-2027-is-off.md`); other June 2027
  dates are (unknown, needs source).
- June rain: the wettest month in Tuzla, with about 11 rain days
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia.md`). The core is indoors; the lake
  day needs a rain plan.

## Rabbit holes

- **Growing Friday into a second core day.** Tempting once workshops fill
  up; it doubles the organiser's load. Friday stays extensions.
- **Booking hotels for attendees.** The guide's other expensive mistake
  (§ Expensive mistakes); GoatConf lists places and prices instead.
- **Waiting for perfect information on the lakes.** The weekend after 7
  June is a reasonable bet; the activity day works without swimming.

## No-gos

- No second core day in 2027.
- No parallel talks in the core.
- No public date until IPI has confirmed it in writing.

## Choices made in auto mode

- **No date chosen; six options for IPI instead.** On the organiser's
  request to stay flexible
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--date-options-for-ipi.md`). If
  asked for a lean, the agent's is option C (the second week, a Saturday
  core): the lake is likelier open and a free, self-funded audience comes
  more easily on a weekend. That is a lean, not a pick.
- **One core day plus extension days, not two core days.** Because it
  keeps the organiser's promise to one day while giving travellers a
  reason to stay.

## Open questions for the organiser

- All six options go to IPI (the organiser picked A to D and asked for the
  weekday ones); which one IPI can host decides.
- Do you know DIEC's usual date?
