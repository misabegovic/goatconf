# The organiser's direction on the site and the program, 2026-10-08

Direction the organiser gave in a working session on 2026-10-08, while
previewing the site locally. Verbatim where quoted; the rest is a close
paraphrase. It is direction, not yet accepted decisions: the program
shapes land through `/shape`, and the site changes through the website
PRD and the organiser's review.

- Explore more photos to make the site rich.
- Work on defining the program: how many days, how many talks, whether
  panel discussions or not.
- The site does not tell the extendable-conference story well: "the idea
  was a minimalistic conference that everyone who wants can make their
  own, kinda like the pi agent and how it's built."
- Food photos beyond ćevapi: "bosnian pita sirnica, burek etc... corbe..."
- "prices of accommodation also, where they could book it maybe too"
- "some sort of speakers profiles and lists... that also translate into
  the program maybe?"
- The salt lake is not a single day-after event: "we can go every day to
  the lake and chill... the day after can be whatever... it can also be a
  trip to Lukomir... or somewhere else magical in Bosnia."
- "we should maybe reconsider the first image shown, maybe we need a
  better hero photo of Tuzla?"
- The event is in June, so the site should show summer photos of Tuzla,
  and spring ones where summer is missing.
