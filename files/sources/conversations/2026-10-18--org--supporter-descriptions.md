---
title: "Supporter profiles say who they are and how to support them, drafted from their own pages"
date: 2026-10-18
kind: conversation
---

With supporter profiles in PR #83, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Also on this page: https://goatconf.com/extensions.html . We should have a card leading to the page of that event on the site
> Maybe we should also say something nice and descriptive about the supporter, who they are, what they do, how others can support them?

The agent checked the live events page: since PR #82 (published
2026-10-18 at 10:59) it already carries a sticker that opens the event's
page; the site lets browsers keep a page for ten minutes, so an earlier
copy was probably showing. Asked whether more was meant, the organiser
answered "That's it, it was cached".

Asked whose words should describe each supporter, the organiser chose
"Draft from their sites, they OK": the agent writes two or three
sentences per supporter from their own public pages, saved and cited in
the brain, with a "How to support them" list of links, and the organiser
or the supporter approves them before they go on the site; the agent
drafts the note to send them.

Profiles take the description as the body of the supporter's record
("Who they are") and the links as `support_them:` ("How to support
them"); neither shows until a record has them, and the supporters page
keeps only the short line. The drafts are on
`wiki/org/outreach/supporter-profiles.md`.
