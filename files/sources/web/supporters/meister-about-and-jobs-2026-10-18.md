## https://www.meistertask.com/pages/about-us

> We're Meister. We help people work, think and collaborate better.

> Meister builds digital productivity tools that make it easy for anyone to go from ideas to action.

> Founded in 2006 by Till Vollmer and Michael Hollauf, Meister is headquartered in Vienna, Austria with 60 team members located across Europe.

> MeisterTask is the secure work management platform that gives teams a central source of truth for projects, tasks and documentation.

> MindMeister is the intuitive mind mapping tool used by millions of students, teachers and professionals to visualize ideas and information.

> We strive to make collaboration easy and work fun. That goes for how we build our products, and how we treat one another.
