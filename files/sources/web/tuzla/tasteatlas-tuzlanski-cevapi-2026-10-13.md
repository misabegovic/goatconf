Page: https://www.tasteatlas.com/tuzlanski-cevapi, read in a browser on 2026-10-13. Summarised, with short quotes.

- TasteAtlas files tuzlanski ćevapi as a ground meat dish from Tuzla and lists Restoran Limenka as a place to eat them.
- The meat is usually "a combination of ground mutton, beef, and lamb (usually in a ratio of 2:1:1)", though some places use beef only; it is mixed by hand, seasoned with salt and pepper, and rested in the fridge for hours or a day.
- They are cooked on an oiled grill and peppered again.
- They are served in a round flatbread called lepina (lepinja), "which has previously been dipped in a broth made from beef bones called poljev or poliv", with spring onions or chopped raw onion.
