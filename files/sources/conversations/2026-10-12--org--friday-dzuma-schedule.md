The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> for Friday, lets leave from 11:45 to 13:45 everything free since it's Dzuma prayer, lets make Friday schedule a bit different

Offered three ways to run Friday, the organiser first answered "three slots but lets not have weird time slots", then raised a second view, verbatim:

> I mean, we could still have the same schedule, just some people would choose to go to Dzuma ? that could also be a perspective?

and asked whether the breaks align with the prayer times on those dates. The Vaktija for Tuzla gives podne at 12:44 on both 3 and 4 June 2027 (`sources/web/tuzla/vaktija-tuzla-2027-06-03-04.md`). The organiser then wrote, verbatim:

> no, I meant with not having a special break for Dzuma, maybe optimize the placement of the 1 hour break instead ? start the day 15 minutes earlier?

Shown that a start 15 minutes earlier puts the break at 12:45, exactly at podne, and that a start 30 minutes earlier puts it at 12:30 to 13:30, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> lets do that for now, and keep flexibility to change later on

Read as: Friday 4 June 2027 keeps four one-hour slots with an hour off after each of the first three, starting half an hour earlier than Thursday: welcome 09:15, slots at 09:30, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30, the hour off at 12:30 for Džuma prayer and lunch, and the day ending at 16:30. Thursday keeps 10:00 to 17:00. The organiser wants this easy to change later.
