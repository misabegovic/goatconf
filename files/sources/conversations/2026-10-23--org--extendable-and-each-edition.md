title: "Extendable is GoatConf's special aspect, and each edition takes its shape from who joins"
date: 2026-10-23
kind: conversation
---

The organiser asked, verbatim:

> How obvious is it to someone that they can extend the event and make it their own? That this is one of the special aspects of GoatConf? Each year the edition might change depending on folks joining and impacting the shape of the event

The agent read the site and the brain and answered that the invitation to
add an event is visible (hero sticker 6 "Extendable", slot 10 "Your event,
any day from 2 to 6 June", the eight "Yours?" kind slots, the Saturday
section of the program), but that it reads as one feature among several
rather than GoatConf's identity, that the propose an event and events
people add pages lead with rules, that "Propose an event" is not in the
top bar, and that nothing on the site or in the brain says one edition
shapes the next. The agent asked five questions (how firm the year to
year idea is, where it should sit, whether "extend" covers sessions and
supporters too, the top bar, and whether to draft first). The organiser
answered with the next message instead
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--host-guides-and-venues.md`).

Stated by the organiser here, as their own words: extending the event
and making it one's own is "one of the special aspects of GoatConf", and
"each year the edition might change depending on folks joining and
impacting the shape of the event". The second is phrased as a
possibility ("might change"), not a commitment.
