Do I need to pay for free tickets?
As a customer, you start with a free quota of
500
free tickets per calendar year.
For every ticket that you sell for money, we will increase your quota by one more free ticket.
Only if you use more free tickets than paid tickets, you will pay our fee for free tickets.
What currencies can I use?
Any currency supported by the payment provider of your choice. In most cases, we'll convert
the resulting fee to EUR using a standard conversion rate before invoicing it to you.
