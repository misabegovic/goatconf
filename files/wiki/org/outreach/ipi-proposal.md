# Draft: the date proposal to IPI Academy (Bosnian)

> **The date question is answered.** IPI Academy has accepted Thursday 3
> and Friday 4 June 2027, with Saturday 5 June, and GoatConf has access to
> all seven rooms, as the organiser reported on 2026-10-12 (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`). The
> letter below is kept for the record; its other asks (a second
> code-of-conduct contact, volunteers, accessibility facts) can still be
> sent.

A draft for the organiser to send; nothing here has been sent. It carries
the six date options and the four questions from
[Dates and the core day](../prds/dates-and-the-core-day.md), and the asks
the other PRDs need from IPI: rooms, a second code-of-conduct contact,
volunteers from the Student Club, and the accessibility facts the site
must publish. It is written in Bosnian, as a formal letter introduced by a
short personal note, on the organiser's choice
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--next-ipi-and-website.md`).

The fillers in square brackets are left for the organiser to complete
before sending: [ime] and [ime i prezime] for the recipient, [funkcija]
for their role, and [datum] for the date. Weekdays for June 2027 were checked against the
calendar on 2026-10-07. The recipient's address is IPI's, Kulina bana
br. 2 (Skver), 75000 Tuzla (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-kontakt.md`).

## English summary, for the record

The letter introduces GoatConf as free, in English and open to many
disciplines, a small core of one lecture day that others extend, with no
company behind it, no money passing through the organiser and a public
budget (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`). It asks
for the hall, about 120 seats, for one core day of three to four talks
with two long breaks on Freedom Square, and other rooms for workshops
around it (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`). It lists
the six options A to F, the midweek two included
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--weekday-lecture-days.md`), and
asks the four questions: free days (teaching, exams, defences, DIEC,
which appears on `sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-app-challenge.md`),
rooms for all days or only the core day, weekend or weekday, and written
confirmation by the end of November 2026.

It also asks for an IPI staff member as the second code-of-conduct
contact (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`),
to approach the Student Club about volunteering
(`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md`), for the accessible-toilet
fact, and for what IPI gives in kind, so it appears on the public ledger.
It promises no public announcement before written confirmation, the
road's first rule.

## Update, 2026-10-12

The Bosnian letter above now asks for two core days, Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June 2027, with Saturday 5 June free, and offers two first-week windows, A and B. It replaces the 2026-10-07 options, which each had one core day, and the sentence about one room and about 120 seats. It follows the organiser's direction of 2026-10-12 (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--two-core-days-and-saturday.md`, `sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--all-available-rooms.md`). The English summary above still describes the 2026-10-07 letter.

Still for the organiser to decide before sending:

- Whether the letter goes out with two core days. The one-core-day rule in the dates PRD is still in place, and a draft ADR waits for approval.
- The hall's seats: 150 in the June 2026 visit notes, under 120 on 2026-10-07. The letter asks IPI to confirm them.
- The 120-place cap. It stays in the letter as a registration cap. The parallel-room format would change it.

## Update, 2026-10-14: not sent, and no longer needed

IPI Academy accepted the two core days, Saturday 5 June and all seven rooms, as the organiser reported on 2026-10-12 (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`), and the organiser is satisfied with that verbal acceptance, so no written confirmation is sought (`sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--decisions-one-by-one.md`). The three points above are settled: the two-core-days decision was approved on 2026-10-12 ([Dates: two core days](../adrs/two-core-days-first-week.md)); the main hall has 120 seats, as the organiser decided on 2026-10-14 (`sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--decisions-one-by-one.md`); and registration has no cap (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--session-registration-later.md`). The letter is kept as a record of what was drafted.
