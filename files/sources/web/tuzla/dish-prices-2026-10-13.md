Research by an agent on 2026-10-13 for the twelve dishes and drinks on the GoatConf home page. Every price found is recorded below with the place, the item text as shown, the price in KM, the URL and the date read. Follows on from vegetarian-places-research-2026-10-13.md in this folder, which already records the Pizzeria Mozaik pita prices.

How the data was read:

- Korpa.ba (delivery app). Plain fetches get a Cloudflare "Just a moment..." page. In a normal browser tab the check cleared by itself after a few seconds (no CAPTCHA was shown or solved). The home page tab "Tuzla" listed 63 Tuzla partners; all 63 menus were read on 2026-10-13, plus the menus of the partners listed under the other city tabs (Sarajevo, Banja Luka, Mostar, Zenica, Doboj, Prijedor, Bijeljina) and under the categories Pita, Kuhano, Kolači i torte, Lokalno, Supe, Ćevapi and Orijentalno, about 410 partner menus in all. Partner page URLs have the form https://korpa.ba/partner/<slug>. Addresses are as shown on each partner page.
- "Trenutno nedostupno" on Korpa means "currently unavailable" at the time of reading. Such items are recorded but marked "(unavailable)".
- No account was used, no form was submitted, no place was contacted.

All Korpa prices below were read on 2026-10-13.
