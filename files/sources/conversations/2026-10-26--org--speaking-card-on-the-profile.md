After the "I'm speaking" cards began showing the session title before a slot (sources/conversations/2026-10-26--org--speaking-cards-show-the-talk.md), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> we should also show the i'm speaking card on their profile, same as we show these card on other places for supporters etc

A speaker's profile only had a "Download the \"I'm speaking\" card" link for the square card under their session. Supporters' profiles show their cards in their own section: a heading, a line on what the card is for, the wide card as an image, and both sizes to download. So every speaker's profile now has a section, "Your card: I'm speaking", in the same shape, after the session, in place of the single link.
