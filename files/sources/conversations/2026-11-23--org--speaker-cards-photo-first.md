---
title: "The speaker cards redesigned over six rounds of pictures: the photo first, blue and paper as the pair, and \"ship it\""
date: 2026-11-23
kind: conversation
---

On Monday 23 November 2026, asking for the pull request with Carmine
Paolino's spotlight to be merged, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok, open a PR and merge it. After it lands I want to iterate on improving the design of his card and thus unanimously the cards of all speakers

The agent drew each round as pictures outside the repository and sent
them, as the organiser had asked for design work on 2026-11-21
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).
Nothing in the repository or on the site changed until the last message
below.

**Round one.** Three directions for the card announcing a speaker, beside
the card as it was: A, a page of the album (a tilted photo sticker and the
date, the town and "Free" as stickers); B, a poster on the album's blue
with the name stacked in white and yellow and a yellow band along the
foot; C, the card as it was, tightened. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I like the poster direction, but I also like the current toghtened... I think I like the way the photo is done on B... We need to iterate further to find what makes sense... Probably larger presence of the speaker photo is good

**Round two.** B's photo (a rounded square with a white edge on a yellow
block), larger, on four layouts: D, the poster with a bigger photo; E, the
poster on paper; F, the tightened card with that photo; G, the photo
first, with the goat beside it and the name on one line under both. The
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Between D, E and G ... Explore these

**Round three.** Six squares: D and E refined, G on blue and on paper, G
with "GoatConf 2027" spelled out beside the photo and the seal on the
band (5), and G with the photo running off the corner (6). The agent
pointed out that the side by side layouts shrink a long name and
recommended 5. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Let's go with 5, blue and paper as the pair... But let's continue iterating

**Round four.** Version 5 on blue and on paper, square and wide, with two
options: "Free" in a dark pill on the band, and the photo tilted like the
album's photo stickers. The agent recommended the pill and advised against
the tilt. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I like tilted and the free pill. The right side after the photo seems off a bit... Let's continue iterating

**Round five.** Four ways to arrange what stands right of the photo: A,
the goat sits on the wordmark; B, the seal above a larger goat, with the
wordmark on the band; C, everything on the right margin; D, a one-line
wordmark over a larger goat. On the wide card C and D came out the same.
The agent recommended B. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> A, for wide I like more C and D

**Round six.** The choices put together for all eight published speakers,
on blue and on paper, with every goat at one height and the speaker's own
card carrying "I'm speaking" on the tab. The agent asked whether this is
the design, proposed that paper be the card under the plain file name and
blue the one ending in `-dark`, so links already shared keep a light card,
and asked whether Carmine Paolino's post of Tuesday 24 November should go
out with the new card. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Sounds good ship it. We should also update other cards to follow the same design

Read as: the design is approved, paper is the plain file and blue the
`-dark` one, and the speaker cards are built and merged now. The other
cards (the posts, the supporters' and the events' cards) are to follow the
same design; they had not been drawn or shown when this was written, so
they are not part of this change.

What the agent chose without the organiser's word, for the organiser to
look at again: the link preview a speaker's page shows takes the new card
too, in its light version, still without a bubble and without "Free", as
it was; the band reads "3–5 June 2027 · Tuzla", the date before the town,
where the cards' line read "Tuzla · 3–5 June 2027 · Free to attend"; the
tab on the photo reads "Confirmed speaker" or "I'm speaking" without "at
GoatConf 2027", which the wordmark now says; and the dark speaker card
stands on the album's blue `#0b3d91`, where every other dark card still
stands on the night ground.
