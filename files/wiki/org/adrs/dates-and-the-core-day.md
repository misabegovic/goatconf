# Give the core day two long breaks on Freedom Square, coffee and then lunch

> **Superseded 2026-10-12** by [two core days first week](two-core-days-first-week.md), on the organiser's approval (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`). Kept as the record of the earlier decision.

Answers the "Decision needed" of
[Dates and the core day](../prds/dates-and-the-core-day.md). Accepted 2026-10-07 from the organiser's answer
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--adr-answers.md`, question 4), and approved by the organiser (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--adrs-approved.md`).

## What

The core day has two long breaks, each one to two hours, both on Freedom
Square: a coffee break mid-morning and a lunch break after the next
talk. Its blocks, in order, are: the welcome and ten minutes on the code
of conduct; the first talk, or two; the coffee break; a talk; the lunch
break; the closing talk; then the evening free for fringe events. The
date option stays IPI's to pick from the six the PRD lists.

## Why

The organiser asked for a day that does not cram: "I want space and time
for folks to hang out", with coffee and lunch on the nearby town square
taking "1-2 hours each"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`). Two
breaks rather than one long midday break mean people go out and come
back twice, so nobody sits through more than two talks in a row, and the
afternoon still has a talk to come back for. The square is under five
minutes from IPI and the day has three to four talks
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--freedom-square-and-few-talks.md`).

## How

The clock times are set with IPI once it picks an option, inside the
hours it gives the hall. The MC says the return time at the start of
every break and it is posted on the website; a volunteer walks the square
ten minutes before the hall reopens. Each person buys their own coffee
and lunch, or a supporter pays a café directly; the organiser pays for no
food or drink
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
Speakers project their voice, since the hall has no microphone
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).

Which option IPI confirms is recorded later as an amendment to this ADR,
when IPI answers in writing; the options with every lecture day on a
weekday stay on the list the organiser asked for
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--weekday-lecture-days.md`).

## Alternatives

- **Two breaks** *(chosen)*: two returns to the square, short runs of
  talks, an afternoon to come back for.
- **One long midday break:** talks gathered either side, a single return;
  rejected because the morning and afternoon blocks grow long and the day
  loses its rhythm.
- **Decide with IPI later:** leaves the run sheet open until IPI answers;
  the shape does not depend on IPI, only the clock times do.

## Consequences

- **Opens:** a run sheet that can be drafted now and timed later.
- **Costs:** two returns from the square are two chances to lose people;
  the walk-the-square volunteer and the posted time are the answer.
- **Costs:** with up to four hours of breaks, the day is long; a short
  IPI window could squeeze the breaks toward one hour each.
