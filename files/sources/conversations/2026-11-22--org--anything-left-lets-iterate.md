before anything is published. The list, as it was given:

  finder's checkbox and labels, the map pins); the top bar wrapping to two
  rows below about 1080 pixels; the brand page's card list on a phone; the
  weight of the share pictures; forced colours on the pages not yet
  checked.
- A proposal, to be shown and not assumed wanted: the same open and close
  on the two other long pages, travel and host an event.
