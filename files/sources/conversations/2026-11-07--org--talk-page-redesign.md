title: "The talk page, redesigned: what a speaker gets first, travel after"
date: 2026-11-07
kind: conversation
---

On Saturday 7 November 2026 the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I'm thinking if we could improve the propose a session page and make it look more appealing to the potential speaker. Currently it might put them immediately off if they think they are paying for stuff etc. maybe split into first parts that sttract them and then clarify travel

The agent read talk.html and found the benefits for a speaker in one
section near the end, "What you get, and what you pay", where "You pay
your own travel and stay" sat beside the room, the profile and the
dinner. It proposed a new order (why speak at GoatConf, what a session can
be, who we are looking for, how a session is picked, getting to Tuzla,
the slot, what the form asks) and noted that the call text in the CFP PRD
was to put the money first, while the speaker persona needs the
self-funded rule before effort is spent on a proposal; travel moving
lower but staying above the form keeps the second.

Asked four questions, the organiser chose:

- **How far:** a full redesign of the page, not only a reorder.
- **Travel:** a warm "Getting to Tuzla" section: you arrange your own
  trip, Tuzla is affordable (hotel prices from the travel page), Hotel
  Vertigos next door, a company that covers travel thanked as a
  supporter, and the possible refund, still saying plainly that travel is
  not paid.
- **The top button:** keep both form buttons as they are, at the top and
  at the bottom.
- **Delivery:** the agent builds it, runs the local CI, sends phone and
  desktop screenshots, and opens a PR for the organiser to merge.

Built as, within the sticker album (DESIGN.md unchanged in its world):

- talk.html moves from one prose page to album pages with bands, like
  Make it yours. The lede invites: "Bring an hour of what you know to
  Tuzla".
- **Why speak at GoatConf** comes first: who comes (120 to 300 people
  expected, people who build and sell software), who organises it, the
  form button, and six numbered stickers: One hour, An hour off,
  Promoted, Dinner (we pay for it), Rehearsal (for first-time speakers),
  and an empty printed slot, Recorded?, since recording the two big halls
  is an aim, not promised.
- **What a session can be** shows the formats as open slots with the
  goat peeking: Talk, Panel, Workshop, Live coding, Fireside chat, Not
  sure?, with workshops, half an hour and "a session or an event?" beside
  them.
- **Getting to Tuzla** comes after who and how: you book your own trip
  and stay, as everyone coming does; a price list of a stay (Hotel
  Vertigos from about 48 €, a guesthouse from about 38 €, a mid-range
  hotel 40 to 65 €, the bus from Sarajevo 12 to 20 €, the airport city
  bus 1.30 €, a simple meal 4.60 €, with the dates they were checked);
  what happens if your company covers it, if you pay your own way, and
  staying for Saturday.
- **Your slot** and **What the form asks** end the page; the profile
  rule reads "This is part of speaking at GoatConf, so by proposing a
  session you agree to it if your session is accepted", and the second
  form button follows it.
