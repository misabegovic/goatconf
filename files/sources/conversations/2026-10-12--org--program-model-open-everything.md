date: 2026-10-12

Asked which program model to write up for the core day and the open rooms, the organiser picked one option, by its label:

> Open everything

The options offered were "Fixed core, open rooms (Recommended)", "Open everything" (described as talks and workshops posted as they are confirmed, in every room on every day, including the core day), and "Keep the plan as it is". Read as: the organiser wants a write-up of the model in which every room takes sessions on every day, the core day included, with the program changing as sessions are confirmed. The choice does not settle the conflicts that a write-up of it has to resolve, and the organiser has not yet answered those.
