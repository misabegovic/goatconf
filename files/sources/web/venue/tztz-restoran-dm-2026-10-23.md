Restaurant ‘DM’ features a modern, fully equipped kitchen and offers seating for over 300 guests, along with additional halls for various occasions, as well as a congress hall and a sufficient number of private parking spaces.
The restaurant is a recipient of the Golden Crown award for national cuisine and is a member of the IHRA organization. It has built a strong reputation as one of the best restaurants in the region, having hosted everyone from local officials and diplomats to international guests.
Its offer includes traditional national dishes as well as international cuisine, cocktails, various celebrations, and catering services for both small and large groups of guests.
