On Saturday 24 October 2026 the organiser asked, verbatim:

> What is the next post I share on social media?

The agent answered from the drumbeat pages: week 2, "Run a session: the
call is open", dated Tuesday 20 October 2026 and still marked drafted.
The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Didn't go out. I want to post on Saturday each week. Do you need to update the post?

The agent checked the week 2 text against the call page and found nothing
to change. It said the calendar would need re-dating, and that the 33
weekly posts would not fit the 32 Saturdays before the event: "See you
Thursday" would land on Saturday 5 June. The organiser then wrote,
verbatim:

The agent found that every post image is published at
goatconf.com/share/<name>.png but that no page links the general post
images (only the speaker, supporter and event cards are linked from their
own pages), and GitHub Pages does not list a folder. It offered links in
the drumbeat pages or a share page on the site, and recommended the
links. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Idk, we can plan to post 2 times a week too. Tuesday and Saturday

The agent laid out three ways: Saturdays only; Tuesdays and Saturdays
with about 31 new posts drafted now; or its recommendation, the existing
plan on Saturdays and a Tuesday slot for news (a confirmed speaker, a new
supporter, an accepted event) and, when there is no news, a short Tuzla
post from what the brain already holds, drafted a month at a time. With
Tuesdays in use, "See you Thursday" goes back to Tuesday 1 June 2027 and
the thank-you stays on Tuesday 8 June 2027. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> go with your recommendation

While the agent was updating the pages, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I posted the second week thing btw
