The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok. I want to add Wroclowe rb as one of the supporters. They are a community partner and provide support with social media presence

wroclove.rb is the Ruby conference in Wrocław, Poland
(sources/web/supporters/wroclove-rb-about-2026-10-21.md). The agent asked
four questions, and the organiser chose:

- **The name:** "wroclove.rb", lowercase, as the conference writes it on
  its own site.
- **The supporter line:** "Community partner: social media". Since that
  line does not start with what they do, their thank-you card gets its
  own last line: "for being a community partner and sharing GoatConf on
  social media."
- **Their profile:** "Draft from their site". Two sentences on who they
  are and links on how to support them, written from wrocloverb.com, for
  the organiser to check before it goes live.
- **Where they show:** "In the same list". wroclove.rb is listed by name
  with the other supporters, IPI Academy still first, with no new section
  and no change to how the site works.

What this means: a community partner that gives in kind, here by sharing
GoatConf on its social media, is listed as a supporter the same way, with
its logo and link. No money goes through the organiser, so there is
nothing to invoice. Being listed buys nothing: no talk, no stage time, no
say over the program and nothing at GoatConf's venue. The logo is the one
wroclove.rb shows on its own site
(sources/web/supporters/wroclove-rb-logo-2026-10-21.md).
