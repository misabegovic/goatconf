Seats

Talks run in all seven rooms at once. Rooms have from 13 to about 120 seats. Closer to June, each session gets its own sign-up in the GoatConf app, which saves you a seat up to the room's size and lets you build your own agenda. We will send everyone with a ticket the link. Empty seats go to whoever is at the door when a session starts. All rooms keep the same times, so you can change rooms at every break.

Using a wheelchair, deaf or hard of hearing, blind or with low vision? See accessibility for what to expect.

How it works

Registration runs on Luma. The button opens it over this page. It asks only for your name and email address. Luma then emails you a confirmation with a calendar invite. Registering for GoatConf gets you your ticket and tells us how many people to expect. By registering you accept the code of conduct.

Your data

Luma (luma.com) stores your registration for the organiser, under its own privacy policy. The organiser, Muhamed Isabegović, keeps your name and email, and uses them only to run GoatConf 2027 and to write to you about it, because you agreed. They are deleted after the feedback survey goes out, and never shared or sold. You can ask to see, correct or delete them through the contact form or at info@muhamed.at.
