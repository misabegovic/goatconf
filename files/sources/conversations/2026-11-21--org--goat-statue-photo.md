On Saturday 21 November 2026, told that the week 6 post "Why a goat" was the one to go out that day, the organiser asked, verbatim:

> Should we use an image of the Tuzla goat statue

Offered the painted sticker of the statue, they wrote, verbatim:

> No, I mean the real statue

The agent found no usable photo: the brain lists the statue as its first image gap, Wikimedia Commons has none, and the tourist board's city guide names "Tuzla's goat" as a photo stop without an image that can be reused. The organiser then shared a photo of the statue: the goat standing on a stone plinth on a lawn under trees, in summer, a parked car behind.

Asked where the photo is from and whether GoatConf can post it, they chose "I took it, no credit", described as: it's your photo; use it freely with no credit on the card. Asked whether it replaces today's card or goes in a later post, they chose "Today's card (Recommended)", described as: remake the "Why a goat" square and wide cards with the statue photo, and add a line to the text that the statue stands in front of Hotel Tuzla.
