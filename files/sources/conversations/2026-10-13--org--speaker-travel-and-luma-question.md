The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> for the luma registration, can we have a text field that's optional where folks can explain why they want to join or something? something we can validate against

and, verbatim:

> on the form to apply as a speaker, maybe we should add "I dont need travel support" and "My company will cover my travel" with a note that they will receive a right to be placed on the website as a supporter of the conference then

Asked three questions, the organiser chose:

- How a speaker's company that covers their travel is listed: "Same as online supporters", that is, listed like the 500 euro online supporters, with no distinction.
- A third travel choice for speakers who pay their own way: "Yes, add it (Recommended)".
- The Luma question: "Why do you want to come? (Recommended)".

Done on 2026-10-13:

- The talk form's travel question, which still asked "If a supporter funds travel grants, would you like one?", now reads "Your travel. Speakers pay their own travel. If the budget allows, the organiser may refund some or all of it after the event; this is not promised. Your answer never affects whether your session is picked." Its optional choices are "I don't need travel support", "My company will cover my travel, and is then listed on goatconf.com as a supporter of GoatConf", and "I'll pay my own travel, and would welcome a refund if the budget allows".
- The Luma event has one optional, multi-line custom question: "Why do you want to come to GoatConf? (optional) A line or two about you and what you build helps the organiser confirm registrations."
- The register, talk and supporters pages say so.
