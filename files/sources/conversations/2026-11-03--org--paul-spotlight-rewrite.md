On Tuesday 3 November 2026, the day Paul Campbell's spotlight is due
(sources/conversations/2026-11-02--org--speakers-from-tomorrow.md), the
organiser asked, verbatim:

> If we learn more about Paul and what he's going to talk about, can we maybe write a better post?

The agent pointed out that the spotlight was drafted on 2026-10-14, before
his session, "The story of CARLOS", was known on 2026-10-26
(sources/conversations/2026-10-26--org--paul-campbell-carlos.md), and
offered a version naming the talk. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Maybe rewrite again from scratch and learn more about Paul before. Goal is to sell the conference

The agent read his site, his posts on CARLOS, the CARLOS site, This Next
Thing, HalfStack Vienna, RubyEvents and two podcast pages
(sources/web/speakers/paul-campbell-carlos-and-events-2026-11-03.md), and
rewrote the spotlight around three reasons to come: Tito, which he
co-founded, has processed over $1 billion in tickets for thousands of
organisers; he has run conferences people remember (Funconf, Úll, This
Next Thing); and his talk, on why software stays slow while computers get
faster and how AI agents rebuilt Tito on CARLOS, with Tito's own figures.
