Automated waiting list
Your event is very popular and you always have a full house? That's great! But what about those
last-minute refund or cancellation requests, isn't it sad if those seats stay empty?
As soon as your event is sold out, our waiting list allows visitory of the shop to
register their interest in a ticket. When a ticket becomes available again (e.g. due to expiry
of a payment term, cancellation, etc.), the first person on the waiting list will have the option to
buy the ticket within two days. After that deadline passes, the option will be given to the next
person on the list and so on.
Of course, this is optional. You can also switch it to manual mode and decide yourself when and who
from the list gets a ticket.
