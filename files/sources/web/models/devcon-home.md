Are there discounts for Indian residents?[]
Yes — we have limited discounted tickets reserved for Indian residents. You can verify your residency using our ticket shop with your Aadhaar Card via Self.
Are there group or team discounts available?[]
While we do not offer group or team tickets, we offer plenty of discounts to individual applicants. You may also consider becoming a Supporter in our Supporter Program.
Will there be opportunities to obtain discounted tickets?[]
Yes! There will be multiple ways to obtain discounted tickets this year:
-   Local Discounts — We will have limited tickets reserved for Indian residents.
-   Community Discounts — This will consist of groups like Protocol Guild members, OSS Contributors and more and can be independently claimed.
-   Applications — This will include Sanctuary Tech Builder Discounts, Student Discounts, and Youth Tickets for those ages 3-17.
