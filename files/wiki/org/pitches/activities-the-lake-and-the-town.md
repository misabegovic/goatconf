# Activities: the salt lake, a town-wide hunt, and a cheap city as the gift


> **Bet on and graduated, 2026-10-07.** The organiser bet on this pitch; it continues as [activities-lake-and-town.md](../prds/activities-lake-and-town.md). This page is kept as the pre-bet record.

Area pitch under [One goat feeds the town](one-goat-minimal-core-and-extensions.md).
Pre-bet, shaped in auto mode on 2026-10-07.

## Problem

The organiser named two assets: "The salt lake in Tuzla is an attraction,
Tuzla itself is a cheap city and I want to make benefit of that for the
conference", and the goal of people who "hang out, spend time together"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`). The
guide agrees that this is what people remember: "The best thing you can
add to a conference is not a talk. It is time and structure for people to
actually experience things together", and post-conference activities work
"because people have already broken the ice"
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Beyond the talks). It cites the organiser's own "city-wide treasure
hunt throughout Vienna during RubyConf Austria" (same section).

But activities cost money and organising time, and the core has neither
to spare. They have to happen without the organiser paying for or running
all of them.

## Appetite

**Small for the core** (a list, a map, one suggested day), with the
activities themselves as extensions run by hosts and paid for by the
people who join them.

## Solution

### The cheap city is the activity

The most generous thing GoatConf can give is information: what things
cost, published with a "last checked" date, as Bansko Nomad Fest does
with its cost table (`sources/web/models/bansko-nomad-fest-life-in-bansko.md`).
An inexpensive meal is about 4.60 EUR and a cappuccino about 1.66 EUR in
Tuzla (`sources/web/tuzla/numbeo-cost-of-living-tuzla.md`). The open
lunch, the evening socials and the lake day are cheap enough to be
everyone's, and the travel page says so in numbers ([Tuzla](../tuzla.md)).

### The breaks are an activity too

On the lecture day, coffee and lunch are one to two hours each, on the
Freedom Square, under five minutes from IPI (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`).
They are where most of the hanging out happens, at the attendees' own
small cost. A short list of cafés and places to eat around the square,
with prices and a "last checked" date, is the only thing the core adds.

### The lake day (the day after the core)

- **The Pannonian Lakes** are in the city centre: three salt-water lakes
  with salt waterfalls, an archaeological park and slides, reached by a
  tourist train from Freedom Square
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-pannonian-lakes.md`,
  `tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`). An adult ticket cost 7 KM on weekdays
  and 9 KM at weekends in peak 2026, about 3.60 and 4.60 EUR
  (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-cijene-kurir-2026-08.md`). Each person
  pays their own; a supporter could pay for everyone as a support
  extension.
- **Slana banja park** next door has paths, courts, cafés and the Peace
  Flame House (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-salty-spa-park.md`,
  `sources/web/venue/tztz-peace-flame-house.md`): a place for the
  unstructured "hang out" the organiser asked for, swimming or not.
- **A rain plan:** June is Tuzla's wettest month
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia.md`). The park cafés and an indoor
  fringe event carry the day if it rains.

### A town-wide goat hunt (the day before or after the core)

The organiser's signature from RubyConf Austria, brought home: a treasure
hunt through Tuzla whose clues tell the goat story, the man who hid his
goat when Austria-Hungary banned them and the saying "The whole of Tuzla
milked one goat" (`sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-why-the-goat.md`),
ending at the goat statue the same page mentions. Teams mix strangers,
which is the point. It can be run by the organiser or handed to a host
with the clues.

### Everything else is an extension slot

Anyone can add an activity through the umbrella's extension record: a
city walk, a day trip, a hike, a karaoke night, a run along the lakes. The
organiser's travel guide Usput.ba already publishes places and itineraries
for Bosnia and Herzegovina built from real place data
(`sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-about.md`), a ready source for the
city walk and for attendees who stay longer. Tuzla's own June events
(the lakes' season opening, Astronomy Days, Summer at Pannonica) can be
listed as things to do if their 2027 dates fall that week
(`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`).

## Rabbit holes

- **Organising tourism.** Group bookings, buses, guides: each adds money
  and liability. The core lists; hosts run.
- **Safety at the water.** Swimming is at the lake operator's facility,
  under its rules; GoatConf does not run swimming.
- **Usput.ba as promotion.** It is the organiser's own product; linking
  to it is useful and should be said plainly as theirs.

## No-gos

- No activity paid for from the organiser's 1,000 EUR.
- No organised transport or group bookings in the core.
- No activity that clashes with the core day's program.

## Choices made in auto mode

- **The lake day after the core.** Because the guide's post-conference
  timing works best; which day that is depends on the date option IPI
  picks (see the [dates pitch](dates-format-and-schedule.md)).
- **The goat hunt as the one signature activity.** Because it ties the
  name story to the place, the organiser has run one before, and it costs
  almost nothing.
- **Prices published as part of the core.** Because "cheap city" only
  helps if people know the numbers before they book.

## Open questions for the organiser

- Do you want to run the goat hunt yourself, or hand it to a host?
- May the travel page link to Usput.ba's Tuzla content?
- Is there a local partner (the tourist board, the lakes' operator) worth
  asking for a group rate?
