After Predrag Radenkovic was added
(sources/conversations/2026-10-23--org--predrag-radenkovic.md), the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I also think we need to have a view all at the bottom of the speakers section on the homepage...
> Don't assign time slots to anyone specific. We will build the program closer to the event
> I also want to keep the "your session here" entry
> Let's also add *codeplain as one of the supporters, for covering Predrag's travel costs

Asked four questions, the organiser answered:

- **Reserved cells:** "We can keep that, but treat it like a general
  reservation that might go to any of the speakers. Not for any specific".
  The eight reserved main-hall cells stay; none is any one speaker's. The
  program builds closer to the event. Friday 15:30, added the same day, is
  the eighth such cell, not Predrag's.
- **The home page's speakers section:** "8 speakers + sticker". It shows
  the first eight speakers and then always one "Your session here?"
  sticker, which had dropped off when the eighth speaker filled the last
  place.
- **The link under it:** "View all speakers (Recommended)", always, in
  place of "Meet the speakers" or "All N speakers".
- **The supporter:** "Yes, like Meister (Recommended)": *codeplain is
  listed as a supporter for Predrag Radenkovic's travel, with its logo
  and a short description from its own site, and links to try it and to
  its blog. Its careers page has no open positions, so no jobs link
  (sources/web/supporters/codeplain-logo-and-site-2026-10-23.md).
