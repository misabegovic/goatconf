Badge scanning and sponsor interactions
Your badge includes a unique barcode. If you choose to allow a sponsor to scan your badge, you consent to LeadDev sharing your professional and contact details (as described in our Privacy Policy) with that sponsor. Sponsors become independent controllers of that data and may contact you according to their own privacy notices.
Choosing not to have your badge scanned will not affect your participation in the event.
See the Event Privacy Summary below for more information.

Sharing data with event sponsors
Your event badge will contain a unique barcode. Sponsors may ask to scan your badge so that they can contact you after the event.
You may also choose to participate in certain sponsored activities via the Event App, for example a sponsored workshop or hands-on lab. Sponsored activities will be clearly marked in the Event App with a notice that you consent to your data being shared with the sponsor by registering for the activity.
