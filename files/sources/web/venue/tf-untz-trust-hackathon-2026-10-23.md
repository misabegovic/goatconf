Realiziran prvi hakaton na Tehnološkom fakultetu Univerziteta u Tuzli u sklopu Erasmus+ TRUST projekta
Realiziran prvi hakaton na Tehnološkom fakultetu Univerziteta u Tuzli u sklopu Erasmus+ TRUST projekta
Datum objave/izmjene: 19/03/2026. godine
Na Tehnološkom fakultetu Univerziteta u Tuzli, u INTERBA laboratoriji-Računarskom centru, od 13. do 15. marta 2026. godine, održan je prvi od četiri hakatona u okviru Erasmus+ TRUST projekta Triggering Resilience in Up-and-coming Security Threats.
Ovaj događaj označava početak serije od četiri hakatona širom Zapadnog Balkana. U Tuzli je 16 odabranih studenata formiralo transdisciplinarne timove, predstavljajući pet partnerskih visokoškolskih ustanova iz Beograda, Tuzle, Podgorice, Banja Luke i Bijeljine.
Cilj hackathona bio je podstaći razmjenu znanja i stvaranje inovativnih rješenja za savremene sigurnosne izazove. Događaj u Tuzli postavlja temelje za predstojeće aktivnosti i organizaciju drugih hakatona u Bijeljini, Banjoj Luci i Podgorici.
Ponosni smo na našu ulogu u Erasmus+ programu i TRUST projektu, kao i na naš zajednički doprinos jačanju regionalne otpornosti na razne izazove.
Više informacija o hakatonu možete pogledati na sljedećem web linku: https://youtu.be/LaS3esaXZKA kao i web stranici TRUST projekta https://sites.google.com/untz.ba/trust-project/acitivities.
The first hackathon was realised at the Faculty of Technology, University of Tuzla, as part of the Erasmus+ TRUST project.
At the Faculty of Technology, University of Tuzla, in the INTERBA Laboratory-Computer Centre, from March 13 to 15, 2026, the first of four hackathons within the Erasmus+ TRUST project Triggering Resilience in Up-and-coming Security Threats was realised.
This event marks the beginning of a series of four hackathons across the Western Balkans. In Tuzla, 16 selected students formed transdisciplinary teams, representing five partner higher education institutions from Belgrade, Tuzla, Podgorica, Banja Luka, and Bijeljina.
The aim of the hackathon was to foster knowledge exchange and drive the creation of innovative solutions to contemporary security challenges. The event in Tuzla lays the foundations for the upcoming activities and organisation of other hackathons in Bijeljina, Banja Luka and Podgorica.
We are proud of our role in the Erasmus+ programme and the TRUST project, as well as our collective contribution to strengthening regional resilience against various challenges.
More information about the hackathon can be found at the following web link:
https://youtu.be/LaS3esaXZKA as well as the web page of the TRUST project: https://sites.google.com/untz.ba/trust-project/acitivities.
