Translation (not verbatim): on Friday 31 May 2024 a festival karaoke party ran at Bluesberry bar from 21:30 to midnight; on Saturday 1 June a board-game session with snacks ran at the Dom mladih chamber stage and a disco party at Caffe bar Jazz; on Sunday 2 June Bluesberry hosted a vegan grill, a craft bazaar and a concert. This is the only dated karaoke night at a Tuzla bar found in this research.
