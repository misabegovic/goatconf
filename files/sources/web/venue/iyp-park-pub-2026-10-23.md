Park Pub
Đorđa Mihajlovića 4, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
The clue is in the name. Park Pub is a pub located in the park (obviously), although dismissing it as that alone would be doing one of Tuzla’s finest a total disservice. This is a dark British-style boozer covered in flags of nations and football clubs alike, the perfect place to sink a few beers or something a little stronger. Live music events are as frequent as the televised sport, giving you plenty of reasons to visit the pub in the park.
