# GoatConf 2027: what is known

The facts the rest of the brain builds on, as stated by the organiser on
2026-10-07. Everything here is cited to the two conversation sources
above; nothing is inferred. Proposals live in the
[pitches](pitches/index.md), not here.

## The event

- **Name:** GoatConf.
- **Organiser:** [Muhamed Isabegovic](organiser.md), who founded and
  organised EuRuKo 2024 and RubyConf Austria and studied at IPI Akademija
  Tuzla.
- **Place:** Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
- **Host and partner:** [IPI Academy](ipi-academy.md), which has agreed;
  it is the venue "for now". Further venues are possible and undecided.
- **When:** Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June 2027 are the two core days, and
  Saturday 5 June is free for the community and the lakes
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--two-core-days-and-saturday.md`,
  approved in `sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--goatconf-ba-program-forms-approved.md`). IPI Academy has accepted
  these dates, as the organiser reported (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--ipi-accepted-dates-all-rooms.md`).
- **Length:** two core days in all seven IPI rooms in parallel, which IPI
  has made available, each with four one-hour slots and an hour off after
  each of the first three. Thursday runs 10:00 to 17:00 with a 09:45
  welcome. Friday runs 09:30 to 16:30 with a 09:15 welcome, so the hour off
  at 12:30 covers Džuma prayer (podne 12:44) and lunch
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--friday-dzuma-schedule.md`, `sources/web/tuzla/vaktija-tuzla-2027-06-03-04.md`)
  ([Dates: two core days](adrs/two-core-days-first-week.md),
  [Core days: parallel rooms](adrs/core-day-parallel-tracks.md)).
- **Language:** English.
- **Audience:** open and multi-discipline: engineers, designers, product
  people, founders: people who build and sell software (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--build-and-sell.md,
  sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--audience-list.md).
  Not tied to any language or stack, Ruby included (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--what-goatconf-is.md).
- **Size:** 120 to 300 people for the first edition, as the home page says (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--outside-review-fixes.md). Registration is one
  free pass per core day; each room is capped by its own seats at the
  door ([Website: goatconf.com and Google Forms](adrs/goatconf-ba-and-google-forms.md)).
- **Venue facts:** the hall has a projector, sound and Wi-Fi, no
  microphone and no recording; several other rooms; step-free to the
  main hall and first floor; Saturday or weekday both possible.
- **Recording:** GoatConf does not record the talks. People who come may
  bring a camera or a podcast and record, and a supporter may pay for a crew.
  Podcasts are welcome in some rooms, not yet named (`sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--recording-and-podcasts.md`).
- **Price:** free to attend. Registration is probably required ("if
  permitted, something like that").
- **Formats named:** talks and workshops, with time to hang out.
- **The day's rhythm:** not a crammed schedule. IPI is used only for
  lectures; a one-hour break follows every slot, on Freedom Square,
  under five minutes from IPI.

## The team and the site

- **No co-organiser:** the organiser runs it from Vienna with volunteers,
  IPI's students and people from the community.
- **Talks:** the keynotes fill four one-hour slots in every room on both
  core days, from an open call the organiser judges, filled as proposals
  arrive ([The call](adrs/call-organiser-judges.md)). Everyone who proposes hears back within three weeks (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--outside-review-fixes.md).
- **Code-of-conduct contacts:** the organiser and a staff member from IPI.
- **Registration, the call for talks, additions and contact** are Google
  Forms, created by a script kept in this repository.
- **Registration:** on Luma, with no limit; the organiser approves each registration (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--approval-back-on.md). The register page says the organiser confirms each registration, usually within a few days (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--outside-review-fixes.md). Closer to June, ticket holders also register for the sessions they want; how is not decided yet, and seats nobody registered for go to whoever is at the door (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--session-registration-later.md).
- **Contact:** info@muhamed.at, next to the contact form (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--contact-email.md).
- **The website** is goatconf.com, with the public budget ledger as one of
  its pages, written and designed in this repository and published on
  GitHub Pages by one Actions workflow, from every merge to `main` ([Website: goatconf.com and Google Forms](adrs/goatconf-ba-and-google-forms.md);
  source: sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--public-repo-pages-goatconf-com.md).

## The money

- The organiser's own money is capped at **1,000 EUR**, and it is a
  fallback only: "Just in case there are any expenses I need to cover.
  Normally, I pay for nothing also." It is never spent on food or drink,
  for anyone: others cover it, or people pay their own.
- **Online supporters** pay the organiser's Austrian business 500 EUR,
  plus VAT where it applies, for online perks only; the money pays event
  costs. Others may pay a supplier directly or give in kind. This replaced
  "No money goes through the organiser" on 2026-10-13 (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--online-supporters-through-the-business.md).
- **The budget is private** and not mentioned on the site (same source).
- **Speakers pay their own travel.** If the budget allows, the organiser
  may refund some or all of it; never promised (same source).
- Expo, booths and companies that hire attendees are open ideas, named as
  the likely extensible part and as a way companies could support the
  event.

## The intent

The goal, in the organiser's words: "get great minds together in an
affordable environment, hang out, spend time together, learn, share
experiences". Tuzla being inexpensive is to be used as a benefit, and its
salt lake is an attraction.

The shape is **minimal and extendable, like the Pi agent**: the organiser
provides the minimum a conference needs, others extend it, and the
extension rules fit the event. The event comes first; the rules are
written so others can reuse the kit later.

## Now, perceived, target

- **Now (2026-10-07):** an organiser with two conferences behind them, a host partner that is their own school, a city, a two-week window, a
  budget ceiling and an idea. No date, website, CFP, registration,
  brand or sponsor exists yet.
- **Perceived:** (unknown, needs source) what IPI Academy expects from
  the partnership, and what it provides beyond rooms, is not recorded.
- **Target:** a free GoatConf in June 2027 for 120 to 300 people, run on
  a minimal core others extend, with the kit reusable afterwards.

## Open questions

- Hotel room blocks for attendees, which IPI was clarifying after the
  June visit.
- What IPI Academy provides (rooms, capacity, equipment, students,
  staff time) and what it asks in return.
- Whether speakers get a contribution, and from whose money.
