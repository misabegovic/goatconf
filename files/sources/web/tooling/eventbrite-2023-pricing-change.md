Upcoming Changes to Eventbrite’s Pricing
Thank you for being a part of Eventbrite's mission to bring the world together through live experiences. We continue to evolve as a marketplace to help you reach new audiences and grow your business.
As part of this evolution,
we're introducing new plans for publishing events on Eventbrite
.
What’s changing?
Starting
September 6th, 2023
, we’ll begin rolling out new plans, Eventbrite Flex and Eventbrite Pro, for publishing events.
For events with fewer than 25 tickets, Organizers will continue to be able to publish their events for free.
For events larger than 25 tickets, Organizers must select a paid plan (Flex or Pro) with varying prices depending on the size and frequency of your events.
All plans now include access to Eventbrite's full suite of marketing tools to help you promote your event and sell more tickets.
These plans apply to both free and paid ticket events and are in addition to our existing
Ticketing Fees
, typically paid by attendees.
We are also discontinuing our Essentials Plan. Organizers on this plan will have their Ticketing Fees updated and gain immediate access to previously restricted tools (more details below).
New Plans: Eventbrite Flex and Pro
Our two new plans, Flex and Pro, enable you to pay as you grow based on your event size and frequency.
Flex: Pay As You Publish
With the Flex Plan, you'll pay per event based on the number of tickets you want to offer. This plan is perfect if you only host events occasionally or are just starting out.
