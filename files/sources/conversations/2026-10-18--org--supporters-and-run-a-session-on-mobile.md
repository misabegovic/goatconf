The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Let's optimize the supporters section for mobile too, also the Run a session section

At 390 pixels wide, the home page's "Support GoatConf" section listed its
three supporters in two columns. The chips were uneven heights, and
Meister sat alone on its own row. In "Run a session", each of the four
format slots (Talk, Panel, Workshop, Something else) was mostly empty
space reserved for its "Yours?" stamp. The steps said "through the short
form", but the section had no link to the form.

Asked to choose, the organiser picked:

- Supporters: one by one, with the logo on top.
- Session formats: one by one, compact.
- Add a "Propose a session" button.

Built as:

- On phones (below 48rem), the supporters stack in one column, at most
  26rem wide and centred. Each logo sits on top in a wide 5:2 box with
  the same padding, and what they support is written below it.
- On phones, the four formats stack in one column. Each has its number
  on the left, the name and description beside it, and the "Yours?"
  stamp under the words on the right, so nothing overlaps at 320 pixels.
  The faint goat drawing is hidden there.
- On every screen size, a "Propose a session" button follows the steps.
  It opens the same session form as talk.html.

Tablets and desktop keep their grids. No other words changed.
