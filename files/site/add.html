Propose an event

A workshop, a meetup, a hackathon, a dinner, a walk, or support for one named thing. You run it and pay for it; if the organiser accepts it, it becomes part of GoatConf 2027.

Propose your eventOpen now. It takes about ten minutes.

What you can add

  • An evening event around the talk days, such as a dinner or a party.
  • A community trip: take a group out of Tuzla for a day.
  • A meetup for your community, your language or your stack, in a café or a room.
  • A live podcast: record an episode in front of the people who come. Podcasts are welcome in some of the rooms. GoatConf does not record the talks itself, so a camera crew, paid for by a supporter, is welcome too.
  • A hike in the hills around Tuzla.
  • A hackathon: you, your group or your company organise all of it: the room, the theme, the judging, any prizes and their costs, paid directly to suppliers. It follows the same rules as everything else, and may not collect attendees' data or recruit beyond what people opt into.
  • Karaoke: book a bar or a room, bring the songs, and sing with the people who come.

More than one of each is welcome: a second meetup, another hike, a dinner every night. Propose as many as you like.

If you or your organisation fund and run it, you can also be listed as a supporter.

Want to support GoatConf with money or something in kind instead? See the supporters page.

The rules

Not everything people propose becomes part of GoatConf. The organiser accepts what follows the code of conduct and the charter and fits the program: its time, its place, who it is for and how it sits next to the talks. Only accepted events are part of GoatConf 2027, listed on the site and shared with the people who come. You run it and you pay for it, and its name says who runs it, for example "Rust night, part of GoatConf 2027". It takes place between about 25 May and 20 June 2027; if not, say why in the description. Anything not accepted may still happen, but not under GoatConf's name.

What happens next

  1. You fill in the form: the name, the kind, the host, where, when, the cost, how people join, who pays, a public contact and a short description.
  2. The organiser decides whether it becomes part of GoatConf, may ask for changes, and writes back either way.
  3. If accepted, it goes on the site, on the page Added by the people who come. If it is between 2 and 6 June, it also goes on the program.
