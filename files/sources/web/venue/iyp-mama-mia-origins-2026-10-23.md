There is something un-Bosnian about MamaMia Origins. Maybe it is the contemporary dishes that fill the menu, the undeniably modern sheen to it all, or maybe even just the portion sizes. We certainly aren’t complaining, as this brilliant restaurant on Trg Slobode might just be the best restaurant in town. The prices correspond with that, although it is important to remember that you’re getting some seriously good food for a fraction of the price you’ll find similar things in more celebrated cities. Embrace, indulge, enjoy.
