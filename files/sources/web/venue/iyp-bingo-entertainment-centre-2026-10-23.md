It is an IYP tradition to seek out a bowling alley in every single city we cover (or at least it should be). Looking for lanes in Tuzla? The Bingo City Center shopping mall has you covered, and even provides pool tables and table football for those who aren’t ready for the mighty pressure found on the lanes. More strikes, turkeys, spares and gutterballs than endless reruns of King Pin, and a great way to spend a couple of hours in Tuzla.
