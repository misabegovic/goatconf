## https://ti.to/about

Team Tito is a software company based in Dublin, Ireland, with team members in Ireland and the UK.
Founded by Paul and Doc, we started by building Tito, a growing platform and a sustainable business which has proudly processed over $1 billion worth of tickets for thousands of organisers worldwide. We have received funding support from Enterprise Ireland and investment from a number of private individuals.
Our team
Right now we're a team of six and believe great things can be done with a small team.

## https://blog.tito.io/posts/a-new-chapter/ ("A new chapter", 21 April 2026, by Paul Campbell)

In 2025, Team Tito Limited, the company, turned 10, which is a pretty good milestone to reach.
In 2023, I started work on "Tito 2", which was really an effort to to produce an app that wasn't burdened by what was (even then) over 10 years of legacy code.
There was potential to improve the Ruby on Rails codebase, the HTML rendered server-side and (less-of) that rendered client-side, the CSS and all of its framework dependencies, and the JavaScript powering the app. If the last 10 years were defined by "more JavaScript", then my plan with "Tito 2" was certainly "less JavaScript".
In any case, Tito 2 became "IO", a semi-joke name that played on the fact that our domain name was "tito dot io", that it was an invitation-only app for invitation-only events.
We also changed the name of "IO" to "Tito Pro". Tito Pro is a name that much clearer states our intent for the app.
