Located at the third Panonian Lake, ‘Restaurant Panonski Lovac‘ offers a rich gastronomic selection, a beautifully designed interior, parking facilities, and two summer terraces.
The restaurant also hosts a variety of events and celebrations, including New Year’s Eve parties, birthdays, weddings, graduation celebrations, International Women’s Day (March 8th), and all other types of gatherings and festivities, accompanied by live music.
