BIT Centar was established in 2005 with the primary objective of providing support and fostering the growth of SMEs in ICT industry.
BIT Center is a Science and Technology Park with its own infrastructure of about 3,300 square meters. Inside BIT Center there are 24 companies with a total of 250 employees, 70% are engineers, 10% are doctors and masters and the rest are final year students of the faculty. We are located in the city center and near the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, which is integrated into the infrastructure in various way.
Today inside BIT Center is: BIT incubator, BIT training center, BIT R&D lab. Further more since 2018, BIT has established the CODEHub coworking space, for freelancers, students, visitors and business professionals.
Coworking space: BIT operates CODEHub Tuzla which is one of the four hubs established through the CODE project. Hub can be modernized and upgraded to provide enhanced support to the regional IT ecosystem. Hub is a meeting place for industry professionals and place for meetups, trainings and conferences to foster knowledge exchange and collaboration.
Additionally, BIT has technical capacities including infrastructure and equipment to support the implementation of training programs, innovation labs and activities in coworking spaces (meetups and events).
