date: 2026-10-19
kind: conversation
---

After the talk page and the proposal form named five areas (engineering,
design and product, research, the business of software, the people side
of the work), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Nice. I was thinking that maybe we should have a nice way to show flavors of sessions on the site and who it is for. Since folks write in their proposals who it is for

Offered three ways, they picked "Tags and a \"For\" line": each session
gets small tags for its format and area, and a "For:" line in the
speaker's own words, on the speaker page, the speakers list and the
program, with the talk page explaining the areas. Not picked: filters on
the program and speakers pages, and grouping the speakers by area.

Asked whether the "For" line keeps the speaker's words exactly or a short
edit of them, they picked "Their words, lightly trimmed": each answer cut
to one short line without changing what it says, the full answer kept in
the proposal.

The two sessions with a title so far, from their proposals:

- Dejan Menges, discipline Engineering, "Who is it for?": "Anyone working
  with real life codebases" (sources/cfp/2026-10-17--dejan-menges--teaching-twenty-languages.md),
  trimmed to "anyone working with real-life codebases".
- Christoph Lipautz, discipline Engineering, "Who is it for?": "Every
  engineer, the talk will be kept inclusive so it speaks to all levels of
  experience." (sources/cfp/2026-10-15--christoph-lipautz--back-to-the-future.md),
  trimmed to "every engineer, at any level of experience".
