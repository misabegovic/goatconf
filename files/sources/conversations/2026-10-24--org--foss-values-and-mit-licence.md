---
title: "FOSS values on the charter, and an MIT licence for the repository"
date: 2026-10-24
kind: conversation
---

On Saturday 24 October 2026 the organiser asked, verbatim:

> Should we mention somewhere that this is a FOSS inspired conference? Built around those values?

The agent found that no page said so; the charter's only mention was "It
follows the spirit of the FOSDEM charter." It listed what is already
decided and published that matches those values: free to attend, a small
core the people who come extend, supporters who cannot buy content, an
open call, events proposed by pull request, open-licensed fonts, and the
site made in a public repository. It noted that the repository had no
licence and that no page linked to the source. It proposed the charter as
the best place, with a line on the home page, llms.txt and a footer link
as options, and asked four questions: the wording ("inspired by FOSS
values" or "an open-source conference"), which values, whether to add a
licence, and where to put it.

The organiser answered, verbatim:

> FOSS values, charter, add MIT licence

So: the wording is about FOSS values, not an open-source conference; it
goes on the charter only; and the repository gets the MIT licence.
