---
title: "Business, soft-skill and research sessions are welcome, for a technical audience that builds and sells software"
date: 2026-10-19
kind: conversation
---

A prospective speaker wrote to the organiser, who passed the message on
verbatim:

> Would you be open to some business talk? I’m not much of a developer nowadays 😅

The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Maybe we should make it clear that this conference also welcomes soft skill talks? Business talks from founders related to technology and software.

Shown three wordings, they answered, verbatim:

> I still want like useful experiences for someone in the audience to learn and be able to utilize to improve themselves, inspire and build on top.... Idk explore what makes sense for a builders/founders conference, come with suggestions and I can choose

The agent looked at how three conferences describe the sessions they want:
MicroConf asks for a story, a framework or advice
(https://microconf.com/pitches); Business of Software wants clear
takeaways from people who have done the work, and turns down product
launches and pitches
(https://businessofsoftware.org/2013/03/how-to-submit-a-killer-business-of-software-conference-speaker-proposal-and-how-to-guarantee-you-will-never-get-to-speak/);
LeadDev groups talks under team, tech, process and personal development
and asks for practical insights and honest lessons
(https://leaddev.com/get-involved/become-a-leaddev-speaker).

Offered four things the call could say, the organiser picked all four:

- name the topics: engineering, design and product, the business of
  software (founding a company, first customers, pricing, selling, hiring,
  funding or bootstrapping) and the people side of the work (leading
  teams, careers, communication, working with clients);
- set the bar: from the speaker's own work, something people can use or
  build on, a story, a framework or advice, with honest lessons including
  what did not work welcome;
- say what does not fit: a pitch for the speaker's product or company, or
  motivation with nothing to take home;
- one line: "You do not need to write code to speak here."

While the change was being made, the organiser added, verbatim:

> We should also open it up to folks to present their research. We need to keep the conference in the domain of those who build and sell software though. Technical conference. Content meant for technical people
