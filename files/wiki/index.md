# goatconf brain

The LLM-maintained knowledge base for goatconf, the conference: program,
speakers, sponsors, venue, attendees, budget and the decisions behind
them. It was scaffolded empty on 2026-10-07 and inherits its
machinery from claude-brain (see [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) § Lineage).
What is known is on [GoatConf 2027: what is known](org/goatconf-2027.md);
what is proposed is on the [pitches hub](org/pitches/index.md).

## What changed

- **2026-10-19**: **Business, people and research sessions are welcome, for a technical room.** The talk page now names research, the business of software and the people side of the work next to engineering, design and product, says no code is needed to speak but the room is technical, and asks for something people can use or build on, not a pitch; the home page, the "Not only engineers" card and the two drafted posts about the call say the same ([drumbeat 2026](org/outreach/drumbeat-2026.md), [drumbeat 2027](org/outreach/drumbeat-2027-jan-mar.md); source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--business-and-people-sessions.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **The page frame ADR is built.** All five steps landed (#125 to #129); the ADR's build notes list them ([the ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md); source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **The day times, typed once.** The core days' hours, welcome times and slot times now come only from the program's day plan: the program page's prose, the register page's day list and the talk page's slot times (as marked parts `tools/program.py` writes), llms.txt, the calendar file and the share cards follow it, and a test proves a changed day reaches all of them. The last step of the [page frame ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md) (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **One build, one marker style.** `tools/site.py build` writes the whole site in the order its generators depend on each other, and `site.py check` is what the local CI runs for every record, generated page and part, and the HTML. Every generated part of a page is marked `<!-- gen:NAME tools/TOOL.py -->`, naming the tool that writes it. The pages changed only in their markers. Step 4 of the [page frame ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md) (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **One page frame.** Every page's footer, the home page's back cover links and the goat sprite now come from `tools/frame.py`; the generators build their pages with it, the hand-written pages carry the footer as a marked block it writes, and the local CI checks it. The sprite sits only on the two pages that use it. Screenshots of every page at three widths match before and after. Step 3 of the [page frame ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md) (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **Shared helpers for the generators.** Reading records, frontmatter, escaping, and writing or checking built pages now live once in `tools/sitelib.py`, used by the program, events and speakers generators; every page they build is unchanged. Step 2 of the [page frame ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md) (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
- **2026-10-19**: **The local CI checks the site's HTML.** `tools/site_lint.py` reads every page and fails on unclosed tags, duplicate ids, links, images or anchors inside the site that do not resolve, images without alt text, and pages without one `h1`, one `main` and at most one banner; the site passes it. The first step of the [page frame ADR](org/adrs/one-page-frame-and-one-site-build.md) (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--site-frame-adr-approved.md).
