Shown PR #269, where on a phone the home page's speakers became the
faces strip and the food folded to four dishes
(sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-page-order.md), the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I don't like the speakers change. The way goats jumped through the site and followed the visitor I want to stay

The agent found that each of the eight speaker cards carries the
speaker's own peeking goat, which the companion goat visits, while the
faces strip carries none; and that all 12 dishes carry a painted goat
scene, 8 of them hidden by the fold until "Show all dishes" is tapped.
On a phone, 35 of the page's 51 goat stops were visible, against 51
before. Asked about the food, the organiser chose to unfold it too.

Built as: the home page shows the speaker cards and all 12 dishes on a
phone again, as before PR #269; the new order and the "Get your free
ticket" band stay. Every goat stop shows on a phone, and the phone page
is about 29,700 px.
