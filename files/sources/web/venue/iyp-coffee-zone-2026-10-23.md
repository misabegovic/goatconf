Coffee Zone
Fra Grge Martica 30 Donja, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
http://coffee-zone.weebly.com/
Tuzla’s most charming cafe, if you ask us. The decor gives the impression of a whimsical cafe by the sea, all pastel blue and nautical ornaments interspersed with mirrors, clocks and everything else, but Coffee Zone is far more light than these eccentric cafes tend to be. The coffee is the sort that will give you all the pep you’ll need for exploring Tuzla, and the sandwiches will do their bit. The summer garden is an added delight.
