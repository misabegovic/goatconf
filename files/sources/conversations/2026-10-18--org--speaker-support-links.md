After supporter profiles gained "Who they are" and "How to support
them", the organiser asked, verbatim:

> We should also have a who they are and how to support them part for each speaker, right?

The agent answered that speaker pages already say who they are, in the
bio each speaker wrote, and that "How to support them" was missing; the
links are new public content on a speaker's page, so each speaker should
agree to theirs. The organiser chose:

- **How:** "Draft links, they OK". The agent adds the field and the
  section, drafts each published speaker's links from sources, and drafts
  a note asking each to confirm or change them; nothing shows until
  approved.
- **The heading:** "\"Who they are\" everywhere". The speaker's bio
  section is headed "Who they are", like supporters', instead of "About
  <first name>".
- **Landing:** "Merge #83 first, then a new PR". PR #83 was merged before
  this work began.

The drafts are on `wiki/org/outreach/speaker-support-links.md`.
