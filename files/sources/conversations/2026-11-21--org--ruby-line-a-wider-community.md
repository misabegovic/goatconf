Shown the week 6 post, "Why a goat", the organiser quoted its sentence and asked, verbatim:

> GoatConf is not only about Ruby or any one stack: Ruby is welcome, and so is every other language and framework.
>
> Is this really the best wording?

Told that it opens with a negation and reads like a disclaimer after the story, that "not only about any one stack" is muddled, and that it says the same thing twice, they were offered three positive wordings that keep Ruby named, and the question of whether the change is for the post only or the home page too. They answered, verbatim:

> Option 1, post and home page

Option 1 joins the sentence before it: "It first appeared as the mascot of EuRuKo 2024, the first Ruby conference in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Now it feeds a wider community: every language and framework is welcome at GoatConf, Ruby included." It replaces the line of 2026-10-18 (sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--not-only-about-ruby.md) in the week 6 post and in the home page's "Why a goat" section. The short post for X and Bluesky does not mention Ruby and stays as it is.
