Park Pub
"The biggest choice of beers in Tuzla"
Park Pub is placed in the city core with a beautiful location-oriented on walking street and City Park.
Beer lovers will find beers from Budweiser, Erdinger, Guinness and offcourse Tuzlanski Pilsner.
Also, you will have a chance to try CRAFT BEER made by Park Pub.
This is one of the Pubs that make live music, watching football or basketball games.
Beautiful girls and nice guys... it depends on what you prefer are like a flower, beers are like water and a good atmosphere is a sun that makes this park SHINE.
Our advice is to try Tuzlanski Pilsner with a Czech recipe.
