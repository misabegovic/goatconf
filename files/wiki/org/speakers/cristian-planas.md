# Speaker: Cristian Planas

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-14 before his session
has a slot or a topic, and listed fifth, after Amar Mahmutbegović; he
agreed to a public profile and to promotion on social media (source:
sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--cristian-planas.md). His public
profile is the record `speakers/cristian-planas.md`, built into
`site/speaker-cristian-planas.html`. This page records only what the
sources say and what bears on GoatConf.

## Scope

- **Who:** "a Ruby developer, conference speaker, and author of Rails Scales!", with over 15 years of Rails, who "has helped build and scale large applications at Zendesk and now works at Combinaut, exploring the intersection of Ruby, testing, and AI-assisted development" (source: sources/web/speakers/kaigi-on-rails-2026-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md). Combinaut makes Sparkle, marketing and communication software for healthcare (same source).
- **Where he works, Now and Perceived:** his newest bio (Kaigi on Rails 2026) says Combinaut; his RubyEvents and Bluesky profiles still say Zendesk, where he was Group Tech Lead and Senior Staff Software Engineer (source: sources/web/speakers/kaigi-on-rails-2026-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md). The site follows the newest bio on the organiser's choice. His title at Combinaut is Principal Engineer, by his RubyConf India 2026 speaker card (sources: sources/web/speakers/rubyconf-india-2026-cristian-planas-2026-10-22.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-22--org--cristian-planas-principal-engineer.md).
- **Before Zendesk:** co-founder and CTO of Playfulbet; he has lived in Barcelona, Helsinki, Copenhagen, San Francisco and New York, and is based in Barcelona (source: sources/web/speakers/kaigi-on-rails-2026-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md).
- **The book:** "Rails Scales! Practical Techniques for Performance and Growth", Pragmatic Bookshelf, May 2025, 270 pages (source: sources/web/speakers/rails-scales-and-talks-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md).
- **Talks:** sixteen listed since 2022, among them RailsConf 2022, EuRuKo 2023, RubyKaigi 2025, Baltic Ruby 2025, Tropical on Rails 2025, RubyConf Taiwan with COSCUP 2025, RubyConf Africa 2026, RBQ Conf 2026 and Kaigi on Rails 2026. His themes are Rails performance and scale ("A Rails Performance Guidebook: from 0 to 1B requests/day", "The Three Villains of Rails Scalability"), the history of the Zendesk monolith, and, lately, safety nets for AI-written code ("The Revenge of the Cucumber") (source: sources/web/speakers/rails-scales-and-talks-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet. On 21 November 2026 he speaks at RubyConf India, at the Novotel Hyderabad Airport, on "Dead Cucumbers & Frozen Capybaras: Building the Safety Net for Agentic Development" (source: sources/web/speakers/rubyconf-india-2026-cristian-planas-2026-10-22.md).
- **Bio on the site:** his Kaigi on Rails 2026 bio, word for word (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--cristian-planas.md).
- **Role on the site and cards:** "Principal Engineer at Combinaut, author of Rails Scales!" since 2026-10-22, in place of "Author of Rails Scales!, at Combinaut" (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-22--org--cristian-planas-principal-engineer.md, sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--cristian-planas.md).
- **Photo:** his COSCUP 2025 speaker photo, on stage with a microphone; not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision (source: sources/web/speakers/rails-scales-and-talks-cristian-planas-2026-10-14.md).
