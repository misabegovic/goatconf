Asked whether to land the "Renting a car" block, which named no company on the organiser's earlier choice (sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--car-rentals.md), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Yes, but we should also maybe list these rental companies

Done on 2026-10-14: the travel page names, in alphabetical order and each linked to its own site, the 20 companies on Sarajevo airport's own rental list (sources/web/travel/car-rental-2026-10-14.md): 387 Rent a Car, Anatolia, AR Rent A Car, AutoWill, Avis, Carwiz, Duo, EG Rental Group, Enterprise (with Alamo and National), Euro BH, Europcar, Gallery, Green Light, Hyundai Rent-A-Car, Kapitals, OtoQ, Sixt, Sub Rosa (with Dollar and Thrifty), Trio Rent and X-Rental. For Tuzla airport, whose page names no company, it names Enterprise with Alamo, and Carwiz, which list an office there on their own sites.

Notes from the agent's reading of the list on 2026-10-14:

- The second entry on Sarajevo's list shows only a logo; the logo reads "Europcar" (its head office line, "Bulevar Meše Selimovića 16 - ASA", is Europcar's local operator's address).
- Names not printed on the list are taken from each entry's web address or email: Kapitals (kapitals.ba), Trio Rent (triorent.ba), Green Light (greenlightbih.com), OtoQ (otoq.rent), X-Rental (carvista.ba, named "X-Rental" on the list).
- Links checked on 2026-10-14: subrosa.hr now redirects to https://www.subrosa.rent/en/, duoent.ba to https://duorentals.com/bs, europcar.ba to https://www.europcar.com/en-ba; dollar.ba, thrifty.ba and carrentalsubrosa.com did not answer, so Sub Rosa is linked at subrosa.rent. Sixt's airport page refuses scripted requests but is its own published address.
