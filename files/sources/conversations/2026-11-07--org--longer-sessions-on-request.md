---

Looking at the redesigned talk page (PR #257), whose first sticker reads
"One hour", the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> We should maybe clarify for the One hour thing. Half an hour is ok also and for anything requiring more than an hour they can reach out and we can see if we can make it work

Until then only a workshop in one of the two computer centres could run
longer than an hour
(sources/conversations/2026-10-19--org--longer-workshops-in-the-computer-centres.md).
Asked how people should reach out, the organiser chose: in the proposal
("Say how long in your proposal, and we see if we can make it work"),
not through the contact page first.

Built as, on talk.html:

- The "One hour" sticker reads: "A room at IPI Academy, in your format.
  Half an hour works too. Need longer? Say so in your proposal, and we
  see if we can make it work."
- In "What a session can be", the "Half an hour" part becomes "Half an
  hour, or longer", adding: "If your session needs more than an hour,
  say how long in your proposal, and we see if we can make it work." The
  workshop rule for the computer centres stays as it was.
- "Your slot" adds "Need longer? Say so in your proposal." after the
  30-minute line.
- "What the form asks" gives needing more than an hour as an example of
  "anything that would help us make it work for you". The live form is
  unchanged.
