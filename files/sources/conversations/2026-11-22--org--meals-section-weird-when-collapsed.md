On 2026-11-22, about the live site, after the collapsible home page was
published
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--ship-the-collapsible-home-page.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> The meals section behaves weird when collapsed due to goats changing

Told how the closed food row would be fixed, the organiser wrote,
verbatim:

> Let's improve those aspects now for each section when collapsed vs opened

Read as: every section of the home page that opens and closes is to be
right in both states, the goats included. A closed row holds still:
nothing on it or below it moves when a goat arrives, leaves or changes. A
goat never covers a word, a link or a stamp. Opening and closing leaves no
goat stranded.

The same day an independent design review of the live site listed 31
faults (kept outside the repository, at
~/scratch/goatconf/shots/review15/REPORT.md). The ones about the sections'
closed and open states were taken with this work, by the review's numbers:

- 3 and 15: goats covering words, links and stamps; a goat on the photo
  in "How we treat each other".
- 5: on a phone, opening Tuzla or the food showed a second closed row
  with the same pictures.
- 6: the strip of pictures on a closed row did nothing when pressed.
- 7: on a wide screen the closed page was ten mostly empty rows.
- 8: closed headings on a phone wrapped to make room for a goat and
  wrapped differently when opened.
- 9: "Why a goat" unlike the other rows, closed and opened.
- 24: closed rows on a phone were five different things at very
  different heights.
- 28: a link to a section landed too low.
- 31: hollow gaps kept for a goat that is not standing there.
- 4: the open phone menu taller than a sideways phone, out of reach.
- 29: no theme control in the footer on a phone.

Not the organiser's words, and for the organiser to confirm: taking the
goat's stop off the photo in "How we treat each other" (the section keeps
the goat of its row), and moving the cook from the food heading to the
food section's corner at the size every section's goat has. The decision
of 2026-11-07 that the home page's goats stay
(sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-goats-stay.md) stands for
everything else.
