---
title: "A flash when the goat is clicked, light and dark cards to download, and goats that say something"
date: 2026-11-22
kind: conversation
---

On 2026-11-22, after rounds one to ten of the design work were published
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--ship-all.md), the organiser looked
at the live site and wrote, verbatim:

> When I click on the goat, the entire section it's connected to flashes
> We should offer light/dark versions of all cards on the site to download, like the speakers ones etc. also... Maybe the goat of speakers on their cards should say stuff like see you in Tuzla ?

Read as three things, taken up in an eleventh round on a branch, shown by
images, with nothing pushed, merged or published before the organiser
approves
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md):

1. A bug: clicking or tapping the goat makes the section it stands on
   flash. A click on the goat should do only what the goat does.
2. A request: every card the site offers (the speakers' cards "etc.") can
   be downloaded in a light and in a dark version.
3. A "maybe": the goat on a speaker's card could say something, "like see
   you in Tuzla". Because it is a maybe, it is built as a proposal on its
   own branch, `site/cards-goat-says`, for the organiser to take or drop,
   with lines drawn only from words already on the site or in the
   organiser's recorded words.

Later the same day, still looking at the live site, the organiser added,
verbatim:

> In the Tuzla the home town section the goats popping up on the images are too big imo, we need a nicer way that is more pleasant for the visitor there

Read as a fourth thing for the same round: on the photos of "Tuzla, the
host town" the goat is smaller and comes in a gentler way that leaves the
picture clear. Every goat stop stays, as decided on 7 November
(sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-goats-stay.md); only its size
and manner change. How it looks is the designer's choice, marked in
DESIGN.md for the organiser to look at again.
