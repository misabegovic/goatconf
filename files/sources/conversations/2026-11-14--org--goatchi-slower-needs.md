The organiser asked, verbatim:

> Let's change how often the goat needs attention. Once a week

Told that today food empties over a day, play over a day and a half and sleep over two days, that the goat sulks below a quarter (about 18 hours after a feed), that a challenge goat wanders off after three days with every need empty and that reminders come at most once a day, they were offered three ways to retime the needs: never sulking within a week (food over about nine and a third days), today's times scaled by seven (a week, a week and a half, two weeks), or all three needs emptying over one week. They answered, verbatim:

> scale today's times x3

For challenge mode they chose "Scale to 3 weeks (Recommended)", described as: a goat wanders off after three weeks with every need empty. For the reminders they chose "Keep once a day (Recommended)", described as: while it sulks the goat may remind at most once a day.

Asked whether "x3" was meant, since a week and the three-week challenge both pointed to seven (by three: food over 3 days, play over 4.5, sleep over 6, first sulk about 2 and a quarter days after a feed), they chose "×3, as typed", described as: food 3 days, play 4.5 days, sleep 6 days; challenge stays at the three weeks chosen.
