# Supporters who fund a named thing, and hiring that attendees opt into

Product requirements for how companies and individuals support GoatConf
2027 and how hiring companies meet attendees. It graduates the pitch
[Supporters who do something](../pitches/partners-sponsors-and-hiring.md),
on which the organiser bet on 2026-10-07
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--bet-on-every-pitch.md`), and sits
under the epic [GoatConf 2027](../epics/goatconf-2027.md).

## Objective

**A menu of named stretch goals that supporters fund by paying the
supplier directly or giving in kind, each thanked by name, plus a job board
and an opt-in "open to work" signal, so companies that want to hire can
help the event without any money passing through the organiser and without
attendees becoming a recruiting list.**

## Background

The organiser floated hiring as a way in: "If companies want to hire these
folks they can reach out and help the event in that way, as a sort of
agency maybe... Idk"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`). The guide
agrees that hiring companies are the clearest sponsors, since one hire can
repay a sponsorship "several times over"
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ What sponsors want).

Three organiser decisions bound the shape. No money goes through the
organiser: "Sponsors can provide no money directly to me. No money goes
though me", and the budget is public
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`). The
organiser's own money is a fallback only, "Normally, I pay for nothing
also"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`),
and it never pays for food or drink
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
So every stretch goal, coffee and lunch included, happens only if someone
funds it.

The calendar is against a first edition: sponsor budgets are often set "by
the previous November for the following year" (guide, § Lower your
expectations), which is weeks from 2026-10-07. A free event that feels like
a recruiting funnel also loses the people it is for
([attendee persona](../../../.claude/personas/users/attendee.md)).

## Affected personas

- **[Hiring partner](../../../.claude/personas/users/hiring-partner.md).**
  *Now:* no way to support the event or meet attendees. *Target:* a short
  menu where each item names what it pays for, its supplier and an
  estimated amount; a job board entry; a hiring corner or office hours on
  the day; and a list of attendees who chose to be visible to hiring
  partners, and only those.
- **[Attendee](../../../.claude/personas/users/attendee.md).** *Now:*
  nothing. *Target:* a free event where support is visible and honest,
  where nobody's details reach a company unless they opted in, and where
  saying no changes nothing about their day.

Secondary:

- **[Extension host](../../../.claude/personas/users/extension-host.md)**:
  support is itself an extension slot; a supporter's item is recorded the
  way other extensions are.
- **[Speaker](../../../.claude/personas/users/speaker.md)**: a company that
  pays its own speaker's travel is thanked as a supporter; a speaker grant
  pool exists only as a funded menu item.

## Now / Perceived / Target

- **Now (2026-10-07):** no supporter, no menu, no job board, no outreach.
  The organiser has decided that no money passes through them and that the
  budget is public.
- **Perceived:** most sponsors expect to pay a conference an invoice and
  get a logo tier in return (guide, § Package design). (unknown, needs
  source) Whether Tuzla suppliers can invoice a foreign supporter, and
  whether supporters will accept paying a café or printer directly, is not
  known; that gap is where the model can fail.
- **Target:** a supporters' page on muhamed.at/goatconf with the menu,
  each item marked funded only when its line is on the public ledger; a
  first round of personal outreach in November 2026; a job board and a
  hiring corner by spring 2027; an opt-in signal collected at
  registration.

## Scope

Support is an extension slot. A supporter funds one named thing from a
menu of stretch goals (coffee for a day, lunch, a volunteers' lunch, the
speaker grant pool, a speaker dinner, video recording, the after-party, the
activity day, badges and lanyards), pays that item's supplier directly or
gives it in kind, and is thanked for exactly that, by name or as "an
anonymous supporter". The prices on the menu come from
[Budget and the public ledger](budget-and-public-ledger.md). Named items
already work elsewhere: Balkan Ruby sells Food and Coffee at 1,500 EUR each
beside tiers starting at 500 EUR
(`sources/web/funding/balkanruby-sponsorship.md`), and EuRuKo 2024 took
"Anonymous support" from 500 EUR
(`sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-sponsoring.md`). GoatConf can go lower
for individuals and local companies.

Thanks are capped and content is not for sale, the line FOSDEM's charter
draws (`sources/web/funding/fosdem-charter.md`). Outreach is personal and
starts with companies connected to the organiser ("You're selling you",
guide, § Lower your expectations), the sponsors of EuRuKo 2024 and RubyConf
Austria, companies hiring in Tuzla and the region, IPI Academy's employer
partners, which it describes as "a number of collaborations with employers"
(`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-about.md`), and local foundations, as the
BH Futures Foundation paid for scholars to attend DevFest Sarajevo 2025
(`sources/web/funding/bhff-devfest-sarajevo-2025.md`).

Hiring is opt-in throughout. A job board on the site carries one entry per
company that wants it, as EuRuKo 2024 ran
(`sources/web/hiring/euruko-2024-job-board.md`). A hiring corner or office
hours at the venue lets companies meet people who walk up. At registration
an attendee may choose to be visible to hiring partners; nobody else is.
Write the Docs states "We do not share any attendee information with
sponsors" (`sources/web/funding/writethedocs-portland-2026-prospectus.md`),
Strange Loop never shares or sells
(`sources/web/hiring/strangeloop-privacy-policy.md`), and LeadDev says
"Choosing not to have your badge scanned will not affect your participation
in the event" (`sources/web/hiring/leaddev-london-terms.md`).

The organiser's "agency" idea, in its full form, is a hiring platform
charging a share of a hire's salary, which is what WeAreDevelopers grew
into (`sources/web/hiring/wbs-wearedevelopers-ben-ruschin.md`). That is a
business needing a company, contracts and placement liability, and it
stays out of 2027.

## No-gos

- No money from any supporter to the organiser or to GoatConf; supporters
  pay suppliers or give in kind, and every line is on the public ledger.
- No food or drink from the organiser's fallback money; food and drink on
  the menu happen only if a supporter funds them.
- No attendee list sold or given to anyone; only attendees who opted in are
  visible, and only to hiring partners.
- No sponsored talks, keynotes or speaking slots.
- No placement fees and no agency in 2027.
- No stretch goal announced as funded before its line is on the ledger.
- No supporter contact details or contracts in this repository, which will
  be public; only what is published on the ledger and supporters' page.

## Rabbit holes

- **Supporters who insist on paying an invoice to the event.** The answer is
  always the supplier's invoice or in kind. If a supplier cannot invoice a
  foreign company, look for an in-kind route rather than a workaround that
  routes money through anyone.
- **Supporters wanting more than thanks:** a talk, the attendee list, a
  keynote. The charter says no to all three; stop negotiating there.
- **Tier design.** Metal tiers and benefit matrices are the work this model
  avoids; a menu of items is enough.
- **Late start.** A first edition approaching sponsors in winter will hear
  "not this year" often. Small, local and personal supporters are the
  realistic base; do not chase large budgets already spent.
- **Building a hiring platform.** If the opt-in signal starts to need
  matching, messaging or profiles, stop and re-pitch.

## Appetite

**Medium:** a menu and a supporters' page in November 2026, personal
outreach through the winter, and a job board, hiring corner and opt-in
signal set up by spring 2027. The outreach is the long part; each piece of
setup is small.

## Success metrics

- **At least three menu items funded by 15 May 2027.** Observed by counting
  ledger lines that name a supporter and an item, on the budget page and
  its source in this repository.
- **No money received by the organiser from any supporter.** Observed in
  the final account published within two weeks of the event: every
  supporter line names a supplier paid or an in-kind gift.
- **At least five job board entries by the week before the event.**
  Observed as a count of entries on the job board page.
- **The opt-in rate is reported and only opted-in attendees are shared.**
  Observed from the registration tool's export: the count who opted in
  against all registrations, recorded as numbers only, and the hiring
  partners' list matching that count.

## Decisions already made by the organiser

- **No money passes through the organiser; supporters give the organiser no
  money.** `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`.
- **The budget is public.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`.
- **The ledger is a budget page on muhamed.at/goatconf.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`
  (question 2).
- **The organiser's own money is a fallback only, at most 1,000 EUR.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`
  and `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`
  (question 8).
- **The organiser pays for no food or drink, for anyone.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`.
- **The event is free to attend.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`.
- **Extension hosts contribute by pull request or the same form as the
  CFP; an agent checks, the organiser merges.**
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--goatconf-will-be-public.md`.

A menu over tiers, opt-in visibility over badge scanning, and deferring the
agency are choices made in auto mode in the pitch; this PRD carries them
for the organiser to approve or change.

## Decision needed

**How the opt-in "open to work" signal is collected and reaches hiring
partners:** a question in the registration tool whose export is shared only
for those who opted in, attendees publishing their own short entry, or a
form separate from registration. The ADR picks one, states where the data
lives (never in this public repository), who may see it, and when it is
deleted after the event; it also records the shape of a supporter's record
on the supporters' page.

**Answered by the ADR** [Ask about open to work as an unticked Luma question, shared only for those who tick it](../adrs/supporters-and-hiring.md), accepted 2026-10-07.
