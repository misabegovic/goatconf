Nardi Pub
Hadžibakirbega Tuzlića 3, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
https://www.nardipub.com/
We aren’t entirely sure how but Nardi Pub manages to simultaneously feel like a cabin and an old boozer at the same time. There is shedloads of space here, two floors of the stuff, making it a great place for live music or an early evening’s eating. We tend to head here for a pint or two, embellished by friendly staff and an atmosphere free of pretension.
