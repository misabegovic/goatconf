IPI Academy, the International Business-Information Academy, was founded in 2014 as the first local private higher education institution in Tuzla. It teaches three- and four-year programs in information technology, market communications, contemporary business and IT management, and informatics and computing, with practical teaching and a mandatory professional placement. At its Student HUB @ipia, students get a space, equipment and mentoring to build their ideas and business plans for free.
