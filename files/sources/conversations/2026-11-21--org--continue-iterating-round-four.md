On 2026-11-21, shown the third round of the design iteration on the local
branch `site/phone-first-and-one-ask`, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok, continue iterating

Read as: a fourth round on the same branch, through the brain's
conventions as before
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--keep-iterating.md,
sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--continue-iterating.md): the
written intent amended before the site changes, an earlier decision of
the organiser winning wherever one exists, nothing getting longer, and
nothing pushed, merged or published before the organiser approves it
from screenshots
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).

The fourth round covers: the statue sticker in "Why a goat" at a proper
size without added height; the program's open rooms beside a reserved
card on a wide screen; the places on Make it yours on a phone, far
shorter with every place kept; a head image of its own for the
supporters, contact, charter and accessibility pages; the four most
visible flaws on the pages no round has looked at closely; and one pass
over every page for consistency.

While the fourth round was under way the organiser added, verbatim:

> Also reevaluate the sheer amount of colour blur on the site. Let's aim to be minimalistic and tasteful

and then corrected it, verbatim:

> colour blue*

So the sentence reads: "Also reevaluate the sheer amount of colour blue on
the site. Let's aim to be minimalistic and tasteful". It is about how
much blue the site uses, not about blur: the page ground, the top bar and
footer, every band, the fact stickers, the page heads and the cover, the
ticket and the session sticker, the links and the headings are all blue
today. Read as: blue becomes a deliberate accent on a calm page. The
album's identity stays: die-cut stickers, numbers, dashed slots, tilts,
stamps, the yellow, the goats and the real photos.

Because this changes the look the organiser approved in October, it is
built as two variants to choose between by eye, each a local branch from
the fourth round's head and neither on the work branch:
`site/less-blue-calm`, a clear reduction that keeps blue as the
recognisable brand colour, and `site/less-blue-minimal`, where blue is a
small accent on paper. The share cards and the brand files are not
changed by either, so they would differ from the site until the organiser
decides. Where an earlier decision on the blue world conflicts with these
words, these words are the newer direction, and each earlier decision
moved past is named in the variant's amendment.

Later in the same round the organiser added, verbatim:

> Share cards and brand files are part of it once we have a way forward

Read as: the share cards (tools/shareables.py) and the brand files
(tools/brand.py) follow the less-blue direction, but only after the
organiser has chosen between the two variants. In this round they are not
rendered again; each variant's amendment names them as the next step.
