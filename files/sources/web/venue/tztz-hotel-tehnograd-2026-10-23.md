‘Hotel Tehnograd’ is located in the residential area of Slavinovići and, as such, offers a pleasant stay away from the noise and crowds of the city. It comprises 67 rooms and two suites. All rooms feature a private bathroom, balcony, cable television, heating and free Wi-Fi access.
Guests have access to a reception area where, in a pleasant atmosphere, they can enjoy a coffee or other refreshments. A breakfast buffet is served in the enclosed restaurant next to the hotel.
Free secure parking with video surveillance is provided for all guests, accommodating both small and large vehicles. The hotel’s ‘Tehnograd’ banquet hall is designed for weddings, formal occasions, business events, graduations, birthdays, seminars, congresses, training sessions, presentations and various promotional events. It also offers clients the possibility of adapting the space to different types of special occasions.
Within the banquet hall, there is also a smaller meeting room with a capacity of up to 30 people, intended for seminars, presentations, training sessions and meetings.
