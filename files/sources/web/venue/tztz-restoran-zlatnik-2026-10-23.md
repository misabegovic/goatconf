Located in the very center of the city, next to the Slana Banja promenade, Restaurant ‘Zlatnik’ offers a unique dining experience. Surrounded by nature and situated right beside the Panonian Lake, the restaurant boasts a beautiful atmosphere enhanced by its spacious summer garden.
Its exceptionally rich gastronomic offer, ranging from traditional local specialties to a variety of international dishes, provides even the most demanding guests with a first-class culinary experience in every bite.
The restaurant’s friendly and professional staff, outstanding chefs, and elegant ambiance are just some of the reasons to visit Restaurant Zlatnik.
With a seating capacity of up to 200 guests, the restaurant is an ideal venue for weddings, anniversaries, seminars, and other special events.
In addition to its extensive food menu, Restaurant Zlatnik also features an impressive wine list, offering carefully selected wines from both domestic and international wineries.
