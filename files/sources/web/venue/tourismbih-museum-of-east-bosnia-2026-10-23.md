Museum of East Bosnia
The Museum of Eastern Bosnia in Tuzla covers the archaeology, history, ethnology, art and natural heritage of north-eastern Bosnia. Its five specialist departments care for a collection of about 30,000 catalogued and studied objects, supported by a research library. Founded in November 1947 as the Museum of the National Liberation Struggle, it initially focused on the war and revolution of 1941–1945 before widening its remit and adopting its present name in 1962. In 2004, the museum received a former internal medicine hospital building near Tuzla’s historic centre. Reconstruction begun in 2010 stalled the following year for lack of funds, but a ground-floor exhibition space was adapted in 2016 for thematic displays.
