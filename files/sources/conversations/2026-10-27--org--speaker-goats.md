While step one of the goat mascot was being built, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> each speaker/session should also get their own custom goat

Asked one question at a time, the organiser answered:

- Where each speaker's goat appears: "Also on their shareable cards", described as peeking over their speaker card on the home page and the speakers page and over their session on the program, larger on their profile page, and on the "I'm speaking" and announcement images they share.
- How each goat is made: "from their photos" (typed in place of the offered choices: drawn from their talk, chosen by the speakers, or a mix).
- Whether a speaker sees their goat before it goes on the site: "Publish, change on request", described as: goats go live once the organiser approves them; a speaker who dislikes theirs can ask for a change or removal.

Shown drafts for all nine speakers, each drawn from their photo (hair, beard, glasses, cap, what they wear, and a microphone, a presentation clicker, or for Christoph Lipautz the cat in his photo; no logos from their clothes), and asked "Are these speaker goats good enough to put on the site?", the organiser chose, verbatim:

> Yes, build them in (Recommended)

described as: add each goat's traits to the speaker records and put them on the site, peeking over their cards on the home and speakers pages and over their sessions, whole on their profile page, and on their shareable cards, shown in the browser before it ships.
