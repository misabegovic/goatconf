On Monday 23 November 2026, shipping the speaker cards
(sources/conversations/2026-11-23--org--speaker-cards-photo-first.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Sounds good ship it. We should also update other cards to follow the same design

The agent drew the other cards in that design and sent them as pictures,
on paper and on the album's blue, before changing any of them: a post of
words (the title large in two tones, the plain goat sitting on the band),
a post with a photo (the photo as a tilted sticker on a yellow block, the
title on one line under it, an upright photo on the left with the title
beside it), a supporter's two cards (the logo's tile in the photo's place,
a tab reading "Thank you" or "Proud supporter", the goat on the wordmark)
and an event's card (a tab reading "Part of GoatConf 2027" over the
event's name).

It asked three things: whether these go ahead as drawn; whether the proud
supporter card's headline may change from "GoatConf 2027" to the
supporter's own name, so that it matches the thank-you card apart from the
tab and the line under the name; and whether the banners, the LinkedIn and
Luma covers, the site's link preview, the Goatchi card and the animated
goats posts should stay as they are. It also said that going ahead renders
again the cards of posts already out. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Looks good

Read as yes to all three: the cards are built as drawn, the proud
supporter card names the supporter, and the banners, the covers, the
site's link preview, the Goatchi card and the goats posts keep the look
they had.

What the agent chose without the organiser's word, for the organiser to
look at again: every such card carries the same plain sitting goat, the
only goat that is about no one that this repository holds a picture of;
a wide post's title of three set lines is joined to two; and on a wide
post with a photo a title without set lines wraps onto two.
