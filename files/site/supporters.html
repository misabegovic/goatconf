The supporters

Companies and people who pay a supplier directly for one named thing, such as a speaker's travel.

Who makes it possible

GoatConf is free, and no money passes through the organiser. A supporter pays the supplier directly for one named thing: a speaker's flight or room, a round of coffee, the printing. We thank each supporter for exactly what they paid for. Every payment is listed in the public budget. Supporters cannot buy talks, stage time or the attendee list. See the charter.

Fund a speaker's travel

Pick a speaker, or fund travel grants: tickets or rooms for speakers who ask for help. You pay the airline or hotel directly. Asking never affects whether a session is picked. Your logo shows on this page and on the profile of every speaker whose travel you covered. Tell us through the proposal form. Choose Support.
