The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> It's weird to see on web 2 empty slots on who's speaking... Why do we limit to 6 and leave emptiness like this?

The agent explained that the grids fit as many cards in a row as the screen allows (4 to 6), so the last row is often short, and that the home page's cap of six was set on 2026-10-12, before any speaker existed. Asked how to handle a row that is not full, the organiser answered in their own words:

> Maybe just plan for 8? And add a filler card with CFP call until we fill all

and chose "Keep a cap, at 8" for the home page. Asked three follow-ups, the organiser chose:

- **The filler:** "One card per open place (Recommended)", each the size of a speaker card.
- **Where:** "Home page only". The speakers page keeps its grid, with no filler cards.
- **Beyond eight:** "No more filler past 8".

Done on 2026-10-14: the home page's "Who is speaking" shows up to eight speakers in the organiser's order, four to a row on a wide screen and two on a phone, so eight places are always two or four full rows. Each place not yet taken is a dashed card, "Your talk here? The call for proposals is open.", stamped "Yours?", linking to the talk page. With no speakers at all, the single wide "The first speaker could be you" card stays as before (sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--speakers-empty-state.md). Past eight speakers, the home page shows the first eight and links to all of them.
