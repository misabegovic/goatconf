After accepting the BH Professionals Meetup
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--bh-professionals-meetup-accepted.md`),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Alao add BHFF as supporters

The agent said the charter and the supporters page name four ways to
support GoatConf (online, 500 euro invoiced by the organiser's Austrian
business; paying a supplier directly for one named thing; something in
kind; a speaker's company covering that speaker's travel), and that
running and paying for one's own accepted event is not one of them,
since every host carries its own costs. It recommended not listing the
foundation on the meetup alone, and asked three questions. The
organiser chose:

- **What the foundation gives:** "Only the meetup". The foundation is
  listed anyway, on the organiser's decision; its line on the
  supporters page names the meetup, so the page says exactly what it
  does.
- **Consent:** "Yes, agreed". The foundation agreed to be listed and to
  have its logo shown; the logo is taken from its website.
- **Where it lands:** "Add to PR #80", with the meetup.

The agent treats this as one listing for this foundation. The rules on
the supporters page and the charter are unchanged; making "hosting an
accepted event" a way to support GoatConf would be an amendment of its
own (unverified intent, 2026-10-18).
