Asked eight questions after the vision (2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md), the operator chose, verbatim option labels:

1. "A framework others build on that fits the event": **Both, event first**. GoatConf 2027 is a small core (venue, schedule, talks, registration) with open slots others fill (side events, workshops, booths, hiring, activities, extra venues), and the slot rules are written so anyone can reuse the kit for their own event later.
2. Audience: **Open, multi-discipline**. Engineering, design, product, founders: great minds broadly.
3. Size of the first edition: **120 to 300**. One main track plus a workshop room, likely a second venue for something.
4. Impeccable: **Install in goatconf only**, with the update check and choice reporting off, then use it for PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md and the website. (Installed the same day, impeccable 4.1.0, engine 0.1.11.)
5. Process: **Auto pitches, you bet (Recommended)**. One umbrella pitch for the minimal core and its extension points plus one pitch per area; the agent decides in-phase and records why; the operator bets on which become committed work.
6. Duration: **Open, explore it**. The schedule pitch compares the options.
7. Language: **English**. International audience; site and talks in English.
8. The operator's own money, as a ceiling before any sponsor or partner money: **Up to 1,000 EUR**. Truly minimal: the venue is the partner's, everything else volunteer or sponsored.
