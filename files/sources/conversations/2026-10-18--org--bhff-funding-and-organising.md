After the foundation was added to the supporters with the line "The BH
Professionals Meetup on Saturday 5 June"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-18--org--bhff-listed-as-supporter.md`),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Funding and organizing

Read as the wording of the foundation's line: it funds and organises
the meetup. The line becomes "Funding and organising the BH
Professionals Meetup on Saturday 5 June", spelled "organising" as the
site spells "organiser" and "Organised". Its thank-you card read "for
funding" in front of every line, so a line that starts with what the
supporter does is now used as it stands: "for funding and organising
the BH Professionals Meetup on Saturday 5 June." The square card also
sets a long supporter name smaller, so the foundation's name and line
no longer run into its logo.
