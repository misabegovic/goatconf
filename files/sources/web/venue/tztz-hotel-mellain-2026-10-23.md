OSM places the Mellain hotel on ZAVNOBiH-a at 44.53398, 18.68726, about 1.3 km straight-line from IPI.

Mellain Hotel is located in the heart of Tuzla, in one of the city’s most attractive areas, connecting its most important economic, educational, and cultural institutions. Its proximity to the airport, university and clinical centres, as well as the Panonian Lakes complex, further enhances the appeal of this modern hotel. Tuzla and Mellain Hotel have preserved a centuries-old tradition of healing, offering visitors the opportunity to experience the unique charm of this renowned wellness destination.
As part of a larger complex, the hotel was designed to be one of the most prestigious hotels in Bosnia and Herzegovina, enriching the tourism offer of the entire region through its high-quality facilities and services. The hotel features 130 luxuriously furnished and spacious rooms and suites.
The hotel offers a wide range of amenities, including a swimming pool with saunas, a fitness centre, a nightclub, a casino, multifunctional halls, a panoramic-view restaurant, and various additional facilities. A particular advantage is its direct connection to a spa and wellness centre and a saltwater pool, creating a comprehensive health tourism experience. The hotel’s facilities and overall concept fully meet the tourism and business needs of modern travellers.
