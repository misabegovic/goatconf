On the travel page's transfer list, the organiser asked, verbatim:

> Are you sure folks cant use services such as BalkanTransfer ?

The agent had read only three Balkan Transfer pages. It then read the routes the company's booking form offers (sources/web/travel/balkantransfer-routes-2026-11-02.md). Balkan Transfer runs shared vans from Sarajevo airport to Tuzla and from Tuzla back to Sarajevo airport, listed from 19 EUR and about 2 hours. It offers nothing from Tuzla airport into Tuzla (from Tuzla airport it goes to Kladanj, Olovo and Sarajevo) and nothing from Banja Luka airport to Tuzla (its nearest stop is Doboj).

Done on 2026-11-02, on the same pull request: the travel page gives Balkan Transfer's price, "from 19 € a person", and says it also runs back to Sarajevo airport for the flight home.
