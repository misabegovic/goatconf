---
title: "Open to anyone with something to share, as themselves"
date: 2026-10-24
kind: conversation
---

On Saturday 24 October 2026, after the charter gained its line on the
values of free and open-source software, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> One of the reasons I like the conference to be like this is that it doesn't limit me in terms of who I can host. I can invite or accept anyone that I identify as a person that has knowledge to share that I or others can learn from and they can be 100% themselves, authentic and enrich the event that way. They don't have to change their views to fit a certain community, they can fully be themselves and bring ans show their best version to stage

The agent asked whether this should go on the site or stay in the brain,
where (the charter, the talk page or both), how views relate to the code
of conduct, and whether the site should say the organiser also invites
speakers directly. It proposed the framing "people are chosen for what
they know, not for what they believe; the code of conduct covers how
everyone treats each other", and this draft charter point:

> **Open to anyone with something to share.** GoatConf is not tied to one language, stack or community. People speak here for what they know and what others can learn from them. They come as themselves and do not have to change their views to fit in. The code of conduct sets how we treat each other.

The organiser answered, verbatim:

> Charter only, your framing is right, use the draft

The question about saying that the organiser invites speakers directly
was left unanswered.
