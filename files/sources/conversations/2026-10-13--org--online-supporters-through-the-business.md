The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> ok. so, I was thinking, maybe it makes sense to accept sponsorships for online only? that help with travel costs of speakers or something else in the end. lets explore what it would look like, see also how sponsorships were done for euruko 2024 and rubyconf austria (you have both projects on this device locally) and let's explore a legally safe way of me accepting these sponsorships for an event in Tuzla. I already have a company registered in Austria with valid VAT and I can use it for event management

After the research (sources/web/legal/online-sponsorship-austria-bih-2026-10-13.md), the organiser answered one question at a time:

- **Lift "no money goes through the organiser" for online-only sponsorships, paid to the Austrian business?** "Yes, online only".
- **Packages:** first "Three small ones" (online supporter 500 EUR, travel grant 500 EUR, online partner 1,500 EUR).
- **A surplus:** "More travel grants".

Then, verbatim:

> ok and we can remove travel support and just leave online support at the same level. we won't support paying to cover a specific speaker's travel

- **How many online levels:** "One level, 500 EUR". Every online supporter gets the same perks.
- **What the money pays for:** "Event costs". Whatever the event needs and nobody else covers, including speaker travel where the organiser decides it helps the program, never tied to a sponsor.

Then, verbatim:

> also maybe we shouldn't have the budget publicly available

- **How public the budget is:** "Private". No budget page; sponsors and anyone who asks get a summary by email.

Read together: the surplus answer ("More travel grants") was given before travel grants were dropped, so it is read as: leftover money pays more of the event's costs, speaker travel included where the organiser decides, and anything still left carries to the next edition. This reading is the agent's and needs the organiser's confirmation.

Then, verbatim:

> also don't mention on the site that folks can ask to get a summary by email

So the site says nothing about the budget: no budget page, and no offer of a summary.

Then, verbatim:

> travel refund for speakers happens only if the budget allows, not promised or guaranteed

So speakers pay their own travel. If the budget allows, the organiser may refund some or all of it; this is never promised or guaranteed.
