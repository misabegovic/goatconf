Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures Foundation

Funding and organising the BH Professionals Meetup on Saturday 5 June

Who they are

Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures Foundation empowers young people in Bosnia and Herzegovina through non-formal education, access to technology and leadership development. Through its Futures Scholarship, Researchers, and Entrepreneurs & Innovators programs, students build globally relevant careers at home, with mentoring from Bosnian and Herzegovinian professionals around the world. It aims to develop 10,000 STEM leaders by 2030.

How to support them

  • Partner as an organisation: scholarships, internships, mentors and speakers
  • Get involved as a professional: host a student, mentor, host a networking event
  • Join as a student
  • Become a monthly donor, from 5 euro a month
