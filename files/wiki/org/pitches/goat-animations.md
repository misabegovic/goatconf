# More goat animations: tap to pet, ears and tail that move at rest, and more tricks

## Problem

The goats on the site move with three tricks on a tap (a jump for joy, a somersault, a shake of the head), a few
moves while they wait (a bob, a wiggle, a stretch, a look around, peek-a-boo) and a blink; the speakers' goats also
look around (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--goat-tricks.md;
sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--paul-campbell-peek-old-style.md). In Goatchi a goat breathes, chews and
sleeps, but does not blink and does nothing when tapped. Between moves a goat is a still painting. The organiser
finds there is "ways to go to improve animation of existing goats" and that "we need more animations", not only in
Goatchi, and chose to start with petting, ears and tail, and more tricks
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--more-goat-animations.md).

## Appetite

**Medium**: about one to two weeks, much of it art in ChatGPT, painted the way the speakers' looking frames were and
sent by the organiser while Chrome blocks downloads on their computer.

## Solution

Fat-marker, for a bet.

- **Tap to pet.** Tapping a goat anywhere, Goatchi included, pets it first: a small heart rises, the goat closes its
  eyes happily and leans into the tap, then says a line. A second tap plays a trick, as today. In Goatchi a pet lifts
  play a little, a few times an hour at most.
- **Ears and tail at rest.** A painted frame with the ears flicked and one with the tail wagging, for the plain goat,
  the role goats and the speakers, swapped in for a moment now and then while the goat waits, so it never looks frozen.
  Moved as whole pictures, like the blink and the looking frames, so they look painted, not drawn in code.
- **More tricks.** A stretch with a yawn, a sneeze, a scratch behind the ear with a hind hoof and a little dance, each
  a painted frame or two, added to the tricks played on a tap and to the moves while waiting; on four hooves.
- **Blinking in Goatchi**, which the companion already does.

## Rabbit holes

- **Art per goat.** Every goat type and speaker needs its frames: for twenty goats, about forty sheets. The plain goat
  and the speakers come first; role goats can follow.
- **Weight.** The new frames load after the page, as the looking frames do, and only for goats on the page.
- **Too busy.** More moves can feel restless; moves stay rare and slow, and reduced motion keeps only the blink.

## No-gos

- No goat upright on two legs.
- Nothing about it is sent anywhere; no tracking of taps.
- Nothing covers a food photo or the when, where and how to register.
