On 2026-11-21, shown the three-panel images of the site as it is, the
calm variant and the minimal variant
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--continue-iterating-round-four.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> I don't like the hooves missing. I lean either calm or minimal, but I want to see more improvements

Read as three things. The blue-ground look is no longer a candidate: the
work goes on only on the two variant branches, `site/less-blue-calm` and
`site/less-blue-minimal`, and the branch `site/phone-first-and-one-ask`
stays as it is. The hoof marks stay in both: they had left the minimal
variant, and in the calm variant they were printed at 5% and read as
missing, so they come back clearly present on the page ground, quiet,
and never under body text inside a white page. And each variant is
designed properly, so that it looks conceived that way and not recoloured:
the album page as an object on paper, the ticket, the cover and the top
bar, the difference between a stuck sticker and an empty slot, the tag
tints as one set, the rhythm, the focus rings and the inner pages.

Nothing is pushed, merged or published before the organiser approves it
(sources/conversations/2026-11-21--org--design-iteration-on-a-branch.md).
The share cards and the brand files are still not rendered again; mock-ups
of three cards in each variant's look are made outside the repository.
