## Scope

- **Who:** co-founder and Head of Engineering at Semblie, "where he leads the utilization of modern C++ for firmware development" (source: sources/web/speakers/eoc-amar-mahmutbegovic-2026-10-14.md). His work includes BLE consumer devices and medical devices, and he mentors young engineers (source: sources/web/speakers/cpp-in-embedded-systems-google-books-2026-10-14.md).
- **A Tuzla company:** Semblie d.o.o. Tuzla is an electronic hardware development company registered in Tuzla, started by two electrical engineering students and now running its own PCB assembly line (source: sources/web/speakers/semblie-about-2026-10-14.md). That makes him a local speaker, and Semblie a local company. Whether he lives in Tuzla is *(unknown, needs source)*.
- **The book:** "C++ in Embedded Systems: A Practical Transition from C to Modern C++", Packt, 2025, 402 pages (source: sources/web/speakers/cpp-in-embedded-systems-google-books-2026-10-14.md).
- **Talks so far:** four at the Embedded Online Conference, each year from 2023 to 2026: "Modern C++ in Embedded Development" (2023), "Modern C++ in Embedded Development: Expressive Compile-Time Computation" (2024), "Managing Software Components with Conan Package Manager" (2025), and "Building Firmware with CMake" (2026) (source: sources/web/speakers/eoc-amar-mahmutbegovic-2026-10-14.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet. Whether he has spoken in person at a conference is *(unknown, needs source)*.
- **Bio on the site:** the first two sentences of his conference bio, word for word, then one sentence added on the organiser's choice: "He is the author of C++ in Embedded Systems (Packt, 2025)." (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--amar-mahmutbegovic.md). His own bio spells his name "Mahmutbegovic"; the book and the record spell it "Mahmutbegović".
