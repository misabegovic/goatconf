The tourist train, managed by Public Company “Traffic and Communications” Tuzla is a tourist attraction on the streets of the City of Salt.

Passengers of all age, especially children, who want to learn about famous sites of Tuzla, travel by this train on daily basis from the Square of Freedom in the downtown to the Pannonian Lakes.

During the ride, passengers are informed about famous sites of the city along with historical details and a rich tourist offer, which justify coming to Tuzla.

The train ordinates regularly on work days, except Mondays. Time sheet of rides from the Pannonian Lakes is the following: 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00.

Passengers may see the Pannonian Lakes, the Archeological Park- Neolithic settlement of pile dwellings, the Geological Museum, memorials dedicated to soldiers in Salty Spa Complex, the Peace Flame House, “Ismet Mujezinović” Atelier, the Catholic School Center and a national monument Turali Bey’s Mosque.
