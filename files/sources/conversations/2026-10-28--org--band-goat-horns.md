title: "On phones, a one-line band is as tall as its goat, so the goat's horns are not cut off"
date: 2026-10-28
kind: conversation
---

With a phone screenshot of Dejan Menges's page, his goat's horns cut off by the top of the page above the "Who they are" band, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> It cuts off a bit here

and, while it was being fixed:

> Check others also

On phones a band with a goat peeking over it is now at least as tall as the goat. The agent checked every stop of the companion goat on seventeen pages (the home, Host an event, Speakers, Travel, Supporters, Propose a session, Register, Stickers and Program pages and the eight speakers' pages) at 360, 412, 768 and 1300 pixels wide: with the change no goat is cut by an edge, and without it the check finds the cut on Dejan's page. Shown the band in the browser, the organiser chose "Ship it (Recommended)".
