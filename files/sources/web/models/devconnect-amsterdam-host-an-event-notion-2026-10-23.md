Host an event at Devconnect!
> So you want to host an event at Devconnect? Read on to find out how!
> Tl;dr: Just do it! No permission needed. Set the goals, determine target demographic, select a date based on Devconnect calendar, choose from one of the many great venues in Amsterdam, and announce (tagging @EFDevconnect)!
Let us know via this form once you have the event confirmed, and if the event is Ethereum ethos-aligned, we may list your event on our official agenda!
First, thank you for expressing interest in participating! As a recap, Devconnect is a week-long series of independent events focused on specific topics organized by independent teams/projects that are most familiar with the topic. Through Devconnect, we aim to foster:
- Smaller, depth-first conversations led by the experts in those topics
- Audiences/attendees that are very relevant to the topic at hand
- Foster collaboration and community throughout Devconnect week
We, the Devconnect team, will be supporting the week in three ways:
  1. Signaling towards a place (Amsterdam) and a time (April 18-25) for in-person collaboration among our distributed Ethereum community
  1. Organizing a Co-working Space throughout the week open to all, and
  1. Helping with publicity through Devconnect.org calendar, twitter, etc. 
  Beyond that, all the events happening throughout the week will be independently hosted. There will be no "main" event. All events will be in different venues around Amsterdam — a city where everything is within biking distance 🚲.
### So what can you do?
Well, it's entirely up to you! Some ideas:
1. Collaborate with an event that is already in the works
  - We have launched the official dates (April 18-25) and an initial calendar of events! Check out the full calendar here, and read the blog post for more info. See if there are any aligned or related events that you could work with, and reach out to them.
1. Create your own event
  - See a void in a particular domain? Create your own part-day, full-day, or multi-day event!
  - Let us know via this form once you have the event confirmed, and we ~may~ list your event on our official agenda!
    Devconnect Events
1. Just come as an attendee
  - Check the schedule for events you are interested in and follow their individual updates for ticket sales, speaker applications, etc!
1. Be creative
  - Maybe a side event, an evening event, an activity among like-minded attendees, etc!

P.p.s. Some general FAQ about Devconnect:
What about COVID?
We are closely monitoring the ongoing situation, and while currently there are some restrictions in place in Amsterdam, we are optimistic that local regulations in Amsterdam will allow us to host the week. We will provide updates as we get closer.
How do I sponsor?
While Devconnect will be sponsor-free, independent events happening throughout Devconnect may be looking for sponsorship. If you’re interested in sponsoring, check out relevant events to see if they are accepting sponsorships.
From my understanding, Devconnect will just be separate events hosted by individual projects/teams/individuals, correct?
This is correct!
Will there be a central venue where these events will be held, or is each host responsible for locating a venue for their event?
Each host is responsible for their own venue. There will be many events in many different venues throughout Amsterdam during the week. Attendees will need to pick and choose based on their interest.
Will there be any "main events" hosted directly by Devconnect?
There will be an open Co-Working space throughout the week with tickets available to all. This will be in Beurs van Berlage. Other than that, all events will be independently hosted, ticketed, and organized.
Approximately how many people will fit at each event?
Sizes will vary, and range from a 25-person zkEVM workshop for example, to a 300-person event on staking by ETHStaker, to a 800-person EthGlobal hackathon. Some are private, some free, some will be based on applications, and others may be ticketed with paid tickets.
