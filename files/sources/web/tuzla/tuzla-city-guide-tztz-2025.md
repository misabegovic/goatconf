The central City Park is located in the very heart of Tuzla
and via a pedestrian bridge it is linked to the Pannonian
Lakes. The park is rich in various monuments, mainly
related to the medieval period. The central ﬁgure of the
park is the statue depicting Tvrtko I Kotromanić, Ban
and the ﬁrst King of the Medieval Bosnian State. Also,
there are several stećak tombstones, as well as a
monument with a display of engraved Charter of Kulin
Ban, the most important document of Bosnian
statehood.

3.THE SALT SQUARE
The center of the historical part of the city is
the Salt Square (orig. name: Soni trg),
around which dozens of narrow
granite-paved streets line up, where the
urban development of the city began.

The Ilinčica picnic area is also called the "lungs of the city"
because this area is rich in coniferous and deciduous forest,
providing visitors with space for various activities, promoting
healthy lifestyle. Ilinčica is a part of the Majevica mountain range,
with exceptional landscape value. It is rich in ﬂora and fauna and
as such Ilinčica is a natural habitat for numerous animal species.
