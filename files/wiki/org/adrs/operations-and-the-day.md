# Adopt RubyConf Austria's code of conduct and its reporting route, with an IPI contact added

Answers the "Decision needed" of
[Operations: volunteers who own a domain, the rules, and the day](../prds/operations-and-the-day.md).
Accepted 2026-10-07 from the organiser's answers
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--adr-answers.md`, questions 12
and 13), and approved by the organiser (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--adrs-approved.md`).

## What

GoatConf's code of conduct is RubyConf Austria's, with the conference's
name changed. RubyConf Austria's text is the Contributor Covenant 1.4
adapted for a conference: its pledge, its standards, the organisers' and
the CoC team's responsibilities, a scope covering event spaces and
anyone representing the conference, and enforcement through reports to
the CoC team by email, kept confidential
(`sources/web/coc/rubyconf-austria-code-of-conduct.md`). GoatConf keeps
the same reporting route: one email address that the CoC team reads. The
CoC team is the organiser and a staff member of IPI Academy, the two
contacts the organiser chose
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`).
It applies to every extension listed under GoatConf's name.

## Why

The organiser typed this instead of picking a template: "Take from
RubyConf Austria", and then "Same as RubyConf Austria" for how reports
travel. RubyConf Austria is the organiser's own conference, so its code
is one the organiser has already enforced and stands behind. The
attribution line it carries, "adapted from the Contributor Covenant,
version 1.4", is kept, as the Covenant's own text ends with it
(`sources/web/coc/contributor-covenant-1-4.md`).

## How

The site's code-of-conduct page carries the text, the email address, and
the names of the two people on the CoC team. The ten minutes on stage at
the start of the core day say where to find it and who the two are
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Code of conduct). Volunteers know to bring anyone who wants to report
in person to either contact. The run sheet keeps the PRD's shape: one
owner per domain (the door, the stage and the MC, speakers, extensions,
the breaks on the square, first aid), with a problem going first to its
domain owner and then to the organiser, and a conduct report going
straight to the CoC team.

The few words that read as a project's rather than a conference's (the
Covenant's "comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues", which RubyConf
Austria kept) stay as they are in the first version; trimming them is a
later amendment if the CoC team wants it.

## Alternatives

- **RubyConf Austria's code and route** *(chosen)*: proven by the
  organiser at their own event.
- **The Geek Feminism / Ada Initiative policy:** public domain, written
  for in-person conferences and with a staff procedure
  (`sources/web/coc/geek-feminism-anti-harassment-policy.md`); it was the
  pitch's recommendation.
- **The Contributor Covenant 3.0:** the current Covenant
  (`sources/web/coc/contributor-covenant-3-0.md`), newer than the 1.4 text
  RubyConf Austria adapts.

## Consequences

- **Opens:** a code the organiser knows how to enforce, ready to publish
  with website v1.
- **Costs:** the PRD planned a phone number for the two contacts; on the
  organiser's answer, the route is email as at RubyConf Austria, so a
  report during the day reaches the team by email or by finding one of
  them in person. One source warns that a template is not enforceable
  without the event's own reporting details
  (`sources/web/coc/confcodeofconduct-com.md`); the address and the two
  names are those details.
- **Open:** which email address the CoC team uses is (unknown, needs
  source); RubyConf Austria's page uses the organiser's own address
  (`sources/web/coc/rubyconf-austria-code-of-conduct.md`). Which IPI
  staff member joins the team is also (unknown, needs source).
- **Closes:** the Geek Feminism policy's written staff procedure, unless
  the CoC team adopts it later as the "further details of specific
  enforcement policies" the text allows to be "posted separately".
