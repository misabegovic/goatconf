title: "Online-only sponsorship through an Austrian business for an event in Bosnia and Herzegovina"
---

- **Online-only perks are an advertising service.** Business-to-business, they are taxed where the sponsor is based (§ 3a Abs. 6 UStG, Art. 44 VAT Directive): https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004873&Paragraf=3a
- **Venue perks change this.** Admission to events and services linked to it are taxed where the event happens (§ 3a Abs. 11a, Art. 53; CJEU C-647/17: https://www.bailii.org/eu/cases/EUECJ/2019/C64717.html). A booth, tickets or a logo in the hall could make a package event-linked and taxable in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Older Austrian rulings show sponsorship is not always treated as advertising: https://findok.bmf.gv.at/findok/resources/pdf/474b8424-0f0e-46da-82d4-45ee436a0fd5/29257.1.1.3.pdf
- **Invoicing:** Austrian business sponsors pay 20% Austrian VAT; EU business sponsors are invoiced net under reverse charge, with their VAT ID, and reported in the recapitulative statement; sponsors outside the EU pay no Austrian VAT (https://www.wko.at/bgld/steuern/rechnung-richtig-ausstellen, https://www.wko.at/aussenwirtschaft/exporthandbuch/rechnungslegung-an-unternehmer-eu-laender).
- **Small business exemption:** 55,000 EUR, counting only turnover taxable in Austria; a VAT ID does not rule it out (https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/weitere-steuertatbestaende-und-befreiungen/kleinunternehmen.html).
- **Income tax:** sponsorship is business income; event costs are deductible. A sponsor can deduct its payment when the advertising is agreed in writing in advance, publicly visible and documented (https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/fuer-unternehmen/einkommensteuer/information-sponsoring-kunst-kulturveranstalter.html). A payment with no counter-performance is a donation, generally not deductible for a company giver (https://www.wko.at/oe/information-consulting/werbung-marktkommunikation/was-ist-der-unterschied-zwischen-einer-spende-und-dem-spon).
- **Austrian withholding tax on lecturers (§ 99 EStG)** covers activity in Austria, so talks in Tuzla should fall outside it (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=99).
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina:** business services from abroad are taxed where the buyer is, the buyer accounting for the VAT (secondary source: https://unija.com/bs/primljene-usluge-od-pravnih-lica-iz-inostranstva/). Hotels and other purchases in Bosnia carry 17% VAT, likely not recoverable (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina/corporate/other-taxes). A three-day event with no fixed place is unlikely to create a taxable presence (judgment call; Austria and Bosnia have a tax treaty: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bosnia-and-herzegovina/corporate/withholding-taxes). Sources are thin on Bosnian sponsors that are not VAT registered.
- **Speakers need no work permit** as lecturers at organised professional gatherings, up to 90 days a year (Law on Foreigners Art. 77(1)(h) and (3): https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/bih/zakon-o-strancima.html).
- **To confirm with a tax adviser:** the trade licence's scope, the small business status, the advertising classification, the recapitulative statement and VAT returns, money received in 2026 for a 2027 event, and what happens to a surplus.
