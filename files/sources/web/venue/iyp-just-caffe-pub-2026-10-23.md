Just Caffe Pub
Jevrejska bb Tuzla, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
Well, what is it? Just a cafe, or just a pub?! Not important, that’s what that is, as this city centre spot is super popular with the city’s younger population. The central bar gives it a different feel to most other places in Tuzla and there are plenty of TVs on which to watch whatever sport you desire. There are also regular live music nights, heavily attended and heartily enjoyed.
