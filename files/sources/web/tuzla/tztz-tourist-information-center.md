Located at Turalibegova Street 5 in Tuzla, the Tourist Information Center opened its doors on June 1, 2023. It is designed to provide visitors with accurate and up-to-date information on attractions across Tuzla, Tuzla Canton, and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

The Government of Tuzla Canton, through its Ministry of Trade, Tourism, and Transport, co-funded the renovation of the space, resulting in a sleek and practical hub for tourist services.

Visitors can find information on current and upcoming events, an overview of available accommodation, tourist maps, and postcards showcasing Tuzla and its cultural identity.

City guests can access essential information on must-see attractions and the most convenient routes to desired destinations, including connections from Tuzla to surrounding towns and natural attractions.

The center provides internet and phone access, as well as an interactive standalone computer with internet connectivity and necessary guidelines. These tools facilitate independent exploration of the city.

Positioned right by the lively Korzo walkway in the heart of Tuzla, it is the go-to spot for starting your city adventure, planning your itinerary, and enjoying authentic Tuzla experiences.

Friendly staff offer advice on festivals, museums, local cuisine, guided tours, and trips to the scenic Pannonian Lakes—covering every aspect of your stay.
