The organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Also, we should improve how we communicate to speakers on the sessions page. We should find a nice way to communicate "Spend any spare 500€ supporter money on travel for self-funded speakers first." And that an effort will be made for providing recordings in the main 2 halls of IPI Academy. Also, maybe we should update the limit to 150? For the first hall, so it is correct information online

Until now the talk page said speakers pay their own travel and stay and
that, if the budget allows, the organiser may refund some or all of it,
never promised. Leftover money was to pay more of the event's costs,
speaker travel refunds included where the organiser decides
(sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--decisions-one-by-one.md). The site
said GoatConf does not record the sessions
(sources/conversations/2026-10-12--org--recording-and-podcasts.md), and that
the main hall has about 120 seats
(sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--decisions-one-by-one.md). The June
2026 visit notes said 150 (sources/conversations/2026-06--org--ipi-visit-notes.md).

Asked to choose, the organiser picked:

- **Travel:** "Supporters' money first (Recommended)". Online
  supporters' money that the event's costs do not need goes first to
  refunding speakers who pay their own way, some or all of it, after the
  event. It is not promised and never affects whether a session is
  picked. The site does not name the 500 EUR level in this line, since
  the budget is private.
- **Recording:** "Aim, not promise (Recommended)". GoatConf aims to
  record the sessions in IPI Academy's two big halls, the main hall and
  the first-floor hall; it is not promised yet. People may still bring a
  camera or a podcast.
- **Where:** "Everywhere it is said (Recommended)": the talk, program,
  accessibility and add pages and the files for agents.
- **The main hall:** "150 seats, everywhere (Recommended)".
