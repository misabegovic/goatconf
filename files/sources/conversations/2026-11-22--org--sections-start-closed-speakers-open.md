On 2026-11-22, told in summary that on a first visit every section of the
home page would be open except the two lists that close on a phone (the
persona study's verdict, with every section closable by the visitor,
sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--every-section-collapsible-and-remembered.md),
the organiser answered, verbatim:

> They start closed probably? Speakers section stays open?

Read as the organiser deciding what a first visit shows, over the persona
study's verdict, on a phone and on a wide screen alike:

- The hero (the cover and "What is decided"): open, no control.
- "Get your free ticket": open, no control.
- "Who is speaking": open, and the visitor can close it.
- Every other section: closed, and the visitor can open it.

What the visitor then chooses is remembered and wins over these. A link, a
text fragment or find in page into a closed section opens it.

Because most of the page now starts closed, a closed section shows its
heading and one row built only from what is inside it: the three dates;
the strip of pictures with its count; the section's own button or link;
the supporters' logos; the two conduct links; one stop for the goat.

The persona study had decided otherwise and its dissents stay on record:
it kept every section open and closed only the six Tuzla photos and the
twelve dishes, on a phone only, because each other section carries
something it ruled must not be hidden (an action, a date, a price, a
supporter's logo, the conduct text) or is too short to earn a control, and
because closing them all "would also turn the album into a list of
headings" for the first-time attendee. It also recorded that two of its
personas wanted the page shorter, and that three would never open the
supporters section.
The organiser's words above set that verdict aside for the home page.

This also goes beyond the decision of 2026-11-07 that the home page's
goats stay (sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--home-goats-stay.md): a
goat inside a closed section is not a stop until the section is opened.
The organiser's words today set that decision aside for the home page's
closed sections, and every goat returns when a section is opened.
