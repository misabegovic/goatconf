Pricing overview
Publish events for free
Eventbrite allows organizers to publish unlimited events of any size at no cost.
Attendees pay Ticketing Fees
When you sell tickets, add-ons, or accept donations, your attendees pay Eventbrite's Ticketing Fees at checkout. If you prefer, you can choose to cover these fees yourself when setting up your ticket types. There are no fees for free tickets.
Paid marketing tools are optional
Eventbrite offers a variety of tools to help you grow attendance and revenue, including paid options:
Eventbrite Ads
: You can pay to advertise your event on Eventbrite's website and mobile apps. Costs are based on your chosen daily budget, which determines how frequently your ads are displayed.
Eventbrite Premium
: An optional subscription that adds extra capabilities on top of everything you can already do today. These options are further described in this article.

What happens to existing Pro plans
If you currently subscribe to a Pro plan, nothing changes for you. You keep your existing plan at your current price, and your higher email send limits keep working as they do today.
Pro plans are no longer available to new subscribers. If you cancel your Pro plan switch to Eventbrite Premium in the future, you will not be able to resubscribe to Pro.
