The organiser pasted an outside review of goatconf.com, Luma and the call for talks form. Its points, in short: the register page says registration "takes a minute" while Luma shows "Approval Required"; the home page tile "Propose a session: about fifteen minutes" reads like a fifteen-minute talk; the call for talks form points to goatconf.ba and speaks of an "organising team"; the site has no "who's organising" block and no attendance estimate; "you hear back" has no time; the program grid shows 56 open slots; hotel prices mix currencies; guide dogs are excluded; there are no microphones in the big halls.

Checked on 2026-10-13: goatconf.ba does not resolve. The call for talks form names goatconf.ba 21 times and the "propose an event" form 29 times; both say "organising team" 10 times. The contact form is clean.

Asked whether to fix the forms, the tile, the register wording, add an organiser block and show prices in EUR, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Ok, ask me one by one

Their answers, one question at a time:

- **Approval on Luma:** "Confirm within days". The register page says the organiser confirms each registration, usually within a few days, and Luma emails when you are in.
- **Reply to proposals:** "Within 3 weeks".
- **First speaker announcement date:** "No date yet". Speakers appear as they are accepted.
- **The empty program grid:** "Keep the full grid".
- **Guide dogs:** "Keep it as it is".
- **A sound system asked of supporters:** "No".
- **The organiser block:** "Say 120 to 300". It names EuRuKo 2024 and RubyConf Austria, IPI Academy as host and partner, and 120 to 300 people expected for the first edition.
