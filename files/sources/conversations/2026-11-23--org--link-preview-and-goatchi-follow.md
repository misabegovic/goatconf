(sources/conversations/2026-11-23--org--banners-and-covers-follow.md), the
agent said that only the site's link preview (`og.png`) and the Goatchi
card still had the old look. The organiser wrote, verbatim:

The agent drew both on the working branch, on paper and on the album's
blue, and sent them as pictures before merging. The link preview is drawn
as the banners are: the wordmark, "Any programming language, any stack.",
the yellow band with "3–5 June 2027 · Tuzla", "Free" in its pill and the
address, and the plain goat. The Goatchi card (its own link preview and
its square and wide post cards) keeps the goat eating burek and the plain
goat saying "Baaa!", now sitting on the band under the wordmark. The agent
pointed out that the Goatchi card's band now carries the conference's
date, town and "Free", where the old card showed only its address, that
Goatchi's promo video and the animated goats posts are video files and
keep the old look, and that LinkedIn and X may show the old link preview
for a while. The organiser wrote, verbatim:
