# Dates and the core days: Thursday 3 and Friday 4 June 2027, every room in parallel, breaks on Freedom Square

A child of the epic [GoatConf 2027: a minimal core others extend](../epics/goatconf-2027.md).
It graduates the pitch
[One core day, wrapped in days others fill](../pitches/dates-format-and-schedule.md),
bet on by the organiser on 2026-10-07
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--bet-on-every-pitch.md`).

## Objective

**IPI Academy picks one of six date options in June 2027 and confirms it
in writing, and GoatConf runs one core day in IPI's hall of about 120:
three to four talks, with coffee and lunch on Freedom Square, one to two
hours each, and the days around it left to extensions.**

## Background

The organiser named the first or second week of June 2027 and left the
length open (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`, question 6).
Asked to pick a date, the organiser chose not to: "we should make it a
bit more flexible and prepare options to propose to IPI Academy"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--date-options-for-ipi.md`), and
asked to include options where "all lecture days take place on a
weekday" (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--weekday-lecture-days.md`).

The organiser also asked for room to breathe: "I don't want to have a
huge schedule that cramps everything together. I want space and time
for folks to hang out", with IPI used only for lectures and breaks "in
the city on the nearby town square", one to two hours each
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`). The
square is Freedom Square (Trg slobode), under five minutes' walk, and the
main day has three to four talks
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--freedom-square-and-few-talks.md`).

The RubyEvents guide says to agree the date and room in writing before
announcing anything, and that "for a first conference, one day is the
safer choice" while two days make "the trip feel more worthwhile"
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Before You Commit, § How many days). One guaranteed day wrapped in
extension days holds both. Registration and the call for talks both wait
on the date: registration opens when IPI confirms in writing
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--coc-ledger-registration-cfp.md`).

## Affected personas

- [Attendee](../../../.claude/personas/users/attendee.md). Today there is
  no date to plan travel around. With this shipped, an attendee knows the
  core day and the days around it months ahead, and spends most of the
  core day with people rather than in seats.
- [Speaker](../../../.claude/personas/users/speaker.md). Knows there are
  three to four slots on one day, in one room of about 120, with no
  microphone, and when that day is.
- [Extension host](../../../.claude/personas/users/extension-host.md).
  Knows which days around the core day are open for workshops, fringe
  events and activities.
- [Volunteer](../../../.claude/personas/users/volunteer.md). Has a day
  with a clear rhythm: getting people back from the square to the hall
  is the job that matters.

## Now / Perceived / Target

- **Now (2026-10-07):** no date. Six options are prepared for IPI, none
  chosen. What the organiser knows of the room: the largest hall seats
  about 120 ("120 i think"), with a projector, sound and Wi-Fi, no
  microphone and no recording; several other rooms; step-free to the
  main hall and first floor; Saturday or weekday both work
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).
- **Perceived:** that IPI is free in early June. IPI's teaching, exams,
  defences and its own DIEC conference in June 2027 are unknown; the App
  Challenge's 2026 topic deadline was 15 June
  (`sources/web/venue/ipi-akademija-app-challenge.md`). Another belief is
  that Freedom Square's cafés absorb about 120 people at once; their
  capacity is (unknown, needs source).
- **Target:** a date confirmed by IPI in writing, the hall and the rooms
  held for the option's days, and a published shape of the core day that
  the program, the website and operations all build on.

## Scope

**Six date options, put to IPI, not chosen here.** Each keeps one core
day the organiser guarantees, with the days around it left to
extensions:

- **A. First week, weekend core:** Thursday 3 to Sunday 6 June 2027,
  core day Saturday 5 June.
- **B. First week, weekday core:** Wednesday 2 to Saturday 5 June 2027,
  core day Friday 4 June.
- **C. Second week, weekend core:** Thursday 10 to Sunday 13 June 2027,
  core day Saturday 12 June.
- **D. Second week, weekday core:** Wednesday 9 to Saturday 12 June 2027,
  core day Friday 11 June.
- **E. First week, midweek:** Tuesday 1 to Friday 4 June 2027, core day
  Thursday 3 June, the weekend after left free.
- **F. Second week, midweek:** Tuesday 8 to Friday 11 June 2027, core day
  Thursday 10 June, the weekend after left free.

The trade-offs carried with them are the pitch's: the salt lake is more
likely open in the second week (it opened on 7 June in 2019,
`sources/web/tuzla/panonika-n1-2019.md`; other reports differ,
`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-sezona-kurir-2026-04.md`,
`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`); Sunday 6 June 2027 is
listed by one source as a regional holiday and not by the Federation's
list (`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-officeholidays.md`,
`sources/web/tuzla/neradni-dani-fbih-2026-paragraf.md`, unverified);
Kurban Bajram falls in mid-May
(`sources/web/tuzla/holidays-2027-calendarlabs.md`); the Džumbus festival
started on 5 June in 2026 (`sources/web/tuzla/dzumbus-festival-festivalfinder.md`);
weekend cores suit people who come on their own time, weekday cores suit
people whose employer sends them, and E and F need IPI on three
weekdays.

**The ask to IPI.** Four questions, so its answer picks the option:
which days are free of teaching, exams, defences and DIEC; whether the
hall and rooms can be held for all of the option's days or only the core
day; whether IPI prefers a weekend or a weekday; and whether it can
confirm in writing by November 2026.

**The core day.** One room, IPI's hall, about 120 seats, one track, no
parallel talks. Three to four talks in short blocks: a welcome and ten
minutes on the code of conduct, then talks; coffee on Freedom Square for
one to two hours; a talk; lunch on Freedom Square for one to two hours;
a closing talk; the evening free for fringe events. Times are set with
IPI. Each person buys their own coffee and lunch, or a supporter pays a
café directly; the organiser's money pays for no food or drink
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
An MC announces when the hall reopens, and a clear return time is said
at every break and posted on the website.

**The cap.** Registration for the core day equals the hall, about 120,
with a waiting list ("I would register per hall capacity"). Speakers
project their voice; there is no microphone
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).

## No-gos

- No second core day in 2027.
- No parallel talks in the core day.
- No more than four talks on the core day.
- No public date until IPI has confirmed it in writing.
- No catering in the hall and no organiser money for food or drink.
- No hotel room blocks.
- No larger venue for the core day; a bigger room would be a venue
  extension, not a core change.

## Rabbit holes

- **Growing an extension day into a second core day.** Tempting once
  workshops fill; it doubles the organiser's load. Stop and re-pitch.
- **Choosing the date for IPI.** The organiser asked for options; the
  brain's lean (option C, from the pitch) is a lean, not a pick.
- **Waiting for perfect information on the lake.** The activity day
  works without swimming.
- **A larger hall.** Hotel Tuzla's Crystal Hall seats "up to 300 guests"
  (`sources/web/venue/tztz-hotel-tuzla.md`), at an unknown price, and the
  guide warns of room blocks and food minimums at hotels. If demand far
  exceeds 120, the cap still holds.
- **Rain on the square.** June is Tuzla's wettest month, about 11 rain
  days (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia.md`). Covered terraces or
  IPI's own rooms are the fallback; a full wet-weather plan belongs to
  operations.

## Appetite

**Medium.** Two parts with different clocks: the date and the room have
to be put to IPI and confirmed in writing in October and November 2026,
which is a short task with a long wait; the shape of the core day is
drafted now and finished when the program is chosen. Neither part is
large, but both span months.

## Success metrics

- **IPI confirms one of the six options, and the rooms, in writing by 30
  November 2026.** Observed as a snapshot of the confirmation under
  `sources/conversations/` or `sources/venue/`, dated.
- **The core day runs with three or four talks, no more.** Observed from
  the published program on muhamed.at/goatconf on the day.
- **Breaks last one to two hours each.** Observed from the published run
  sheet for the core day against the times actually kept, noted by a
  volunteer in a source note the day after.
- **The hall is full or nearly so:** 90 or more checked in of about 120.
  Observed from the registration tool's check-in export, counted the day
  after.

## Open questions

- Which of the six options IPI can host.
- DIEC's usual date, and whether exams or defences fall in early June.
- Whether IPI holds the rooms for all of the option's days or only the
  core day.
- Whether the hall has an accessible toilet (the organiser did not
  answer).
- How many people Freedom Square's cafés seat at once (unknown, needs
  source).

## Decision needed

The ADR records the option IPI confirms and the shape of the core day's
run sheet: the order of the blocks, the times agreed with IPI, how long
each break is within one to two hours, and how people are called back
from Freedom Square. The choice it makes is between two coffee-and-lunch
layouts: two separate breaks of one to two hours, or a single long
midday break with the talks gathered either side.

**Answered by the ADR** [Give the core day two long breaks on Freedom Square, coffee and then lunch](../adrs/dates-and-the-core-day.md), accepted 2026-10-07.
