---

After seeing the redesigned talk page (PR #257, source:
sources/conversations/2026-11-07--org--talk-page-redesign.md), the
organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Let's improve phone version and the cards render one by one?

Measured on a 390 px and a 360 px phone, the page was about 11,000 px
tall; the six "Why speak at GoatConf" stickers sat two across in narrow
columns after three paragraphs and the form button, and the format slots
came after about 900 px of detail, with about 130 px between each slot,
kept free for the peeking goat.

Asked, the organiser chose:

- **One by one:** one sticker per row on phones, at most 26rem wide,
  with bigger lettering, like the home page's "What is decided" on
  phones. No entrance animation (stickers stay still, as decided on
  2026-10-27).
- **Cards first on phones:** in "Why speak at GoatConf", the first
  paragraph, then the six stickers, then the rest of the text and the
  form button; in "What a session can be", the six format slots, then
  workshops, half an hour and "a session or an event?". The wide layout
  stays: text on the left, cards on the right.
- **One goat over Talk:** only the first format slot, Talk, keeps a
  peeking goat, and the slots close up to normal spacing on phones and
  wide screens, instead of the goat hopping through each format with a
  gap kept above every slot.

Also on phones, the stay price list's prices are set smaller (1.3rem) so
its lines fit a 360 px screen.
