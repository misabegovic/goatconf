Panonsko Lake is an artificial lake of Bosnia and Herzegovina . It is located in the municipality of Tuzla . This lake is commonly used as a leisure spot by both tourists and locals alike. It is the only salt lake in mainland Europe. [ 1 ] [ better source needed ]
