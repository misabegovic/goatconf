title: "The speaker pages and the register page say when, where and that it is free: ship with existing words"
date: 2026-11-22
kind: conversation
---

On 2026-11-22 the organiser was asked a question with answers to choose
from, after an independent review of the live site found that a visitor
arriving from a shared speaker card is never told the dates.

Question: the speaker page never states the dates and shows "free" three
screens down, and the register page's first phone screen has no date or
city; how should the fix go out?

Answer chosen: "Ship with existing words (Recommended)"

That is: both pages get the cover's existing date and place line near the
top, and the speaker pages a "Register for free" link, using only words
that are already on the site, published with the next round once the
checks pass. The words and where each already stands:

- "3–5 June 2027 · Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina": the cover of the home
  page, under the title.
- "Free to attend.": the home page, in "See you in Tuzla".
- "Register for free", linking to the register page: the home page, the
  button in "See you in Tuzla".
