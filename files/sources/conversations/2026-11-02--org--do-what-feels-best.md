After the speakers moved to Tuesdays (sources/conversations/2026-11-02--org--speakers-one-by-one.md),
the agent left two questions open: "See you in June" on Saturday 2 January
2027 already ended with "register now", a week before the registration
post on Saturday 9 January; and five Saturdays were open (19 December
2026, 30 January, 20 February, 13 March and 27 March 2027). The organiser
answered "Yes" to opening the pull request, then wrote, verbatim:

> Do what feels best for the event

The agent chose:

- **Registration stays on Saturday 9 January 2027,** straight after the
  last speaker, as the organiser asked. "See you in June" keeps its
  new-year message but links to goatconf.com instead of the register
  page, so the two posts do not make the same ask a week apart.
- **Saturday 19 December 2026 gets a Tuzla post,** "Named after Tuzla":
  tuzlaite, the mineral named after the city, shown at the Pannonica
  geological exhibition at the Pannonian Lakes, where GoatConf's
  community day is. It was chosen over a fourth salt-history post in a
  row and over the Salt Museum, whose source gives no visiting details.
- **The open Saturdays in 2027 stay open for news** (supporters, events,
  a newly confirmed speaker), with a Tuzla post drafted about a month
  ahead if no news comes, as the Tuesday Tuzla posts already are.
