Four pages of the foundation's website: getting involved as an
organisation, as a professional and as a student, and donating. The
footer line repeated on every page is quoted once.

## Every page (footer)

> The foundation aims to transform Bosnia & Herzegovina into a prosperous nation that can respond to the challenges of the 21st century. It does this by empowering the youth through non-formal education, technology access and development of world class leadership skills.

The home page header (https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/, fetched the
same day) reads "empowering the youth through education, technology and
leadership" and "Transforming young talents into future leaders and
breaking down ethnic barriers in Bosnia & Herzegovina through access to
technology and education." Its news lists a Bosnia & Herzegovina
Professionals Meetup at the University of Toronto ("Over 80
professionals, academics, entrepreneurs, researchers, students and
community leaders"), an evening in NRW with Tako Lako, and Innovation
Nation 2026 beginning at "BHFF House", the foundation's new home, on 3
October 2026.

## https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-organisations

> We at the BH Futures Foundation help identify, nurture and retain STEM talent in the country by growing our community of like-minded, well-intentioned and growth-oriented people.

> Besides our three flagship programs (Futures Scholarship Program, Researchers Program, Entrepreneurs & Innovators Program) you can decide to collaborate on other projects (such as Futures Leaders Summit, Vera Snajder Award) and connect with the rest of the community through meet-ups and events.

> Your partnership with the BH Futures Foundation could be shaped around:

The list that follows names joint scholarships, internships, company
visits, professional courses, conferences and forums, networking events,
providing mentors and speakers, donating licenses, software and
equipment, and donating money.

## https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-professionals

> The greatest asset Bosnia and Herzegovina has are its people and the collective knowledge they have acquired. Nearly 2 million of them live all around the world and have built incredible careers. Many of them who remain in our homeland are also highly successful. Our foundation strives to utilise this collective “know-how” to supercharge and empower Bosnia & Herzegovina youth.

> Make the connection and host a foundation networking event in your city
> Host a student for work experience to improve their employability
> Join the academic & researchers database (Akademski Imenik)
> Become a monthly donor and transforms lives with us
> Donate equipment and enable learning in rural areas

## https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/get-involved-students

> Bosnia and Herzegovina Futures Foundation was founded with a goal of empowering youth in Bosnia & Herzegovina through technology, education and leadership. Whether you are a student in primary/elementary school, high school or university, our foundation is your home.

## https://www.bhfuturesfoundation.org/donate

> 1 person can make a difference, 1000 good people perform miracles. Become one of them!

> Our goal is to develop and educate 10,000 STEM leaders by 2030. 10,000 young people who will have global experience, yet implement change at a local level. To make that happen, we need you.

> We want to reach 1000 active monthly donors who will contribute just $5 (5€) a month and help us make a difference.
