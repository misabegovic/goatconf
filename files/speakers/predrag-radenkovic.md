Predrag Radenkovic builds reliable code generation tools as the CTO of *codeplain. A two-time founding engineer, he has helped guide startups from their early stages to unicorn valuations and acquisitions, leading technical teams at Silverfin, Toptal, and FIS.

He focuses on putting guardrails on agentic skills frameworks. Predrag advocates for spec-driven development, teaching developers how to turn modular specifications into the ultimate source of truth. Rather than hoping generated code works, his approach ensures implementations are continuously validated against strict behavior specs.
