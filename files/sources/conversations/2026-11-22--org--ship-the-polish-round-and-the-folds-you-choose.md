On 2026-11-22 the organiser was shown the polish round
(sources/conversations/2026-11-22--org--anything-left-lets-iterate.md) and,
as a proposal, the travel page and the host page opening and closing like
the home page, with three questions: whether to ship the polish round,
whether to ship or drop the folds on the two long pages, and which of four
drafted wordings to use, if any. The four were: a sentence under "Who pays"
tying it to the hour-off rule and to Tuzla's rule on charging for entry;
"Tuzla · 3–5 June 2027 · Free to attend" as the speaker cards' foot line,
which the post cards already carry; a line saying a supporter supports
GoatConf 2027 on each supporter's page; and "Register with the host", the
button's own words, as the label of the raw form address on the event
page. The organiser answered, verbatim:

> Ok, ship it, you choose

Read as: the polish round is published, and the two open choices are left
to the agent. Chosen, for the organiser to revisit:

- The folds on the travel page and the host page are published with the
  round. They take the host page's first visit from about 15,400 to about
  3,800 pixels on a wide screen and bring "Propose yours" to the top third.
- The sentence under "Who pays" is not added. It would say that each paying
  their own bill counts as free in an hour off, which the organiser has not
  said.
- The speaker cards' foot line gains "Free to attend", since the post
  cards already say exactly that.
- Each supporter's page gains the line "Supporter of GoatConf 2027" above
  its existing lede. It is a plain statement of what the page already is.
- The event page's raw form address is labelled "Register with the host".

The three wording changes are built and published after this round, once
their checks pass.
