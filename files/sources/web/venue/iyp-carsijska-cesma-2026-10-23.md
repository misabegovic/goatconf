Čaršijska Česma
Trg Žrtava srebrenice 2, Tuzla, Bosnia & Herzegovina
Google Maps
Tuzla’s resident traditional restaurant, Čaršijska Česma is famous throughout the city for its ćevapi, pljeskavica and and all the rest. If you’re looking for a little slice of traditional cuisine, look no further, although by ‘little slice’ we obviously mean ‘massive portion’. Don’t eat before coming here, that’s for sure.
