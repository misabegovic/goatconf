# Activities: the salt lake, a goat hunt, and a cheap city as the gift

A child of the epic [GoatConf 2027: a minimal core others extend](../epics/goatconf-2027.md),
graduated from the pitch
[Activities: the salt lake, a town-wide hunt, and a cheap city as the gift](../pitches/activities-the-lake-and-the-town.md),
on which the organiser bet on 2026-10-07
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--bet-on-every-pitch.md`). This
PRD waits for the organiser's approval before its ADR is written.

## Objective

**GoatConf publishes what Tuzla costs and where to spend the long breaks,
proposes one lake day and one goat hunt, and lists every other activity
as an extension, so attendees spend their days together at their own
small cost while the organiser pays for and runs nothing beyond that
list.**

## Background

The organiser named two assets and one goal: "The salt lake in Tuzla is
an attraction, Tuzla itself is a cheap city and I want to make benefit of
that for the conference", and people who "hang out, spend time together"
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`). They
also asked for space rather than a crowded schedule, with coffee and
lunch breaks in town lasting one to two hours each
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`), on
Freedom Square, under five minutes from IPI Academy
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--freedom-square-and-few-talks.md`).

The RubyEvents guide agrees that this is what people remember: "The best
thing you can add to a conference is not a talk. It is time and structure
for people to actually experience things together"
(`sources/web/guides/rubyevents-how-to-organize-a-ruby-conference.md`,
§ Beyond the talks). The same section names the organiser's own
"city-wide treasure hunt throughout Vienna during RubyConf Austria".

Activities usually cost money and organising time, and the core has
neither to spare. The organiser's own money is a fallback of at most
1,000 EUR (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--first-answers.md`,
`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`),
never spent on food or drink for anyone
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`),
and no money passes through the organiser
(`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`). So the
core lists and proposes; people pay their own way; hosts run the rest.

## Affected personas

- [Attendee](../../../.claude/personas/users/attendee.md). Today they
  have no idea what a trip to Tuzla costs or what there is to do beyond
  the talks. With this work they see prices with a "last checked" date
  before they book, know where to go in each long break, and have a lake
  day and a goat hunt to join with strangers who become company.
- [Extension host](../../../.claude/personas/users/extension-host.md).
  Today there is nothing to add an activity to. With this work a city
  walk, a hike or a karaoke night is one record in the extension
  mechanism, listed beside the core's two activities.
- [Volunteer](../../../.claude/personas/users/volunteer.md). The goat
  hunt and the break lists need a few people who know the town; IPI's
  students are the natural fit, and the work is light and owned.

Secondary: the [speaker](../../../.claude/personas/users/speaker.md),
who pays their own travel and gains most from knowing the city is cheap.

## Now / Perceived / Target

- **Now (2026-10-07).** No activity is planned, no price is published,
  and no date is fixed: six date options go to IPI and its answer decides
  ([the dates pitch](../pitches/dates-format-and-schedule.md),
  `sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--date-options-for-ipi.md`). The
  facts exist in snapshots: the Pannonian Lakes are three salt-water
  lakes in the city centre with salt waterfalls and slides, reached by a
  tourist train from Freedom Square
  (`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-pannonian-lakes.md`,
  `sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-city-guide-tztz-2025.md`); an adult ticket was
  7 KM on weekdays and 9 KM at weekends in peak 2026, about 3.60 and 4.60
  EUR (`sources/web/tuzla/slana-jezera-cijene-kurir-2026-08.md`); an
  inexpensive meal is about 4.60 EUR and a cappuccino about 1.66 EUR
  (`sources/web/tuzla/numbeo-cost-of-living-tuzla.md`); June is Tuzla's
  wettest month (`sources/web/tuzla/tuzla-wikipedia.md`).
- **Perceived.** "Tuzla is cheap" is believed by the organiser and is
  true on the snapshots, but an attendee from abroad does not know it
  unless the numbers are in front of them. The lake is perceived as a
  swim, and it is: the season opens on 1 June (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-13--org--lake-season-opens-1-june.md).
- **Target.** By the week of the event, the site carries a prices and
  places page with a "last checked" date, a short list of cafés and
  places to eat around Freedom Square, a lake day with a rain plan, a
  goat hunt with written clues, and every other activity listed as an
  extension. The organiser's money spent on activities is 0 EUR.

## Scope

The core publishes three things and proposes two. It publishes a prices
page (food, coffee, the lake ticket, the tourist train) with a "last
checked" date, after the cost table Bansko Nomad Fest keeps
(`sources/web/models/bansko-nomad-fest-life-in-bansko.md`); a short list
of cafés and places to eat on and around Freedom Square for the long
breaks, each person paying their own; and a short activities page that
links the extension listings. It proposes a lake day on the day after the
core day, at the Pannonian Lakes and the Slana banja park next door with
its paths, cafés and the Peace Flame House
(`sources/web/tuzla/tztz-salty-spa-park.md`,
`sources/web/venue/tztz-peace-flame-house.md`), with the park cafés and
an indoor fringe event as the rain plan. It proposes a town-wide goat
hunt whose clues tell the goat story, the man who hid his goat and the
saying "The whole of Tuzla milked one goat", ending at the goat statue
(`sources/web/funding/euruko-2024-why-the-goat.md`). Everything else (a
city walk, a day trip, a hike, a run along the lakes, Tuzla's own June
events if their 2027 dates fall that week) is an extension record.

## No-gos

- No activity paid for from the organiser's fallback money, and no food
  or drink paid for by the organiser, for anyone.
- No money collected by the organiser for any activity; a supporter who
  wants to pay everyone's lake ticket pays the lakes' operator directly, on the
  public ledger.
- No organised transport, group bookings or guides in the core.
- No swimming run by GoatConf; swimming is at the lake operator's
  facility, under its rules.
- No activity that clashes with the core day's talks.

## Rabbit holes

- **Organising tourism.** Buses, guides and group bookings each add money
  and liability. If the work starts to look like a travel agency, stop:
  the core lists, hosts run.
- **Chasing a group rate.** Asking the lakes' operator or the tourist
  board for a discount is worth one message, not a negotiation; if it
  needs a contract, drop it.
- **Promoting the organiser's own product.** The organiser's travel guide
  Usput.ba publishes places and itineraries for Bosnia and Herzegovina
  (`sources/web/organiser/muhamed-at-about.md`). Linking to it is useful
  only if it is said plainly that it is the organiser's own.
- **Keeping prices live.** A price list is a snapshot with a date, checked
  once in spring and once in the week before; it is not a feed.

## Appetite

**Small.** The core's share is two short pages, one list of cafés and one
set of clues, written by the organiser and a volunteer or two. The
activities themselves are extensions run by hosts and paid for by the
people who join them, so they cost the core nothing to scale.

## Success metrics

- **The prices and places page is live with a "last checked" date by
  31 March 2027.** Observed as the page's source file in this repository,
  its date line, and the commit that copies it to muhamed.at/goatconf.
- **The organiser's money spent on activities: 0 EUR.** Observed from the
  public ledger page's final account, published within two weeks of the
  event; no line names an activity.
- **At least three activities listed as extensions by the week before the
  event, none run by the organiser.** Observed as a count of extension
  records of the activity kind in this repository.
- **The goat hunt runs with at least five mixed teams.** Observed as a
  count of teams on the hunt's sign-up or start sheet, recorded under
  `sources/` the day after.

## Decisions already made by the organiser

- The salt lake is an attraction and the cheap city is to be used as a
  benefit (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`).
- The organiser provides the minimum; other things they do not cover
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--conference-vision.md`).
- Coffee and lunch breaks happen in town, one to two hours each
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--space-to-hang-out.md`), on
  Freedom Square, under five minutes from IPI
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--freedom-square-and-few-talks.md`).
- The organiser pays for no food or drink, for anyone
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-food-or-drinks-and-site-path.md`).
- No money passes through the organiser, and the budget is public
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--no-money-through-me.md`).
- The organiser's own money is a fallback only, at most 1,000 EUR
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--ipi-rooms-and-fallback-money.md`).
- Extensions come in by pull request or through the same form tool as the
  CFP; an agent checks each and the organiser merges
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--goatconf-will-be-public.md`).
- The dates are options for IPI, not fixed
  (`sources/conversations/2026-10-07--org--date-options-for-ipi.md`).

## Open questions

- Does the organiser run the goat hunt, or hand the clues to a host?
- May the travel and activities pages link to Usput.ba's Tuzla content,
  marked as the organiser's own?
- Is one message to the tourist board or the lakes' operator about a
  group rate worth sending? Sending it is the organiser's act.
- Which of Tuzla's own June events (the lakes' season opening, Astronomy
  Days, Summer at Pannonica) fall in the chosen week in 2027 (unknown,
  needs source).

## Decision needed

**Is the goat hunt part of the core, run by the organiser, or an
extension handed to a host with the clues?** The ADR records the answer
together with the shape of what the core publishes for activities: the
prices and places page (which items, how the "last checked" date is
kept), the lake day with its rain plan, and how the activities page
links the extension listings.

**Answered by the ADR** [The organiser runs the goat hunt; the core publishes prices, the breaks list and the lake day](../adrs/activities-lake-and-town.md), accepted 2026-10-07.
