In the host's words

Bosnia and Herzegovina Futures Foundation has a pleasure of inviting you to our Professionals Meetup in Tuzla, part of GoatConf 2027.

📅 Date: Saturday, June 5th, 2027

🕐 Time: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

📍 Location: TBD in Tuzla

🥂 Light refreshments will be served

After short introductions from our partners we will also have short presentations from local professionals.

Whether you are building your career, conducting research, working in industry, studying at a university, or simply looking to connect with others who care about Bosnia and Herzegovina, we would be happy to have you with us.

🤝 Connect with professionals and researchers from the community

💡 Exchange ideas, experiences, and opportunities

🎓 Meet academics and students working across different fields

🌍 Strengthen connections between BiH and the global community

🚀 Explore potential collaborations and ways to get involved with BHFF

👉 This is an RSVP event. Kindly register your attendance through this registration form to secure your spot.

We look forward to meeting you in Tuzla and to building stronger connections within our growing community.
