## Pages

WEB NOVI, PROMOTIONS, ai research, export, Thumbnail, Style guide, Sponsorship offer, Comic moodboard, IDENTITY, Assets, EXPORT, Archive, and a working page each for six team members (Denis, Amila, Midhet, Sonja, Zehra, Muhamed), plus Web Kaigi.

## The goat

- **The main 2024 mascot (Thumbnail, WEB NOVI).** A 3D-style white goat with curled horns, a red fes with a tassel, a red vest with Bosnian embroidery over a cream shirt, holding a steaming fildžan of coffee; on the event thumbnail it sits in a dark stepped-cross shape over the word EuRuKo. Its layers are named "kouza" and "koza". WEB NOVI also has a dancing version of the same goat.
- **The multiverse (PROMOTIONS, frame "wall"; Denis, "Frame 24").** Twelve renders of one goat in different roles: a jewelled goat on a pedestal, a samurai, the goat hidden beside a little stone house with a fildžan set, a coder with glasses at several screens, a painter at an easel, the Bosnian goat with fes and coffee on a cobbled street, a pharaoh, a soldier, a goat in a hijab before a mosque, a pirate, a goat with a rainbow scarf at a pride march, and a chef with ćevapi. A comment on PROMOTIONS asks to add "12 Koza - Bosnian Goat" as a card.
- **The prompts (ai research).** The text layers name the character "Multiverse Goat" and its roles, among them: "Multiverse Goat transformed into a powerful wizard", "Pirate Captain Goat: Swinging from ropes on a pirate ship, brandishing a cutlass and a treasure map", "Detective Noir Goat", "Robin Hood, the legendary outlaw goat", "a mystical goat ninja", a superhero goat "in mid-flight, wearing a custom-made cape", and "a goat mascot, leaping and twisting in dynamic movement, symbolizing its ability to traverse the multiverse".
- **A cuter direction already explored (ai research).** Among the renders: a goat on a postage stamp with headphones and a backpack, a small chibi goat, a goat in a Japanese-style cartoon with a fan, goats in Sarajevo trams, a graffiti goat, and goat mascots in costume at a basketball game.
- **The trading cards (Denis).** A card front and back: the front titled "The Panonnian Pirate" (spelled so on the card) with the pirate goat, the stepped-cross motif and placeholder text, "EuRuKo", a dash, and "September 11-13, Sarajevo B&H" along the bottom; the back is the hex goat logo on a circuit pattern.
- **The sticker pack (PROMOTIONS).** A pixel goat, the hidden-goat render, a red cosmic goat under the word EURUKO, "minaswan", the hex goat logo and the stepped-cross motif.
- **Early marks (Sonja).** Geometric line goats as logo options, and a landing page draft with a section "The myth about goat" showing a goat on a pedestal.

## For Tuzla

- **export.** A five-page "Introduction to Sarajevo" visitor guide: top places, food and coffee, activities in and around the city. A format a Tuzla guide could follow.
- The hidden goat by its stone house, and the Pannonian pirate (the Pannonian Sea left Tuzla its salt), tie the goat to Tuzla's own stories.
