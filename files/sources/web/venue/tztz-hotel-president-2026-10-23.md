About 10 km from Tuzla (per the page); not walkable from IPI.

‘Hotel President’ has a capacity of 14 rooms and one suite. The rooms are modernly equipped and air-conditioned. Access to the rooms is provided via magnetic key cards.
For relaxation, guests have access to a wellness centre featuring a swimming pool, jacuzzi, saunas and other accompanying facilities.
The hotel also offers a modern, fully equipped multifunctional hall, which provides an ideal venue for hosting congresses, panels, seminars and other types of meetings. The hall is designed for groups of up to 80 people.
‘Hotel President’ features a contemporary restaurant with a capacity of 150 guests. With its diverse gastronomic offer, it attracts visitors not only from the Tuzla Canton but also from a much wider area.
The restaurant serves a variety of international cuisine, including Greek, Chinese, Italian and traditional Bosnian dishes. The hotel is situated in an excellent location, only 10 km from Tuzla and 5 km from the centre of Živinice. Its accessibility is further enhanced by its proximity to Tuzla International Airport.
