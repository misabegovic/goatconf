Who they are

Meister builds digital productivity tools that make it easy for anyone to go from ideas to action. MeisterTask is a work management platform that gives teams one place for projects, tasks and documentation, and MindMeister is a mind mapping tool used by millions of students, teachers and professionals. Founded in 2006 by Till Vollmer and Michael Hollauf, Meister is based in Vienna, Austria, with a team across Europe.
