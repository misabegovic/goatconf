# Speaker: Paul Campbell

A confirmed GoatConf 2027 speaker, added on 2026-10-14 before his session
has a slot or a topic; he agreed to a public profile and to promotion on
social media (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--paul-campbell.md).
His public profile is the record `speakers/paul-campbell.md`, built into
`site/speaker-paul-campbell.html`. This page records only what the sources
say and what bears on GoatConf. He is not the British media entrepreneur of
the same name on Wikipedia.

## Scope

- **Who:** "I make software in Dublin, Ireland. I'm the founder of Tito, ticketing software for events." (source: sources/web/speakers/paulca-com-2026-10-14.md). Tito's own page says it was "Founded by Paul and Doc"; his conference bios call him co-founder and CEO (sources: sources/web/speakers/team-tito-2026-10-14.md, sources/web/speakers/confex-paul-campbell-2026-10-14.md).
- **Tito:** Team Tito is a team of six in Ireland and the UK; Tito "has proudly processed over $1 billion worth of tickets for thousands of organisers worldwide". Its invitation-only app IO was renamed Tito Pro in April 2026 (source: sources/web/speakers/team-tito-2026-10-14.md).
- **A conference organiser himself:** Funconf, "a conference series that happened on moving busses, in a castle, and on an island", and This Next Thing, "150 tech leaders together in the Swiss Alps" (source: sources/web/speakers/confex-paul-campbell-2026-10-14.md). Turing Fest calls him "an experienced Ruby on Rails and JavaScript developer, and a conference organiser" (same source).
- **Talks and podcasts:** "A Modern Approach to Third-Party Embedded Widgets" at Turing Fest 2018; a speaker at International Confex on 3 and 4 March 2027; a guest on IndieRails (episode 49, January 2025), ShopTalk Show (366) and Code with Jason (227) (source: sources/web/speakers/confex-paul-campbell-2026-10-14.md). None is picked for GoatConf yet.
- **Lately writes about:** AI as augmentation and as accessibility, and using an LLM off the grid, on paulca.com, July to October 2026 (source: sources/web/speakers/paulca-com-2026-10-14.md).
- **Bio on the site:** his International Confex bio, word for word, on the organiser's choice. It still says "They have recently introduced IO", the product now called Tito Pro (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--paul-campbell.md, sources/web/speakers/team-tito-2026-10-14.md).
- **Role on the site and cards:** "Co-founder and CEO of Tito" (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-14--org--paul-campbell.md).
- **Photo:** the portrait on his own site, 1200 by 1200, resized to 800 by 800; not freely licensed, used on the organiser's decision (source: sources/web/speakers/paulca-com-2026-10-14.md).
