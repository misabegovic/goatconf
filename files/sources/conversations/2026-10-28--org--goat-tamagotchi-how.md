---
title: "The goat tamagotchi's plan approved, and built as a page of its own, fully separate"
date: 2026-10-28
kind: conversation
---

Shown the plan for the goat tamagotchi (wiki/org/prds/goat-tamagotchi-on-the-site.md), the organiser chose "Approve (Recommended)", and the plan was merged.

Asked how it should be built, with a small extra script loaded on adoption recommended, all of it in album.js, a page of its own, or not building it, they chose "A page of its own": a "Your goat" page made by a tool, with the companion goat unchanged.

Asked whether the rest of the site should still connect to it (album.js noting a speaker's page read, the register link followed or a card shared, for people with a goat, and tapping a goat offering "Keep me" with a link to the page), they chose "Fully separate": the goat page stands alone, reached from a link in the footer, the cheers become things done on the goat page itself, and the rest of the site does not change.

Reading the decision record, which called the page "Your goat", the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Why your goat? Why not GoatConf Tamagochi?

The agent said "Your goat" was its working title, and that "Tamagotchi" is a registered trademark of Bandai, so a game named "GoatConf Tamagotchi" would use their mark in its name; the page can still describe it as a little game like a tamagotchi. Asked what to call it, the organiser chose "Goatchi (Recommended)": "Goatchi: keep a GoatConf goat", on goatchi.html.
