The organiser sent a phone screenshot of goatconf.com/extensions and
wrote, verbatim:

> Also here, the text breaks weird for the event title

The event card sat in a half-width column, and with the phone's text
size "PROFESSIONALS" did not fit, so it broke as "PROFESSIONAL / S".
Built as: on phones, event cards (on the events page, the home page's
"Accepted so far" row and supporter profiles) take the full row, and a
name never breaks inside a word. Checked at 360 pixels wide with text
at 130%, at 412 pixels and on a wide screen.
