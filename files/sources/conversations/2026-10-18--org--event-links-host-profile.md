title: "An event's page links its host's supporter profile"
date: 2026-10-18
kind: conversation
---

After supporter profiles and event pages went live, the organiser wrote,
verbatim:

> From the event page of a supporter, we should link to the profile of the supporter

Built as: when a supporter's record lists an accepted event under
`events:`, that event's page links the supporter's profile three times:
the "Run by" name, a line under the details ("<host> is a supporter of
GoatConf 2027. See their profile: who they are and how to support
them."), and a link at the foot of "In the host's words". The BH
Professionals Meetup's page now links the Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures
Foundation's profile.
