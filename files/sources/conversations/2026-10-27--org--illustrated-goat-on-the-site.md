After the watercolour goat was chosen (sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--mascot-watercolour.md), the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> ok, now lets continue. you are the illustrator. if you need to download a tool for yourself, do it

The agent installed tools on the organiser's machine to work as the illustrator: a background remover (rembg) and an upscaler (Real-ESRGAN), and used Gemini in the organiser's Chrome, on their own signed-in account, to paint more poses from the character sheet: the goat in its roles peeking over a ledge, each speaker's goat peeking and sitting (described from their photos in words, with no names or photos uploaded), the same sheets with eyes closed for a blink, and later each goat's head turned left and right. Asked what next, the organiser chose "Put it on the site (Recommended)". Asked to ship that step, they wrote:

> do the roles now as well... everything we built with the existing mascot needs to be replaced with the thematic new one. also, I want to explore the concept of only a single goat being on the site visible at a time for a visitor... we also need ways to animate in different versions also, different movements...

Shown three prototypes, they chose "Companion, ducks and pops (Recommended)", kept every move offered ("Bob, wiggle, stretch", "Peek-a-boo", "Look around and shy", "Hop between slots"), and chose "Ship all together" over shipping the roles first. They added, verbatim:

> we also need to improve and optimize the animations before shipping anything, needs to look seamless and function on even bad internet connections

> also turning should feel like movement, also ducking and hiding and popping back up, etc

Trying the preview, they wrote:

> seems like the goat is popping up in weird places

> should we tie the positioning to the elements on the page?

and after the goat was moved into the slot it stands on:

> looks better now, continue with the stickers. also maybe improve the turning animation, so the goat actually turns, instead of rotating the image

On the speakers page:

> also on who's speaking, lets render rows of 4 people at a time

> on the speakers page that is, we already do that on the homepage

On the stickers:

> we should also have goats for the tuzla images and for the food images. eating, bathing in the lakes...

On the speakers' share cards:

> also dont forget to update speaker cards

> I'm looking at Dejan's announcement card. the text seems broken down too many times. why dont we benefit from the width available and have it a bit easier to read for folks?

> not only Dejan's card obviously, for other speakers the same should apply

> why not display both the square and the wide cards on these pages?

> also, the two cards differ in content too much?

Asked how alike the "I'm speaking" and "Confirmed speaker" cards should be, they chose "Same, only the headline differs (Recommended)". Then:

> also, should the goat on these cards be positioned a bit differently? so it doesn't block the view? idk.. seems a bit crowded. wdyt? maybe I'm wrong

Shown the goat moved beside the photo, they wrote "looks bad. I dont like this." and, asked which part, chose "The goat's new spot on the cards". They added "also there's a bunch of noiseon the cards" and "not the hoofs, they are good. the text + everything else". Asked what should stay besides the name and photo, they chose "Headline", "Role and talk", "The goat" and "One footer line". Offered the goat rising from the bottom corner or small against the photo's ring, they chose "Rising from the bottom corner", then wrote:

> I'd like the seal back though

> I see the goat's legs cut off, like for Amar's photos. folks are gonna notice

> why not load the entire goat? looks weird to cut off?

Shown a prototype of the host goat turning its painted head left and right, they chose "Yes, do all goats (Recommended)", and wrote while it was being built:

> ok. make sure legs are not cut off etc

> seems it's not completely loading?

They also left an idea for later, verbatim:

> idea for after: tamagochi game as part of the conference, for each speaker. folks can play and keep alive their favorite speaker

Finally:

> ok, do 2 and 3. also test and validate it all works as expected. I think it might not maybe?

where 2 was the decision records and design notes, and 3 showing the Stickers page in the browser before shipping.

After the testing (every page with goats audited at desktop and phone width, a script error in the hop fixed, the seam at the turned head's neck and holes in the turned heads fixed, and the Stickers page given small previews), the organiser was asked whether to ship the whole batch, with Christoph's and Predrag's head turns to follow once Gemini's limit resets. They chose:

> Ship it (Recommended)
