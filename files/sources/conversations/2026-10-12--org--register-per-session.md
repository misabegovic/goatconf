---
title: "Every session gets its own registration on Luma, which saves a seat"
date: 2026-10-12
kind: conversation
---

While the Luma event was being updated, the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> We should have every session from the agenda as a separate session to register for also

Told that Luma has no sessions inside an event, so each session would be its own Luma event linked from the program, the organiser answered three questions:

- What registering for a session means: "Reserves a seat (Recommended)": each session's limit is its room's seats, Luma keeps a waiting list when it is full, and people who did not register can take empty seats at the start.
- Who approves session registrations: "No approval (Recommended)": anyone can register for a session straight away; the main event's approval still applies.
- Who creates each session's Luma event: "I do it in Chrome": when the organiser accepts a session, the agent creates its Luma event in the organiser's logged-in browser and links it from the site.
