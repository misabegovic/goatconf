After wroclove.rb's profile and thank-you cards went up (PR #150,
sources/conversations/2026-10-21--org--wroclove-rb-community-partner.md),
the organiser wrote, verbatim:

> Also... For supporters, we should also have the "We are supporting GoatConf" cards for them to download and post?

Each supporter's profile had only GoatConf's thank-you card to them. The
agent asked four questions, and the organiser chose:

- **The headline:** "Proud supporter of GoatConf 2027".
- **The host:** "Same headline for everyone". IPI Academy's card reads
  "Proud supporter of GoatConf 2027" too, not "hosting".
- **What the card shows:** "Same, linking to their profile". The
  supporter's logo, their supporter line as the supporters page shows it,
  "Tuzla · 3–5 June 2027 · Free to attend", and the address of their
  GoatConf profile, such as goatconf.com/supporter-wroclove-rb.
- **Where it goes:** "Above the thank-you card". On each profile, a
  section with the new card, square and wide, to download, then the
  thank-you card. A link to the profile still previews with the
  thank-you card, or with the card of the event they host.

While checking the new cards the agent saw that on the square thank-you
card of the Bosnia & Herzegovina Futures Foundation the long profile
address ran into the seal. On both kinds of card a square card's address
longer than 36 characters is now set smaller, so it stays clear of the
seal; that card was rendered again, and no other card changed.
