“Center of Youth Culture“ Tuzla , whose founder is the City of Tuzla, functions as an open system institution which directly satisfies needs of individuals, organizations and other public institutions which deal in culture, art and youth. Every association, art organization, school, formal or informal group of youth can use resources of the Center of Youth Culture for realization of their program contents within the field of contemporary art and culture and various educational activities for youth. The advantage of the institution is its openness where users can shape their activities independently, in the most suitable way for them but with technical, media and other necessary prerequisites for realization of activities and with flexibility of employees.
Activities of the Center of Youth Culture Tuzla happen at several locations, i.e. organizational units: “Social Center“ Gornja Tuzla, Memorial House “Husinska buna“ at Husino, “Swedish Solidarity House “ Gornja Lipnica, ”Kaleidoskop“, “Literature Center“ and central facility in Tuzla. At all mentioned locations
International festivals, musical programs, events in local production (performances, concerts, exhibitions, shows, musical videos recording, etc.) take place at all of the mentioned locations. Regular activities (local bands’ rehearsals, workshops, practicing, etc.) and movies projections, presentations, conferences and gatherings of various associations, meetings of various organizations and many other activities also take place at those locations.
As an executive producer, Center of Youth Culture Tuzla participates in realization of manifestations organized by the City of Tuzla, such as international literary meetings ”Cum grano salis“, a festival of contemporary art of youth “Kaleidoskop“ and a traditional manifestation “Summer in Tuzla” and various fairs and similar events which take place during the whole year.
