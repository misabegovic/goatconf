# Goatchi as a full game played with one goat, and more poses for every goat on the site

## Problem

Goatchi shipped on 2026-10-28 as a small bet: pick the plain goat or one of six
role goats, feed it, play with it and let it sleep; it sulks but never dies,
and in challenge mode a neglected goat wanders off
(sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-28--org--goatchi-ship.md,
sources/conversations/2026-10-28--org--goatchi-challenge-mode.md). An audit on
2026-10-31 found it sound, with no console errors at phone or desktop width,
but thin as a game (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goatchi-full-game.md):

- **The goat does not stay itself.** A kept pirate turns into the plain goat
  while it eats or sleeps, because only the plain goat has eating and
  sleeping art.
- **No moods.** The goat smiles with a need nearly empty; nothing painted
  shows it hungry, bored, sleepy or sulking.
- **Play is one hop**, and each act has one picture and one line.
- **No arc.** Nothing changes over weeks and the game has no end, though it
  counts the days to 3 June 2027.
- **On phones** the page's large goat pushes the game below the first
  screen, and the Sleep button wraps onto a line of its own.

The organiser wants it whole before it is promoted: "Make goatchi feel like a
full game. Played end to end with the same goat type", and "Only when goatchi
is at its final shape should we promote". The Saturday drumbeat slot of
31 October 2026 is held for Goatchi; the registration post waits until after
it (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goatchi-full-game.md).

The same gap shows on the home page, which the organiser named next: the
speakers' goats, the goats on the food photos and the goats on the Tuzla
photos (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goatchi-full-game.md).
Each speaker's goat peeks, blinks, turns its head and sits, and a photo of
Tuzla or of food gets a goat only where a painted scene matches it; on
2026-10-27 only five did, and new paintings were to come "once the limit is
lifted" (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--goats-on-photos-and-spots.md).
Every painted goat is already used somewhere
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--every-painted-goat-used.md).

## Appetite

**Big**: about three to four weeks. Most of it is art, made the way the
current goats were, in an image generator in the organiser's Chrome, a sheet
at a time, and cut by the site's tools
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--mascot-watercolour.md). The
agent builds while the art comes in and shows each step in the browser. It
ships in two parts on the same art: **Goatchi first**, which is then
promoted, and **the home page's goats after**
(source: sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goatchi-full-game.md).

## Solution

Fat-marker, for a bet; the details are for a decision record after one.

**Part one: Goatchi, a whole game with one goat.**

- **Each goat type's own poses.** The plain goat and the six role goats
  (host, coder, speaker, cook, pirate, the shy one) each get eight: eating,
  sleeping, playing, hungry, sulking, happy, hiking and packing for Tuzla,
  about 56 pictures. One sheet per type keeps each goat recognisably
  itself. Playing is the type's own trick: the coder types, the speaker
  takes the microphone, the cook stirs, the host pours coffee, the pirate
  swashbuckles, the shy one hides in its basket.
- **Moods follow the needs.** Well, it looks happy; with one need low, it
  looks hungry, bored or sleepy; below a quarter, it sulks.
- **Animation.** At rest it breathes, blinks and flicks an ear; eating
  chews, sleep lets "Zzz" rise, and each trick moves its own way.
- **A story to Tuzla.** Days kept reach milestones, at 7, 30 and 100, that
  unlock postcards from Tuzla: the salt lakes, Salt Square, Meša and Ismet,
  the places the Tuesday posts tell of
  (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-24--org--tuesday-and-saturday-posts.md).
  In the last week before 3 June it packs; on the day it arrives at
  GoatConf.
- **Phones first.** With a goat kept, the game comes first on the page and
  the three buttons sit on one row.
- **Weight.** Only the kept goat's own sheet loads, after it is picked:
  about 250 KB.

**Part two: the home page's goats, on the same art.**

- **The speakers' goats** get more than a peek and a sit: a wave, a happy
  jump, and their own trick, drawn as each was, from their photo. The site
  shows them as already agreed, published with a change on request
  (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--speaker-goats.md).
- **Paul Campbell's goat, painted again in ginger.** It was painted from a
  black and white photo with dark hair and recoloured by hand to his ginger
  hair and beard; the organiser wants it painted properly
  (sources: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--goats-on-photos-and-spots.md,
  sources/conversations/2026-10-31--org--goatchi-full-game.md).
- **The food photos** each get their goat eating that dish, and **the
  Tuzla photos** each get a scene, so the companion has a goat for every
  photo, not five.
- **The companion** uses the new poses and moves where it visits.

## Rabbit holes

- **Keeping each goat the same.** A generator can drift between poses; one
  sheet per type, with the type's first sheet as the reference, is the
  guard, and a pose that drifts is redone or dropped.
- **The generator's limit.** The organiser's plan caps images a day; sheets
  batch poses to save it, and the art may take more days than the build.
- **The illustrator.** The plan has the organiser's illustrator draw the
  final goat; a larger set means a larger redraw later
  (source: sources/conversations/2026-10-27--org--mascot-watercolour.md).
- **Minigames creeping in.** Each trick is a picture and a move, not a game
  of its own.
- **Streaks that guilt.** Milestones count days kept, never days in a row,
  and missing days costs nothing but a sulk.
- **Weight on the home page.** More poses must not mean more bytes before a
  visitor scrolls to them.

## No-gos

- Nothing to buy, no accounts, no tracking, no server, no notifications.
- The goat never dies; only a goat in challenge mode wanders off.
- No speaker's goat kept in Goatchi without that speaker's agreement.
- Goatchi is not promoted until the organiser calls it final.
- The generators' sheets stay out of this repository, as they do today; only
  the cut pictures the site shows come in.
